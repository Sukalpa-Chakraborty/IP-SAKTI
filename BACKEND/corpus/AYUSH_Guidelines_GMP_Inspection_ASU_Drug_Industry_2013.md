## Introduction

- D&C Act 1940 + Rules provide for ASU Inspectors (Sec 33G), key role in implementation
- Inspectors under State Licensing Authority inspect ASU units before licence under D&C Act/Rules
- Qualifications vary: Pharmacy / Pharma Chemistry / Medicine (Clinical Pharma/Microbio) with ASU practical training; or Degree/Diploma in A/S/U or Ayurveda Pharmacy
- No induction training; many States designate ASU doctors without analytical capacity-building – manual fills gap
- Covers qualifications, duties, responsibilities; detailed GMP interpretation; helps augment regulatory capacity, develop master trainers

## Chapter I – GMP: An Overview

### 1. GMP
Production + testing practice ensuring quality product. Not prescriptive; general principles. Company determines effective quality process.
Per Rule 157 + Schedule T, GMP for ASU ensures:
I. Raw materials authentic, prescribed quality, contamination-free
II. Manufacturing as prescribed to maintain standards
III. Adequate QC
IV. Released drug acceptable quality
V. Each licensee evolves documented methodology/manual for reference/inspection. Exemption: IMCC Act 1970 registered Vaidyas/Siddhas/Hakeems preparing for own patients, not for market.

### 2. Importance
Unlike synthetic pharma (reproducible), ASU mainly herbal from varied geo/commercial sources, variable composition, different QC techniques; contamination with toxic plants, small defined actives; production/primary processing directly affects quality – GMP essential.

### 3. Basic Principles
Common to all countries – safeguard patient, quality medicine:
- Processes clearly defined, controlled, validated; changes evaluated/validated
- Clear unambiguous instructions/procedures
- Operators trained, document procedures
- Records (manual/instrument) prove steps taken, quantity/quality; deviations investigated
- Batch history traceable (including distribution), retained comprehensible/accessible
- Recall system
- Complaints examined, defect causes investigated, preventive measures

### 4. Legal provisions
D&C Rules 1945, Rules 151-160 + Schedule T.

### 5. Roles / Responsibilities of Inspectors

**Appointment:** Central/State Govt under Sec 33G, notified in Gazette. Qualifications (Rule 167/49):
(a) Degree Pharmacy/Pharma Sciences/Medicine (Clinical Pharma/Microbio) + practical ASU manufacture training, or
(b) Degree in Ayurveda/Siddha/Unani or Ayurveda Pharmacy from recognized University/State/Statutory Faculty/Council/Board, or
(c) Diploma in A/S/U from State/recognized Institution.
No financial interest in manufacture/sale.

**Duties re manufacture for sale (Sec 22, 23):**
- Inspect premises manufacturing ASU drugs + standardization/testing means; premises selling/stocking/exhibiting/offering/distributing
- Take samples being manufactured/sold/stocked/exhibited/distributed, or in transit (from conveyor/deliverer)
- Search person/place/vehicle/vessel/conveyance on reason to believe Ch IV-A offence; order in writing not to dispose stock ≤20 days, or seize stock + articles/means (unless defect remediable)
- Examine/seize records/registers/documents/objects as evidence
- Receipt in Form 16; require production of records re manufacture/sale/distribution on suspected Ch IV-A offence; other powers for Ch IV-A; CrPC 1973 applies (Sec 94 warrant procedure); seized/produced records returned within 20 days after certified copies/extracts taken
- Obstruction/refusal: punishable up to 3 years +/or fine

**Routine duties (subject to controlling authority):**
(i) inspect ≥twice/year all licensed ASU manufacture premises in area, ensure licence conditions + Act/Rules compliance
(ii) forthwith detailed report to controlling authority after each inspection
(iii) sample drugs, send for test/analysis per Rules
(iv) institute prosecutions
Public servant per IPC Sec 21, subordinate to specified authority.

**Sample collection procedure:**
- Tender fair price, may require written acknowledgement; if refused or stock seized, receipt in Form 17A
- Intimate test purpose in Form 17, divide in presence (unless wilful absence) into 4 portions sealed/marked (permit person's seal); if from manufacturing premises – 3 portions only; if small-volume containers – take 3/4 containers marked/sealed (must if deteriorable by exposure)
- Restore 1 portion/container to person; dispose remainder: (i) 1 to Govt Analyst forthwith (registered post/hand, sealed, memo Form 18-A, outer cover with distinguishing number, copy + seal specimen separately; Analyst records seal conditions on opening), (ii) second to Court if proceedings, (iii) third to person disclosed under Sec 18A (non-manufacturer distributor must disclose source name/address/particulars if required)

**Action under Sec 22(c):**
(i) expeditiously ascertain if contravenes Sec 33EEC; if not, revoke order/return seized stock
(ii) if seized, inform Judicial Magistrate, take custody orders
(iii) without prejudice to prosecution, if defect remediable, revoke on satisfaction of remedy
Seized records/objects – inform JM for custody.

**Joint inspection for drug testing lab approval (Rule 160A-J):**
Before Form 48 approval, joint inspection by Central + State appointed/designated Inspectors – examine premises/equipment, verify expert staff qualifications; Central inspectors (Rule 160-E) forward detailed report to approving authority. Approved lab must allow Inspector entry with/without notice, inspect premises/equipment/procedures/registers/records, supply info.

### 6. Role of Licensing Authority
State-notified authority enforces D&C Rules re GMP certification (detail follows).

## Chapter II – Legal Provisions (Part XVI D&C Rules)

**151:** Separate application/licence per set of premises.
**152:** State Govt notifies Licensing Authorities/areas via Gazette.

**153 – Application for manufacture licence:**
Form 24-D + Rs 1000 fee. Renewal: before expiry or within 1 month; after 1 month but within 3 months: Rs 1000+200 + additional Rs 600. Duplicate Rs 300 if defaced/damaged/lost.

**153-A – Loan licence:** Form 24-E + Rs 600. Loan = no own arrangements, uses Form 25-D licensee's facilities. Renewal similar (+ Rs 300 extra if 1-3 months late). Duplicate Rs 150.

**154 – Form 25-D licence:** Subject to Rule 157, issued within 3 months of application; after consulting State-approved Ayurveda/Siddha/Unani expert.
**154-A – Loan Form 25-E:** After expert consultation; Authority must satisfy unit has adequate equipment/staff/capacity/testing to undertake manufacture for applicant.

**155 / 155-A:** Renewal certificates Form 26-D / 26-E.
**155-B – GMP certificate:** For ASU manufacturers complying Schedule T, 5 years; issued from licence issuance date (sub-rule says simultaneous with grant/renewal – see Rule 157(1)(a) Form 26E-I).

**156 / 156-A – Duration:** 5 years unless suspended/cancelled. If renewal applied before expiry/within 1 month (or within 3 months with Rs 500 additional fee for 25-D), licence continues till orders; deemed expired if not applied within 3 months. Loan similar per 153-A.

**157 – Conditions for grant/renewal Form 25-D:**
(1) Manufacture in premises/hygienic conditions per Schedule T. (a) For GMP certificate, verify Schedule T, issue Form 26E-I simultaneously with grant/renewal.
(2) Under direction/supervision of competent whole-time technical staff ≥1 person with:
(a) Degree Ayurveda/Ayurveda Pharmacy/Siddha/Unani from recognized University/State/Statutory Faculty/Council/Board, or
(b) Diploma A/S/U from State/recognized Institution, or
(c) Graduate Pharmacy/Pharma Chemistry/Chemistry/Botany + ≥2 years ASU drug manufacture experience, or
(d) Registered Vaid/Hakim with State Register + ≥4 years ASU manufacture experience, or
(e) Pharmacist in A/S/U with ≥8 years manufacture experience as recognized by Central Govt.
(3) Ayurveda staff for Ayurveda drugs, Siddha/Unani respectively.

**157-A – Raw material records:** Each unit keeps Schedule TA proforma of all raw materials used preceding financial year, submits by June 30 to State Licensing Authority + National Medicinal Plants Board / nominated agency.

**158 – Licence conditions (25-D):** (a) maintain manufacture/test records (raw/finished, self/other); (b) allow Inspector entry/inspection/sampling/records; (c) maintain Inspection Book Form 35.

**158-A – Loan (25-E):** (i) deemed cancelled/suspended if lender 25-D cancelled/suspended; (ii) comply Act/Rules + future Ch IV-A Rules (effective 4 months after Gazette); (iii) records; (iv) allow inspection/records/info; (v) Inspection Book Form 35.

**158-B – Guidelines for licence re ASU drugs:**

I.(A) Sec 3(a): all medicines internal/external for diagnosis/treatment/mitigation/prevention in humans/animals, manufactured exclusively per First Schedule authoritative books formulae.
(B) Patent/Proprietary Sec 3(h): (i) all formulations with only First Schedule formulae ingredients, excluding parenteral + excluding authoritative-book formulations themselves; (ii) Balya/Poshak/Muqawi/Unavuporutkal/positive health promoters with First Schedule ingredients for promotional/preventive health; (iii) Saundarya Prasadak (Husane afza/Azhag-sadhan) with First Schedule ingredients for oral/skin/hair/body care; (iv) Aushadh Ghana (medicinal plant extracts dry/wet, aqueous/hydro-alcohol) from First Schedule plants.

II.(A) Classical + dosage-form change + new indication table:
1. 3(a) drugs – as per text ingredients/indications – safety not required, published experience required, proof not required
2. Dosage-form change of 3(a) – same
3. 3(a) for new indication – as per text ingredients, new indication – safety not required, experience if required, proof required

II.B Patent/Proprietary safety/effectiveness:
1. As per text + textual rationale – safety not required – ingredients experience – pilot study per AYUSH protocol
2. With Schedule E(1) ingredients – as per text/existing – safety required, experience required, proof required

III. Balya/Poshak: need (i) photocopy textual reference, (ii) safety if Schedule E(1) per evaluation guidelines, (iii) no safety/efficacy for textual indications.
IV. Saundarya: same (i)-(iii).
V. Aushadh Ghana extracts:
1. Aqueous – as per text/text – none required
2. Aqueous new indication – not required/not required/required
3. Hydro-alcohol as per text/text – not required/if required/not required
4. Hydro-alcohol new indication – required/if required/required
5. Other than hydro/hydro-alcohol – as specified – required (acute, chronic, mutagenicity, teratogenicity)/if required/required
*Standard protocol includes Anupan, Prakriti, Tridosha etc. published by Central Research Councils/Govt bodies. **New = other than First Schedule books.

**159 – Cancellation/suspension:** After ≥15 days show-cause opportunity, written reasoned order, wholly/partly. Appeal to State Govt within 3 months.

**160 – Raw material ID:** Identify/test where tests available, maintain methods/records.

### Schedule T [Rule 157] – GMP for ASU

Objectives (i)-(v) as above, including documented manual; Vaidya/Siddha/Hakim exemption.

**PART-I Factory Premises:** Adequate space for receiving/storing RM, process areas, QC, finished store, office, rejected store.

1.1(A) Location: avoid open sewerage/drain/lavatory/factory with obnoxious odour/fumes/soot/dust/smoke.
1.1(B) Buildings: hygienic, free cobwebs/insects/rodents, light/ventilation, non-damp floors/walls, Factory Act compliance; compatible operations; adequate working space to avoid mix-up/cross-contamination/omission; insect/rodent-proof, smooth crack-free impervious cleanable walls/floors/ceilings, smooth even flooring, proper drainage, safe sanitary/electrical, furnace/Bhatti tin roof + ventilation + fly/dust care, fire safety/exits, separate protected drying space (flooring, wire-mesh, glass pans).

1.1(C) Water: pure potable for manufacture; adequate washing water.
1.1(D) Waste: suitable treatment per pollution control before disposal if prejudicial.
1.1(E) Containers (bottles/vials/jars): separate washing/cleaning/drying arrangement.
1.1(F) Stores: ventilated, damp-free, independent adequate space for RM/packaging/finished.

1.1(F)(A) Raw Materials Store: appropriate containers protecting quality vs dampness/micro/rodents; sub-divide with cabins for controlled conditions. Categories: (1) metallic, (2) mineral, (3) animal, (4) fresh herbs, (5) dry herbs/plant parts, (6) excipients, (7) volatile oils/perfumes/flavours, (8) concentrates/extracts/exudates/resins. Each container labelled: name, source, status UNDER TEST/APPROVED/REJECTED, batch/lot, receipt date. Sampling/testing by in-house ASU expert or Govt-approved lab, use only on approval; rejected in separate room; FIFO; receipt/testing/approval/rejection/use records.

1.1(F)(B) Packaging: proper storage; clean/dry containers/closures before packing.
1.1(F)(C) Finished Store: transfer after packaging to Quarantine; QC/expert check packing/labelling/quality then to Approved Stock; dispatch only approved; distribution records; special conditions if needed.

1.1(G) Working Space: adequate orderly equipment/material placement, safe, minimize mix-up/cross-contamination.
1.1(H) Health/Clothing/Sanitation: free from contagious disease; proper clean uniform (hand/foot/head covering where needed); towels/soap/scrub brushes; separate men/women lavatories away from processing; changing/personal storage.
1.1(I) Medical: first aid; pre-employment + annual physician exam (infection-free), records.
1.1(J) Machinery: suitable manual/semi-auto/full-auto for crushing/grinding/powdering/boiling/mashing/burning/roasting/filtering/drying/filling/labelling/packing; adequate spacing; Part II-A list; SOPs for cleaning/maintenance/performance.

1.1(K) Batch Manufacturing Records: per batch (classical or proprietary) – RM list/quantities from store, stage tests (taste/colour/physical/chemical per approved First Schedule books, in-house/pharmacopoeial), signed by Production/QC, transfer to finished store (dates/quantities), testing/packaging, verification before sale; record date/manpower/machine/equipment, in-process shodhana/bhavana/fire burning/specific grindings.

1.1(L) Distribution Records: sale/distribution per batch for recall; retention till expiry; for no-expiry categories (Bhasma, Rasa, Kupi-pakva, Parpati, Sindura, Karpu/Uppu/Puram, Kushta, Asava-arista – efficacy increases) – maintain till 5 years after stock exhaustion.

1.1(M) Market Complaints: register all complaints, investigation/corrective action, submit to Licensing Authority every 6 months, available for inspection. Separate ADR register; investigate defect vs literature vs new.

1.1(N) Quality Control: in-house or Govt-approved lab; per AYUSH pharmacopoeia, else manufacturer spec; verifies RM, in-process, finished release; separate expert. Facilities: (a) 150 sq ft, (b) reference books/samples for raw drug ID, (c) manufacturing records, (d) controlled finished samples till expiry, (e) storage condition supervision, (f) shelf-life/storage records, (g) own specs for proprietary, (h) Bhavana/Mardana/Puta process records, (i) comply identity/purity/strength pharmacopoeias, (j) monitor fungal/bacterial. Staff minimum: (a) Ayurveda/Siddha/Unani degree (IMCC Act Sch II), (b) Chemist BSc/Pharmacy/Ayurveda Pharmacy, (c) Botanist/Pharmacognosist BSc Medical/Pharmacy/Ayurveda Pharmacy; or outside recognized lab under Rule 160-A with records. Equipment Part II-C.

1.2 Sterile Products: separate enclosed areas with airlocks, dust-free, filtered (HEPA) higher pressure, performance checks/records, cleanable surfaces, routine microbial counts vs in-house standards/records, minimum authorized access, displayed entry/exit procedures; design precludes mix of to-be-sterilized vs sterilized (terminal sterilization mix-up). Precautions: separate isolated block/enclosure, pressure differential, exhaust, laminar flow, UV efficiency checks (burning hours/intensity), individual liquid/ophthalmic containers examined vs black-white diffused light for foreign matter, expert yield check (actual vs theoretical) before distribution, master formula process controls (temp/RH/volume/leakage/clarity) recorded.

## Chapter III – Stepwise Inspection Checklist (p56-69)

Tabular checklist for inspectors: GMP clause, areas/activities, observations, document review, remarks. Covers:
1. General (unit name/address, licence, phone/fax/email, team)

## Chapter IV – WHO GMP Site Certificate / CoPP for Herbal Products (p70)

Many importers ask CoPP. Procedure:
1. Apply to Drugs Controller General (India) per product: (i) facility name/address, (ii) contact person, (iii) product name/dosage, (iv) active ingredients per unit (INN where exists), (v) formula incl excipients (especially if no licence or differs), (vi) approved product info/patient leaflet in exporting country.
2. Submit Site Master File per WHO TRS.
3. DCG(I) in consultation with AYUSH fixes inspection date.
4. Team: (a) AYUSH expert, (b) DCG(I) nominee, (c) State Drugs Controller/Licensing Authority.
5. Inspect per checklist, report to DCG(I).
6. If suitable, DCG(I) issues Herbal CoPP.
