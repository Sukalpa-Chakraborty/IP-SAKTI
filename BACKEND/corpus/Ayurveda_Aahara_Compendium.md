## Introduction

The **Ayurveda Aahara Compendium** is a comprehensive collection of traditional Ayurvedic food preparations (*Aahara Kalpana*). It categorizes recipes based on their consistency, method of preparation, and therapeutic properties. The text draws from classical Ayurvedic scriptures and provides detailed information on ingredients, preparation methods, health benefits, indications, and contraindications.

The compendium is organized into major categories such as:
- **Khadita** (Hard eatables)
- **Lidha** (Lickable)
- **Pana** (Drinkables)
- **Ashita** (Eatables)
- **Avaleha** (Confectionery)
- **Asava** (Fermented beverages)
- **Ghrita** (Clarified butter preparations)
- **Manda** (Rice gruel)
- **Yusha** (Soups)
- **Yavagu** (Gruel)
- **Peya** (Drinks)
- **Tarpana** (Satiating drinks)
- **Udaka** (Water preparations)
- **Vataka** (Dumplings)
- **Modaka** (Sweet balls)
- **Sharbata** (Syrups)
- and many more.

Each recipe includes:
- **Name of formulation**
- **Reference** (classical text and verse)
- **Category**
- **Label claim** (health benefit / disease risk reduction)
- **Ingredients** (with botanical names, parts used, and ratios)
- **Method of preparation**
- **Best use before** (shelf life)
- **Properties / Health benefits** (Ayurvedic attributes)
- **Classical indications** (diseases/conditions)
- **Contraindications**
- **Special precautions**
- **Others** (dosage, anupana, etc.)

### 3. Avaleha (Confectionery) – General Description

**Avaleha** or **Lehya** is a semi-solid preparation made with jaggery, sugar, or sugar-candy, boiled with prescribed juices or decoction, and mixed with powders/pulps of drugs, ghee/oil, and honey.

**General Method**:
1. Dissolve jaggery/sugar in liquid, strain.
2. Boil over moderate fire until thread-like consistency (*Tantuvat*) or sinks in water without dissolving.
3. Add fine powders of drugs, stir continuously.
4. Add ghee/oil while hot; add honey when cool.
5. Store in glass or porcelain jars. Use within one year.
