## Prologue / Background

This document contains the FDA regulations for **biological products** as set forth in Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR), Part 600. It covers general provisions, establishment inspection, and reporting of adverse experiences. The regulations apply to manufacturers of biological products, including vaccines, blood components, therapeutic sera, toxins, antitoxins, and analogous products. Key sections include:

- Mailing addresses for submissions
- Definitions (biological product, lot, filling, etc.)
- Personnel requirements
- Physical establishment and equipment
- Records and retention samples
- Reporting of biological product deviations
- Temperatures during shipment
- Establishment inspection
- Postmarketing reporting of adverse experiences
- Distribution reports
- Notification of permanent discontinuance or interruption in manufacturing
- Waivers

### § 600.3 Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Act** | Public Health Service Act (58 Stat. 682), approved July 1, 1944 |
| **Secretary** | Secretary of Health and Human Services and delegated officers/employees |
| **Commissioner of Food and Drugs** | Commissioner of the FDA |
| **Center for Biologics Evaluation and Research (CBER)** | CBER of the FDA |
| **State** | State or District of Columbia, Puerto Rico, or Virgin Islands |
| **Possession** | Includes Puerto Rico and Virgin Islands |
| **Products** | Biological products and trivalent organic arsenicals |
| **Biological product** | Virus, therapeutic serum, toxin, antitoxin, vaccine, blood, blood component or derivative, allergenic product, protein, or analogous product, or arsphenamine or derivative, applicable to prevention, treatment, or cure of disease in humans |
| **Virus** | Product containing minute living cause of infectious disease (filterable viruses, bacteria, rickettsia, fungi, protozoa) |
| **Therapeutic serum** | Product obtained from blood by removing clot/clot components and blood cells |
| **Toxin** | Soluble substance poisonous to laboratory animals or man in doses of 1 mL or less, causing production of neutralizing substance |
| **Antitoxin** | Soluble substance in serum/body fluid of immunized animal that neutralizes the toxin |
| **Analogous product** | (i) To virus if prepared from or with infectious agent; (ii) To therapeutic serum if composed of whole blood/plasma or containing organic constituent; (iii) To toxin/antitoxin if intended for prevention/treatment/cure through specific immune process |
| **Protein** | Alpha amino acid polymer with specific defined sequence >40 amino acids; size based on total number of amino acids in associated chains |
| **Trivalent organic arsenicals** | Arsphenamine and derivatives applicable to prevention/treatment/cure of diseases |
| **Proper name** | Name designated in license for use on each package |
| **Dating period** | Period beyond which product cannot be expected to yield specific results |
| **Expiration date** | Calendar month and year, and where applicable day/hour, that dating period ends |
| **Standards** | Specifications and procedures applicable to establishment or manufacture/release of products |
| **Continued** | As applied to safety, purity, potency: applies to dating period |
| **Safety** | Relative freedom from harmful effect to persons affected |
| **Sterility** | Freedom from viable contaminating microorganisms, as determined under §610.12 |
| **Purity** | Relative freedom from extraneous matter in finished product |
| **Potency** | Specific ability/capacity of product to effect a given result |
| **Manufacturer** | Legal person/entity engaged in manufacture of product subject to license; includes applicant for license |
| **Manufacture** | All steps in propagation/manufacture/preparation, including filling, testing, labeling, packaging, storage |
| **Location** | All buildings, appurtenances, equipment, animals, personnel within a particular area |
| **Establishment** | Same meaning as "facility" in section 351 of PHS Act; includes all locations |
| **Lot** | Quantity of uniform material identified by manufacturer as thoroughly mixed in single vessel |
| **Filling** | Group of final containers identical in all respects, filled with same product from same bulk lot |
| **Process** | Manufacturing step performed on product itself affecting safety, purity, or potency |
| **Selling agent or distributor** | Person engaged in unrestricted distribution (other than retail) of products subject to license |
| **Container (final container)** | Immediate unit, bottle, vial, ampule, tube, or other receptacle containing product as distributed |
| **Package** | Immediate carton, receptacle, or wrapper, including labeling matter and contents |
| **Label** | Written, printed, or graphic matter on container/package or visible through wrapper |
| **Radioactive biological product** | Biological product labeled with radionuclide or intended solely to be labeled |
| **Amendment** | Submission of information to pending license application or supplement |
| **Supplement** | Request to approve change in approved license application |
| **Distributed** | Biological product has left control of licensed manufacturer |
| **Control** | Responsibility for maintaining continued safety, purity, potency and compliance with standards and cGMP |
| **Assess the effects of the change** | Evaluate effects of manufacturing change on identity, strength, quality, purity, potency as related to safety/effectiveness |
| **Specification** | Quality standard (tests, analytical procedures, acceptance criteria) in approved application |
| **Complete response letter** | Written communication describing all deficiencies in BLA or supplement |
| **Resubmission** | Submission of all materials to fully address deficiencies in complete response letter |

### § 600.10 Personnel

| Paragraph | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **(a)** | [Reserved] |
| **(b)** | Personnel shall have capabilities commensurate with assigned functions, thorough understanding of manufacturing operations, necessary training and experience, and adequate information on pertinent provisions. Professionally trained persons as necessary. |
| **(c)(1)** | Persons whose presence can adversely affect safety/purity shall be excluded from manufacturing room. |
| **(c)(2)** | Personnel performing sterile operations shall wear clean or sterilized protective clothing. |
| **(c)(3)** | Persons working with pathogenic viruses or spore-forming organisms, and animal caretakers, shall be excluded from other manufacturing areas or change outer clothing/protective covering. |

### § 600.11 Physical Establishment, Equipment, Animals, and Care

| Paragraph | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **(a) Work areas** | Kept orderly, clean, free of dirt, dust, vermin. Precautions against drainage back-siphon. Exclude extraneous infectious agents. Well lighted and ventilated. Ventilation prevents dissemination of microorganisms. Filling rooms constructed for thorough cleaning and minimal airborne contaminants. Refrigerators, incubators, warm rooms maintained at proper temperatures and free of extraneous material. |
| **(b) Equipment** | Sterilization apparatus must insure destruction of contaminating microorganisms (e.g., 121.5°C for 20 min saturated steam or 170°C for 2 hours dry heat). Containers, filters, filling apparatus designed for thorough cleaning. Surfaces in contact with products clean and free of leachable contaminants. Sterile equipment for sterile products. |
| **(c) Laboratory and bleeding rooms** | Fly-proofed, free of flies/vermin. Constructed to insure freedom from dust, smoke, deleterious substances. Disinfected, with necessary services. |
| **(d) Animal quarters and stables** | Appropriate construction, fly-proofed, lighted, ventilated, clean, vermin-free. No manure/refuse storage that breeds flies. |
| **(e) Restrictions on building and equipment use** | (1) Diagnostic work not in manufacturing space (except occasional with cleaning/disinfection). (2) Spore-forming organisms for sterilization control test only under conditions. (3) Work with spore-forming microorganisms: process containment or separate building; procedures for removal/decontamination; environmental monitoring. (4) Live vaccine processing: dedicated area or procedural controls/process containment; written procedures for decontamination. Equipment/supplies exposed to pathogenic agents kept separate. |
| **(f) Animals used in manufacture** | (1) Care: sufficient caretakers, sanitary quarters, daily inspection, isolation of ill animals, veterinary care. (2) Quarantine: 7 days general; monkeys 6 weeks with negative tuberculin tests. (3) Immunization against tetanus for horses. (4) Immunization and bleeding: sterile antigens, no injections within 6 inches of bleeding site, no bleeding within 5 days of viable microorganisms, no bleeding with intercurrent disease. (5) [Reserved] (6) Reporting of certain diseases: notify Director immediately for foot and mouth disease, glanders, tetanus, anthrax, gas gangrene, equine infectious anemia, equine encephalomyelitis, pock diseases. (7) Monkeys used previously for experimental purposes: not for kidney tissue if used with live microbiological agents. (8) Necropsy examination of monkeys: by qualified pathologist/veterinarian for tuberculosis, herpes-like lesions, conjunctivitis. |
| **(g) Filling procedures** | Shall not adversely affect safety, purity, potency. |
| **(h) Containers and closures** | Made of material that will not deteriorate product. Clean, free of leachable contaminants. Sealed to maintain integrity. For injectables: sterile, pyrogen-free, colorless, transparent. Labeled as prescribed or stored in sealed receptacle with name, lot number, filling identification. |

### § 600.12 Records

| Paragraph | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **(a) Maintenance of records** | Records made concurrently with each step, traceable by inspector, legible, indelible, identify responsible person, include dates, detailed. |
| **(b) Records retention** | (1) General: retained beyond expiration date, no less than 5 years after manufacture completed or 6 months after latest expiration date, whichever later. (2) Records of recall: complete records maintained. (3) Suspension of requirement: Director may authorize suspension with summary retained. |
| **(c) Records of sterilization** | Mode, date, duration, temperature, other conditions recorded by automatic devices or equivalent. |
| **(d) Animal necropsy records** | Kept for each animal from which biological product obtained and which dies or is sacrificed. |
| **(e) Divided manufacturing responsibility** | Records show degree of responsibility; participating manufacturer furnishes copies to final manufacturer; final manufacturer retains complete record. |

### § 600.13 Retention Samples

Manufacturers shall retain for at least 6 months after expiration date a quantity of representative material of each lot sufficient for examination and testing for safety and potency, except Whole Blood, Cryoprecipitated AHF, Platelets, Red Blood Cells, Plasma, Source Plasma, and Allergenic Products prepared to physician's prescription. Samples selected at random from final container material or bulk and final containers. Stored at proper temperatures. Exceptions authorized by Director.

### § 600.14 Reporting of Biological Product Deviations by Licensed Manufacturers

| Paragraph | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **(a) Who must report** | Manufacturer holding license and control over product when deviation occurred. If another person performs step under your control, you must have procedure for receiving information. Exceptions: in vitro diagnostic products not licensed (report under part 803); blood and blood components (report under §606.171); Source Plasma used in further manufacture (report under §606.171 or this section). |
| **(b) What to report** | Any event associated with manufacturing, testing, processing, packing, labeling, storage, holding, or distribution that: (1) Represents deviation from cGMP, regulations, standards, or specifications that may affect safety, purity, potency; or (2) Represents unexpected/unforeseeable event that may affect safety, purity, potency; and (3) Occurs in your facility or contract facility; and (4) Involves distributed biological product. |
| **(c) When to report** | As soon as possible, not to exceed 45 calendar days from acquiring information reasonably suggesting reportable event. |
| **(d) How to report** | Form FDA-3486. |
| **(e) Where to report** | CBER: send to Document Control Center or submit electronically. CDER: send to Division of Compliance Risk Management and Surveillance (paper only). Identify envelope as BPDR. |
| **(f) Effect on other regulations** | Supplements and does not supersede other provisions. All deviations should be investigated per parts 211 and 820. |

## Subpart D – Reporting of Adverse Experiences

### § 600.80 Postmarketing Reporting of Adverse Experiences

| Paragraph | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **(a) Definitions** | Adverse experience, Blood Component, Disability, ICSR, ICSR attachments, Life-threatening adverse experience, Serious adverse experience, Unexpected adverse experience. |
| **(b) Review of adverse experiences** | Licensee must promptly review all adverse experience information from any source. Must develop written procedures for surveillance, receipt, evaluation, reporting. |
| **(c) Reporting requirements** | (1)(i) 15-day Alert reports: serious and unexpected, as soon as possible but no later than 15 calendar days. (ii) Follow-up reports within 15 days of new information. (iii) Submission of reports: obligations of other persons may be met by submission to applicant within 5 days. (2) Periodic adverse experience reports: quarterly for 3 years, then annually. Contents: descriptive information, ICSRs for serious, expected and nonserious adverse experiences. (3) Periodic reporting does not apply to postmarketing studies, scientific literature, foreign marketing experience. |
| **(d) Scientific literature** | 15-day Alert report based on literature must be accompanied by copy of published article. |
| **(e) Postmarketing studies** | 15-day Alert report required only if reasonable possibility that product caused adverse experience. |
| **(f) Information on ICSRs for nonvaccine biological products** | Patient information, adverse experience, suspect product(s), initial reporter, applicant information. |
| **(g) Information on ICSRs for vaccine products** | Patient information, adverse experience, suspect vaccine(s), vaccine(s) administered in 4 weeks prior, initial reporter, facility and personnel, applicant information. |
| **(h) Electronic format** | Safety reports must be in electronic format FDA can process. Waivers available. |
| **(i) Multiple reports** | Do not include adverse experiences from clinical trials already submitted in BLA. |
| **(j) Patient privacy** | Do not include names/addresses; assign unique code. Names not releasable. |
| **(k) Recordkeeping** | Maintain records for 10 years. |
| **(l) Revocation** | Failure to maintain records/reports may result in revocation of license. |
| **(m) Exemptions** | Whole blood/components; in vitro diagnostic products. |
| **(n) Disclaimer** | Report does not constitute admission that product caused adverse effect. |

### § 600.81 Distribution Reports

| Paragraph | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **(a) Reporting requirements** | Submit information about quantity distributed every 6 months. Report includes bulk lot number, fill lot numbers, label lot number, expiration, doses, date of release, returns. FDA may require more detailed info. |
| **(b) Electronic format** | Must be electronic unless waiver granted. |

---

### § 600.82 Notification of Permanent Discontinuance or Interruption in Manufacturing

| Paragraph | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **(a) Notification** | (1) Applicant of biological product (other than blood/components) must notify FDA of permanent discontinuance or interruption likely to lead to meaningful disruption if life supporting/sustaining or for debilitating disease; not radiopharmaceutical. (2) Blood/components applicant must notify if life supporting/sustaining and manufacturer of significant percentage of US blood supply. |
| **(b) Submission and timing** | Electronically: at least 6 months prior; or if not possible, within 5 business days after occurrence. |
| **(c) Information included** | Name, NDC, applicant, type (discontinuance/interruption), reason, estimated duration. |
| **(d) Public list of shortages** | FDA maintains list of shortages with names, NDC, applicant, reason, estimated duration. Confidentiality protections. |
| **(e) Noncompliance letters** | If failure to notify, FDA issues letter; applicant must respond within 30 days; FDA makes letter/response public within 45 days unless reasonable basis. |
| **(f) Definitions** | Biological product shortage, debilitating disease, life supporting/sustaining, meaningful disruption, significant disruption. |

---

### § 600.90 Waivers

| Paragraph | Requirement |
|-----------|-------------|
| **(a) Waiver request** | Applicant may request waiver of any requirement under §600.80 and §600.81 with supporting documentation: explanation why compliance unnecessary/cannot be achieved; description of alternative; other information. |
| **(b) FDA may grant waiver** | If finds compliance unnecessary/cannot be achieved; alternative satisfies requirement; or submission justifies waiver. |
