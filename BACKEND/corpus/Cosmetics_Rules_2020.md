## Prologue / Background

The Cosmetics Rules, 2020 were published by the Central Government in exercise of powers conferred by section 12 and section 33 of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940. These rules codify separately and update the rules relating to cosmetics, replacing the earlier provisions under the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945. The draft was published as Cosmetics Rules, 2018, and after considering objections and suggestions, the final rules were notified on 15th December, 2020.

The rules apply to cosmetics as defined in clause (aaa) of section 3 of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940. They cover import, manufacture, sale, distribution, labelling, packing, standards, sampling, testing, and enforcement. They also establish licensing authorities, laboratories, and procedures for registration and approval.

## Chapter I: Preliminary

### 1. Short title and commencement
(1) These rules may be called the Cosmetics Rules, 2020.
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

### 2. Application
These rules shall be applicable to the cosmetic as defined in clause (aaa) of section 3 of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 (23 of 1940).

### 3. Definitions
In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Act** | Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 (23 of 1940) |
| **Actual manufacturer** | In relation to import, a person who manufactures cosmetics at his own manufacturing site in a country other than India approved by National Regulatory Authority or any authorised competent authority in that country |
| **Appendix** | Appendix appended to these rules |
| **Authorised agent** | A person in India authorised by the manufacturer; responsible for business activities in India including compliance |
| **Bureau of Indian Standards** | Established under section 3 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 |
| **Central Licensing Authority** | Drugs Controller General of India |
| **Change in constitution of a licensee** | Firm: proprietorship to partnership (incl. LLP) or vice versa; Company: conversion private to public or vice versa; change in ownership of >50% voting capital; etc. |
| **Director** | Director of Central Cosmetics Laboratory appointed under sub-rule 3 of rule 11 |
| **Form** | Form set out in Appendix |
| **Government Analyst** | Appointed by Central or State Government under section 20 of the Act |
| **Import registration certificate** | Certificate issued under rule 13 for registration of cosmetics manufactured for import into India |
| **Inspector** | Inspector appointed under section 21 of the Act |
| **Laboratory** | Central Cosmetics Laboratory established or notified under rule 11 |
| **Legal manufacturer or brand owner** | Person who authorises other manufacturer for manufacture of cosmetics by way of authorization under rule 12 |
| **Licence** | Licence granted by State Licensing Authority under rule 25 |
| **Loan licence** | Licence granted under rule 25 for manufacturing a cosmetic utilizing manufacturing site of another licensee |
| **Manufacturer** | In relation to import: actual manufacturer or legal manufacturer |
| **New cosmetic** | Cosmetic containing novel ingredient not used anywhere in the world or not recognised in any National or International literature |
| **Schedule** | Schedule appended to these rules |
| **State Government** | In relation to Union Territory, the Administrator |
| **State Licensing Authority** | State drugs controller authorised to perform duties of Licensing Authority |
| **Subsidiary** | Entity in India owned by the manufacturer |
| **Use before or date of expiry** | Date recorded on container, label or wrapper up to which cosmetic shall retain its characteristics |
| **Other terms** | As defined in Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Drugs Rules, 1945 |

## Chapter II: Authorities, Officers and Laboratory

### 4. Licensing Authorities
(1) **Central Licensing Authority** shall be competent for:
- Import of all categories of cosmetics
- Coordination with State Licensing Authorities

(2) **State Licensing Authority** (State Drugs Controller) shall be competent for:
- Manufacture for sale or distribution of all categories of cosmetics
- Sale, stock, exhibit or offer for sale or distribution of all categories of cosmetics
- Grant of approval to laboratory for testing cosmetics and raw materials under Chapter VIII

### 5. Delegation of Powers
- Central Licensing Authority may delegate powers to officer not below rank of Assistant Drugs Controller (with prior approval of Central Government).
- State Licensing Authority may delegate powers to officer not below rank of Assistant Drugs Controller (with prior approval of State Government).

### 6. Controlling Officer
Any officer not below rank of Assistant Drugs Controller shall be controlling officer to supervise and give instructions to subordinates.

### 7. Government Analyst
Central or State Government may appoint Government Analyst by notification.

### 8. Functions of Government Analyst
- Analyse or test cosmetics sent by Inspector or any person under Chapter IV of the Act.
- Furnish reports within 60 days of receipt of sample.
- If not possible, seek extension of time with reasons.
- Forward reports of analytical work and research to Central Licensing Authority from time to time.

### 9. Powers, Duties and Functions of Inspectors
Inspector appointed under section 21 by State Government authorised to inspect manufacture:
- Inspect premises licensed to manufacture cosmetics at least once in three years.
- Send detailed report after each inspection.
- Institute prosecution for breach of Act and Rules.
- Take samples of cosmetics for test.
- Maintain record of inspections, samples, seizures, actions.
- Make enquiries and inspections to detect contraventions.
- Investigate complaints.
- Review technical dossier.

Central Government Inspectors may discharge these duties when directed.

### 10. Prohibition of Disclosure of Information
Inspector shall not disclose information acquired during official duties without written sanction of official superior, except for official business or court order.

### 11. Establishment and Functions of Central Cosmetics Laboratory
(1) Central Government may establish Central Cosmetics Laboratory for:
- Analysing or testing samples under section 11(2) or 25(4) of the Act
- Functioning as appellate laboratory
- Any other assigned function

(2) Central Government may designate any laboratory under its control as Central Cosmetics Laboratory, provided it is accredited by National Accreditation Body for Testing and Calibration Laboratories.

(3) Headed by a Director appointed by Central Government.

## Chapter III: Import and Registration

### 12. Import of Cosmetics
(1) No cosmetic shall be imported unless registered by Central Licensing Authority.
(2) Application for registration in **Form COS-1** through online portal (or offline till portal operational) by manufacturer, authorised agent, importer, or subsidiary.
(3) Authorisation by manufacturer to agent must be authenticated.
(4) Documents as per Part I of Second Schedule.
(5) Fee as per Third Schedule.
(6) Additional fee for each category and variant.
(7) Offline application permitted till online portal operational.
(8) Testing fees to be paid to approved laboratory.
(9) Inspection fees for overseas manufacturing site as per Third Schedule.

### 13. Grant of Import Registration Certificate
(1) Central Licensing Authority may grant **Form COS-2** within 6 months or reject with reasons.
(2) Appeal to Central Government within 45 days; decision within 90 days.
(3) For new cosmetic, prior permission in **Form COS-3** required.
(4) Single application and certificate for multiple cosmetics from same manufacturer.
(5) Duplicate certificate fee as per Third Schedule.

### 14. Validity of Import Registration Certificate
- Valid in perpetuity subject to payment of retention fee before completion of 5 years.
- Late fee: 2% per month up to 180 days; non-payment leads to cancellation.

### 15. Fresh Application in Case of Change in Constitution
- Application for fresh registration within 180 days of change.
- Existing registration valid until fresh registration or rejection.
- Inform Central Licensing Authority of changes in labelling, composition, testing within 15 days.
- Amendment for change in name/address within 60 days.

### 16. Suspension and Cancellation of Registration Certificate
- For non-compliance, after show cause, suspend or cancel.
- Appeal to Central Government within 30 days.

### 17. Import of Cosmetics Already Registered
- Application in **Form COS-4** with undertaking in Sixth Schedule.
- Grant of import registration number in **Form COS-4A** valid for 3 years.
- Suspension/cancellation for non-compliance.

### 18. Prohibition of Import of Certain Cosmetics
- No import if manufacture, sale or distribution prohibited in country of origin.
- No import if "Use Before" date is less than 6 months from date of import.
- No import of cosmetics containing hexachlorophene.
- No import of cosmetics tested on animals after 12th November 2014.

### 19. Documents to be Supplied to Commissioner of Customs
Declaration signed by manufacturer/importer.

### 20. Procedure for Import of Cosmetics
- Officer at port may take samples if contravention suspected.
- If defects noticed, advise Customs.
- Sample sent to Laboratory; consignment detained until report.
- If contravention not remediable, importer must send back or destroy within 2 months.
- If remediable, importer may import on undertaking.

### 21. Import of Cosmetic for Personal Use
Small quantities may be imported for personal use if part of passenger's baggage and declared to Customs.

### 22. Import Through Points of Entry
Only through points specified in rule 43A of Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945.

## Chapter IV: Manufacture, Sale and Distribution (Licensing)

### 23. Application for Grant of License or Loan License
(1) Application to State Licensing Authority in **Form COS-5** (license) or **Form COS-6** (loan license) with fee and documents.
(2) New cosmetic requires prior permission in **Form COS-3**.
(3) Self-declaration in **Form COS-7** for GMP compliance.
(4) License granted within 45 days if requirements fulfilled; else inform within 45 days.
(5) After grant, upload copy on CDSCO website.
(6) Inspection within 30 days; if not, license deemed valid.
(7) False information may lead to cancellation.
(8) Duplicate license fee as per Third Schedule.

### 24. Manufacture at More Than One Premises
Separate application and license for each premises.

### 25. Form of License or Loan License
License in **Form COS-8**; Loan license in **Form COS-9**.

### 26. Conditions of License or Loan License
- Manufacture under direction of competent technical staff (Diploma in Pharmacy, registered pharmacist, Intermediate with Chemistry, or Bachelor in Cosmetic Technology).
- Factory premises comply with Seventh Schedule.
- Provide staff, premises, laboratory equipment or arrange with approved laboratory.
- Maintain documentary evidence.
- Comply with Act and Rules.
- Keep batch records as per Eighth Schedule for 3 years after expiry.
- Loan license cancelled if manufacturing license cancelled.
- Test raw materials and final product; maintain records for 3 years.
- Allow Inspector entry and sampling.
- Maintain Inspection Book in **Form COS-11**.
- Inform Licensing Authority of changes in labelling, composition, testing, specification within 30 days.
- Inform change in constitution; current license valid for 6 months.
- Amendment for change in name/address within 60 days.

### 27. Grant or Refusal of License
- Grant **Form COS-8** or **Form COS-9** if satisfied.
- Refuse with reasons and conditions to be satisfied; supply inspection report.

### 28. Further Application After Rejection
Within 6 months, if requirements fulfilled and fee paid, license may be granted.

### 29. Appeal to State Government
Appeal within 90 days to State Government.

### 30. Validity of License
- Valid in perpetuity subject to retention fee before completion of 5 years.
- Late fee: 2% per month up to 180 days; non-payment leads to cancellation.

### 31. Inspection for Verification of Compliance
- Inspect at least once in three years or as per risk-based approach.
- Central Inspectors may carry out special inspections.

## Chapter V: Permission for Import or Manufacture of New Cosmetic

### 32. Permission for Import or Manufacture of New Cosmetic
- Apply to Central Licensing Authority in **Form COS-12** with fee and safety/effectiveness data.
- Central Licensing Authority may grant prior permission in **Form COS-3**.
- Prior permission to be furnished with import or manufacture application.
- Safety evaluation as per IS 4011:2018.

## Chapter VI: Labelling, Packing and Standards

### 33. Prohibition of Sale or Distribution
No person shall sell or distribute any cosmetic unless manufactured by licensed manufacturer and labelled/packed as per rules.

### 34. Manner of Labelling
Inner and outer labels shall contain:
- Name of cosmetic
- Name and address of manufacturer; if not manufactured in own factory, name/address of actual manufacturer or "Made in..."
- Use before/date of expiry (month and year)
- Batch number (preceded by "B" or "Batch No." etc.) – not required for ≤10g solid or ≤25ml liquid
- Manufacturing licence number (preceded by "M" or "M.L. No.") – not mandatory for imported if not required in country of origin
- Net contents (weight/volume)
- Hazard warnings and directions for safe use
- Import registration certificate number for imported cosmetics
- List of ingredients in descending order (if >1%)
- Compliance with BIS standards if applicable
- Export labels: as per destination country, but must include name, batch/lot/serial no., expiry, manufacturer address, licence no., internationally recognised symbols.

### 35. Prohibition Against Altering Inscription
No person shall alter, obliterate or deface any inscription on container/label/wrapper without permission.

### 36. Prohibition Against False or Misleading Claims
No cosmetic may purport or claim anything false or misleading.

### 37. Labelling of Hair Dyes
Hair dyes containing Paraphenylenediamine or other dyes must bear cautionary statement in English and local languages on inner and outer labels, plus instructions for preliminary test.

### 38. Special Provisions for Toothpaste Containing Fluoride
Fluoride content ≤1000 ppm; must be mentioned on tube and carton.

### 39. Standards of Cosmetics
- Must comply with Ninth Schedule or other applicable standards.
- Raw materials in ANNEX A of IS:4707 Part 2 not allowed.
- Dyes, colours, pigments only as per BIS (IS:4707 Part 1 & 2) and Tenth Schedule.
- Limits: Arsenic ≤2 ppm, Lead ≤20 ppm, Heavy Metals ≤100 ppm.
- Hexachlorophene prohibited except in soaps ≤1% w/w with cautionary note.
- Mercury: eye area ≤70 ppm; other cosmetics unintentional ≤1 ppm.
- Lead and arsenic compounds for colouring prohibited.
- Animal testing prohibited.

## Chapter VII: Procedure of Sampling, Seizure and Report

### 40. Testing of Cosmetic from a Purchaser
Application in **Form COS-13**; report in **Form COS-14**.

### 41. Procedure for Sampling
As per section 23 of the Act.

### 42. Forms of Receipts for Seized Cosmetic
Receipt in **Form COS-15**.

### 43. Manner of Certifying Copies of Seized Documents
Return within 20 days after copies signed by Inspector and person.

### 44. Form of Intimation of Purpose of Taking Samples
Intimation in **Form COS-10**.

### 45. Procedure for Dispatch of Sample to Government Analyst
Sealed packet with memorandum in **Form COS-17**; copy of memorandum and seal impression sent separately.

### 46. Form of Order Not to Dispose of Stock
Order in **Form COS-18**.

### 47. Prohibition of Sale
No sale of stock subject to order under section 22(1)(c).

### 48. Procedure on Receipt of Sample
Government Analyst compares seals, tests, supplies report in triplicate in **Form COS-19** with protocols.

### 49. Dispatch of Samples for Test by Order of Court
Sealed packet with memorandum in **Form COS-20** to Director.

### 50. Recording of Condition of Seals
Director or authorised officer records condition of seal.

### 51. Report of Result of Test or Analysis
Report in **Form COS-21** with protocols.

### 52. Issuance of Certificates
Signed by Director or authorised officer.

### 53. Confiscation of Cosmetics, Implements, Machinery
Liable to confiscation on conviction.

### 54. Procedure for Disposal of Confiscated Cosmetics
Inspector reports to Court; if not of standard quality, Court orders destruction; if standard quality, report accordingly.

## Chapter VIII: Approval of Laboratory

### 55. Application for Grant of Approval
Laboratory accredited by NABL may apply to State Licensing Authority in **Form COS-22** with fee.

### 56. Approval for Carrying Out Tests
Granted in **Form COS-23** if conditions met:
- Well-lit, ventilated, air-conditioned premises
- Adequate space
- Suitable equipment
- Qualified person-in-charge
- Qualified testing staff
- Books of standards

### 57. Inspection Before Grant of Approval
Joint inspection by Central and State Inspectors; report within 24 hours.

### 58. Procedure of Approving Authority
Grant **Form COS-23** if satisfied; else reject with reasons.

### 59. Further Application After Rejection
Within 6 months with inspection fee.

### 60. Validity of Licence
Valid if retention fee paid before 5 years; late fee 2% per month up to 6 months; non-payment leads to cancellation.

### 61. Inspection for Verification of Compliance
Joint inspection at least once in three years.

### 62. General Conditions After Approval
- Adequate staff, premises, equipment as per Eleventh Schedule
- Proper storage facilities
- Maintain records of tests for 2 years after expiry (or 6 years)
- Allow Inspector entry
- Report changes in person-in-charge, staff, premises, equipment
- Furnish reports in **Form COS-24**
- Report non-standard samples to Central Licensing Authority and State Licensing Authority
- Maintain Inspection Book in **Form COS-11**

### 63. Withdrawal and Suspension of Approval
State Licensing Authority may withdraw/suspend after show cause; appeal to State Government within 3 months.

## Chapter IX: Miscellaneous

### 64. Exemption of Cosmetics
As specified in Twelfth Schedule.

### 65. Voluntary Recall of Cosmetics
Manufacturer/authorised agent must initiate withdrawal if cosmetic poses risk; inform authorities.

### 66. Fees for Examination of Samples
As per Fifth Schedule.

### 67. Mode of Payment of Fee
Central: through challan/electronic mode in Bank of Baroda; State: as specified.

### 70. Digitalisation of Form

### 71. Amendments in Drugs Rules, 1945
As specified in Thirteenth Schedule.

### 72. Savings

### Fourth Schedule: Categories of Cosmetics
List of 80 categories (e.g., Lip care, Hand care, Foot care, Body care, etc.).

### Fifth Schedule: Fees for Test or Analysis
Physical tests (e.g., Adhesion Strength ₹150, Ash Content ₹100), Chemical tests (e.g., Acid Insoluble Ash ₹200), Instrument tests, Microbiological tests.

### Sixth Schedule: Undertaking for Import
Undertaking by importer regarding compliance, testing fees, quality failures, records, inspection.

### Seventh Schedule: Good Manufacturing Practices
General requirements: location, buildings, personnel, water supply, waste disposal, health/clothing, medical services, maintenance, testing and release, working benches, washing facilities.
Requirements of plant and equipment for various categories (Powders, Skin Powder for Infants, Creams, Nail Polishes, Lipsticks, Depilatories, Eye Preparations, Aerosol, Alcoholic Fragrance, Hair Dyes, Tooth-powders/pastes, Toilet Soaps).

### Eighth Schedule: Manufacturing Records
Particulars to be shown: serial number, product name, batch size/number, dates, ingredients, quantities, control reference numbers, analytical report numbers, production/packing particulars, release date, signature.
Records of raw materials: quantity received, control reference number, issued, batch numbers, disposal.

### Ninth Schedule: Standards for Cosmetics
List of Indian Standards (IS) for various cosmetics (e.g., Skin Powders IS:3959, Toothpaste IS:6356, Lipstick IS:9875, etc.).

### Tenth Schedule: List of Colourants
**Part I**: List of colourants allowed for use in cosmetic products as per IS:4707 (Part 1).
**Part II**: List of colours permitted in soaps.

### Eleventh Schedule: Requirements for Laboratory Approval
General requirements, premises, personnel, equipment, chemicals/reagents, good housekeeping/safety, maintenance/calibration/validation, reference materials, microbiological cultures, quality system, internal audits, management review, SOPs, protocols, raw data, storage/archival.

### Twelfth Schedule: Exemptions
Classes of cosmetics exempted from certain provisions (e.g., cosmetics in bulk for 100% export, for research, duty-free shops, amenity kits, etc.).

### Thirteenth Schedule: Amendments to Drugs Rules, 1945
Omission of words "and cosmetics" from various rules and schedules; omission of certain parts/forms.
