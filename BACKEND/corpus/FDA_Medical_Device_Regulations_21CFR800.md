## Overview

This document contains regulations from **21 CFR Part 800** of the Code of Federal Regulations, which establishes requirements for specific medical devices under the jurisdiction of the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA). The regulations cover:

- **Subpart B**: Requirements for Specific Medical Devices
  - Contact lens solutions (sterility, tamper-resistant packaging)
  - Patient examination gloves and surgeons' gloves (quality control, leakage testing)
  - Over-the-counter (OTC) hearing aid controls

- **Subpart C**: Administrative Practices and Procedures
  - Administrative detention of devices
  - Requests for supervisory review of CDRH decisions

## Subpart B - Requirements for Specific Medical Devices

### § 800.10 Contact Lens Solutions; Sterility

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Section** | 800.10 |
| **Title** | Contact lens solutions; sterility |
| **Authority** | 21 U.S.C. 321, 334, 351, 352, 355, 360e, 360l, 360j, 360k, 361, 362, 371 |
| **Effective Date** | November 5, 1982 (47 FR 50455) |

#### Key Requirements

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Sterility** | All preparations offered or intended for ophthalmic use, including contact lens solutions, must be sterile |
| **Adulteration** | Non-sterile liquid ophthalmic preparations may be regarded as adulterated under section 501(c) of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act |
| **Misbranding** | May be deemed misbranded under section 502(j) of the Act |
| **Container Sterility** | Containers must be sterile at time of filling and closing |
| **Seal Integrity** | Container or individual carton must be sealed so contents cannot be used without destroying the seal |
| **Tamper-Resistant Packaging** | Packaging and labeling must comply with § 800.12 |

#### Multiple-Dose Containers

Liquid ophthalmic preparations packed in multiple-dose containers should:

1. Contain one or more suitable and harmless substances that inhibit microorganism growth; OR
2. Be packaged and labeled with adequate protection and warnings to minimize contamination hazard

#### Eye Cups and Droppers

- Eye cups, eye droppers, and other dispensers intended for ophthalmic use should be sterile
- Should be packaged to maintain sterility until package is opened
- Must be labeled with adequate directions and warnings

### § 800.12 Contact Lens Solutions and Tablets; Tamper-Resistant Packaging

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Section** | 800.12 |
| **Title** | Contact lens solutions and tablets; tamper-resistant packaging |
| **Effective Dates** | February 7, 1983 (solutions); May 5, 1983 (tablets); February 6, 1984 (retail level) |
| **Amendments** | 48 FR 1706, Jan. 14, 1983; 48 FR 16666, Apr. 19, 1983; 48 FR 37625, Aug. 19, 1983; 53 FR 11252, Apr. 6, 1988; 73 FR 34859, June 19, 2008 |

#### Key Requirements

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **General** | Contact lens solutions and tablets must be packaged in tamper-resistant retail packages |
| **Purpose** | Prevent malicious adulteration; improve security of OTC packaging |
| **Adulteration** | Non-compliant products are adulterated under section 501 or misbranded under section 502 of the Act |

#### Tamper-Resistant Package Definition

A tamper-resistant package is one having an indicator or barrier to entry which, if breached or missing, can reasonably be expected to provide visible evidence to consumers that tampering has occurred.

| Characteristic | Description |
|----------------|-------------|
| **Distinctive by Design** | Package cannot be duplicated with commonly available materials or processes |
| **Identifying Characteristic** | Pattern, name, registered trademark, logo, or picture |
| **Intactness** | Must remain intact when handled in a reasonable manner during manufacture, distribution, and retail display |

#### Labeling Requirements

- Each retail package must bear a prominently placed statement alerting consumers to the tamper-resistant feature
- Statement must be unaffected if tamper-resistant feature is breached or missing
- If identifying characteristic is used, it must be referred to in the labeling statement

**Example Labeling Statement:**
> "For your protection, this bottle has an imprinted seal around the neck."

#### Exemptions

- Manufacturers may request exemption via citizen petition under § 10.30
- Petition must be identified as "Request for Exemption from Tamper-resistant Rule"
- Must contain information specified for OTC drugs in § 211.132(d)

#### Effective Dates

| Product Type | Packaging Requirement | Labeling Requirement | Retail Level |
|--------------|----------------------|---------------------|--------------|
| Contact lens solutions | February 7, 1983 | May 5, 1983 | February 6, 1984 |
| Tablets | May 5, 1983 | May 5, 1983 | February 6, 1984 |
| Distinctive indicator | — | May 5, 1983 | — |
| Specific label reference | — | February 6, 1984 | — |

### § 800.20 Patient Examination Gloves and Surgeons' Gloves; Sample Plans and Test Method for Leakage Defects; Adulteration

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Section** | 800.20 |
| **Title** | Patient examination gloves and surgeons' gloves; sample plans and test method for leakage defects; adulteration |
| **Effective Date** | December 12, 1990 (55 FR 51256) |
| **Amendments** | 71 FR 75876, Dec. 19, 2006 |

#### Purpose

- Address HIV/AIDS transmission risk in healthcare context
- Establish quality control for barrier devices (medical gloves)
- Define adulteration for patient examination and surgeons' gloves

#### Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Patient Examination Glove** | See § 800.6250; finger cots excluded from test method |
| **Surgeons' Glove** | See § 878.4460 |
| **Leak** | Appearance of water on outside of glove (watertight barrier failure) |
| **Defect** | Leaks, tears, embedded foreign objects, extrusions, fused gloves, adhering gloves, or other visual defects affecting barrier integrity |

#### Test Method

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Water Volume** | 1,000 mL |
| **Water Temperature** | 20°C to 30°C |
| **Test Duration** | 2 minutes |
| **Examination** | Visual examination and water leak test |
| **Excluded Area** | Top 40 mm of glove not counted as defect |

#### Test Materials

1. 60 mm × 380 mm clear plastic cylinder with hook and 40 mm mark
2. Elastic strapping with velcro or other fastening material
3. Automatic water-dispensing apparatus or manual device (1,000 mL capacity)
4. Stand with horizontal rod for hanging (must hold ~5 kg for 5 gloves)
5. Timer capable of measuring 2-minute intervals

#### Leak Test Procedure

1. **Visual Examination**: Inspect gloves for defects; visually defective gloves counted but not further tested
2. **Leak Test Set-Up**: Attach glove to plastic fill tube at 40 mm mark; fasten with elastic strapping
3. **Water Addition**: Add 1,000 mL room temperature water
4. **Immediate Examination**: Check for water leaks; do not squeeze; use minimum manipulation
5. **Suspension**: Hang assembly vertically from horizontal rod
6. **Second Observation**: Check for leaks 2 minutes after water addition
7. **Recording**: Record number of defective gloves

#### Sampling Plans

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Standard** | ISO 2859, "Sampling Procedures For Inspection By Attributes" |
| **Sample Size** | General inspection level II |
| **Lots ≤ 1,200 gloves** | Single normal sampling |
| **Lots > 1,200 gloves** | Multiple normal sampling |

#### Acceptable Quality Levels (AQL)

| Glove Type | Minimum AQL |
|------------|-------------|
| Surgeons' gloves | 1.5 |
| Patient examination gloves | 2.5 |

### § 800.30 Over-the-Counter Hearing Aid Controls

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Section** | 800.30 |
| **Title** | Over-the-counter hearing aid controls |
| **Authority** | 21 U.S.C. 321, 334, 351, 352, 355, 360e, 360l, 360j, 360k, 361, 362, 371; Sec. 709, Pub. L. 115-52 |
| **Scope** | OTC air-conduction hearing aids |

#### Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Air-Conduction Hearing Aid** | Hearing aid that conducts sound to the ear through the air |
| **Hearing Aid** | Any wearable device designed for, offered for, or represented as aiding persons with or compensating for impaired hearing |
| **Licensed Person** | Person holding a license or degree for diagnosis, assessment, or treatment of hearing loss; or license to sell/distribute hearing aids |
| **OTC Hearing Aid** | Air-conduction hearing aid that does not require implantation or surgery; intended for persons age 18+ with perceived mild to moderate hearing impairment; allows user control/customization |
| **Prescription Hearing Aid** | Hearing aid that is not an OTC hearing aid or does not satisfy OTC requirements |
| **Rebuilt Hearing Aid** | OTC hearing aid inspected, tested, modified, and reprocessed for next user |
| **Sale** | Includes lease, rental, or other purchase/exchange for value |
| **Tools, Tests, or Software** | Components allowing lay user to control and customize device |
| **Used Hearing Aid** | Worn by user for any period; not "used" if worn during bona fide evaluation |

#### Labeling Requirements

##### Outside Package Labeling

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Warning Against Use in People Younger than 18** | "WARNING: If you are younger than 18, do not use this..." |
| **Symptoms of Mild to Moderate Hearing Loss** | Trouble hearing speech in noisy places; hard to follow speech in groups; trouble hearing on phone; listening makes tired; need to turn up volume |
| **Advice of Availability of Professional Services** | When to see a hearing healthcare professional |
| **"Red Flag" Conditions** | Birth defect, injury, blood/pus/fluid, pain, wax, dizziness, sudden hearing change, worse hearing in one ear, tinnitus in one ear |
| **Notice of Contact Information** | Weblink, telephone, email, postal address |
| **Notice of Manufacturer's Return Policy** | Succinct, accurate statement |
| **Statement of Build Condition** | If used or rebuilt |
| **Statement of OTC Availability** | "OTC" and "hearing aid" on principal display panel |
| **Battery Information** | Type, number, whether included |
| **Control Platform** | Whether mobile device or other platform required |

##### Inside Package Labeling (User Instructional Brochure)

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Warning Against Use in People Younger than 18** | Must appear first |
| **"Red Flag" Conditions** | When to see a doctor |
| **Warning About Pain from Device Placement** | Remove if pain or discomfort |
| **Caution About Hearing Protection** | Not hearing protection |
| **Caution About Excessive Sound Output** | Turn down volume if uncomfortable |
| **Caution About Components Lodging in Ear** | Seek medical help if piece stuck |
| **Advice to Seek Professional Services** | Consult professional if concerned |
| **Note About User Expectations** | Will not restore normal hearing |
| **Note About Reporting Adverse Events to FDA** | MedWatch, 1-800-FDA-1088 |
| **Illustration(s)** | Operating controls, user adjustments, battery compartment |
| **Function of Controls** | All user-adjustable controls |
| **Accessory Description** | Wax guards, computer/TV/phone accessories |
| **Specific Instructions** | Sizing, inserting eartip; tools/tests/software; accessory use; maintenance; battery; expected battery life |
| **Physiological Side Effects** | Skin irritation, cerumen accumulation |
| **Technical Specifications** | OSPL90, full-on gain, THD, self-generated noise, latency, cutoff frequencies |
| **Avoidable Events** | Wax buildup, drops, water immersion, heat exposure |
| **Wireless Technology** | EMC, RF exposure warnings |
| **Repair Service** | Address for repairs/replacements |
| **Clinical Studies Summary** | If conducted |

##### Labeling on the Device

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Serial Number** | Clearly and permanently |
| **Battery "+" Symbol** | Unless prevented by design |
| **Used/Rebuilt Tag** | Removable tag declaring fact |

##### Technical Specifications

| Specification | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| **OSPL90** | Maximum output limit value |
| **Full-On Gain** | Gain with 50 dB SPL pure-tone input, volume full on |
| **Total Harmonic Distortion** | THD value |
| **Self-Generated Noise** | Noise value |
| **Latency** | Latency value |
| **Bandwidth** | Upper and lower cutoff frequencies |

#### Software Device Labeling

For OTC hearing aid software not distributed with hearing aid:

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Prior to First Use or Payment** | Compatibility, fees disclosure, required information |
| **Prior to First Use** | Warnings, cautions, all other information |
| **Accessibility** | All labeling accessible after acknowledgment |
| **Changes** | Updated labeling presented until dismissed |

#### Output Limits

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| **General Output Limit** | 111 dB SPL at any frequency |
| **Input-Controlled Compression Activated** | 117 dB SPL at any frequency |

#### Electroacoustic Performance Limits

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| **Total Harmonic Distortion + Noise** | ≤ 5% at 70 dB SPL and 100 dB SPL (sine wave) or 67 dB SPL and 97 dB SPL (pulsed noise) |
| **Self-Generated Noise** | ≤ 32 dBA |
| **Latency** | ≤ 15 ms |
| **Frequency Response Bandwidth** | Lower cutoff ≤ 250 Hz; upper cutoff ≥ 5 kHz |
| **Frequency Response Smoothness** | No single peak > 12 dB relative to average |
| **Acoustic Coupler** | 2 cm³ coupler or scientifically valid alternative |

#### Design Requirements

| Requirement | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Insertion Depth** | Limit insertion so component remains ≥ 10 mm from tympanic membrane |
| **Atraumatic Materials** | Eartip material must be atraumatic |
| **Proper Physical Fit** | Enable safe, customized, acoustically favorable, comfortable fit |
| **Tools, Tests, or Software** | Allow lay user to control and customize |
| **User-Adjustable Volume Control** | Required |
| **Adequate Reprocessing** | If used or rebuilt |

#### Conditions for Sale

| Condition | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Age Minimum** | Sale to persons younger than 18 prohibited |
| **Statement of OTC Availability** | Labeling must bear required statement |

#### Effect on State Law

| Provision | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Preemption** | State/local requirements specifically related to hearing products that restrict OTC hearing aids are preempted |
| **Professional Requirements** | No specialized licensing required for OTC hearing aid activities |
| **Private Remedies** | Section does not modify private right of action |

#### Incorporation by Reference

- **ANSI/CTA-2051:2017**: "Personal Sound Amplification Performance Criteria"
- Available from Consumer Technology Association (CTA)

## Subpart C - Administrative Practices and Procedures

### § 800.55 Administrative Detention

| Field | Details |
|-------|---------|
| **Section** | 800.55 |
| **Title** | Administrative detention |
| **Purpose** | Protect public by preventing distribution/use of adulterated or misbranded devices |

#### Key Provisions

| Provision | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **General** | Procedures for detention of medical devices believed adulterated or misbranded |
| **Criteria** | Authorized FDA representative has reason to believe device is adulterated or misbranded |
| **Detention Period** | ≤ 20 calendar days; may be extended by 10 additional days; maximum 30 calendar days |
| **Issuance** | Written detention notice signed by authorized FDA representative |
| **Approval** | Must be approved by FDA Division Director |
| **Labeling/Marking** | Official FDA tags with required information |
| **Appeal** | Written appeal within 5 working days; informal hearing may be requested |
| **Movement** | No movement except as authorized |
| **Recordkeeping** | Records required for up to 2 years |

#### Detention Order Contents

1. Statement that devices are detained
2. Brief statement of reasons
3. Location of devices
4. Statement prohibiting use, movement, alteration, tampering
5. Identification of detained devices
6. Detention order number
7. Date and hour of order
8. Period of detention
9. Text of section 304(g) of the Act
10. Statement about informal hearing
11. Mailing address, telephone, name of FDA Division Director

#### Appeal Process

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Who May Appeal** | Person entitled to claim devices if seized |
| **Deadline** | Within 5 working days of receipt of detention order |
| **Hearing Request** | Within 5 working days or later date (≤ 20 calendar days) |
| **Presiding Officer** | Office of Regulatory Affairs Program Director or senior FDA official |
| **Decision** | Affirm or revoke detention |

#### Movement of Detained Devices

- Generally prohibited
- Manufacturer may move within establishment to complete final form
- FDA may approve movement for:
  - Preventing interference or harm
  - Destruction
  - Bringing into compliance
  - Other appropriate purposes
