# FORM NO. 158 – Application for Tax Clearance Certificate under Section 420(5)

**Source PDF:** `form-no-158-TAXHEAL.pdf`
**Reference:** [See rule 228], Gazette of India Extraordinary Part II Sec 3(i), Page 2389-2390
**Source site watermark:** https://www.taxheal.com/

## Purpose

Application for Certificate under section 420(5) – Tax Clearance Certificate required in view of departure from India.

To: The Assessing Officer

Request: "I request that Tax Clearance Certificate be granted to me. I hereby apply for a Tax clearance Certificate in view of my departure from India as required under section 420(5)"

## Fields Required

1. Name (Refer Note 1)
2. Name of Father / Husband* (Refer Note 1)
3. Permanent Account Number (if available)
4. Domicile
5. Nationality
6. Present Address (Refer Note 2)
7. Permanent Address (Refer Note 2)
8. Contact details
   - Mobile Number (Country Code + Number)
   - Email ID
9. Nature of Business / Profession in India
10. Address of all the places of Business / Profession in India (Refer Note 2) – Repeat if required
11. Details of previous visits outside India:
    - (i) Date of arrival in India dd/mm/yyyy – Repeat if required
    - (ii) Period of stay outside India – Repeat if required
12. Purpose of visit outside India
13. Departure Details
    - (i) Date of departure dd/mm/yyyy
    - (ii) Details of flight / ship / road journey by which travel is being undertaken
14. (i) Whether exemption is claimed under any section of the Act? Yes / No
    (ii) If yes, then provide section of the Act
15. Destination of journey
16. Date of Intended Return dd/mm/yyyy
17. Passport Number / Emergency Certificate Number details
    - (i) Passport / Emergency certificate number
    - (ii) Date of Issue dd/mm/yyyy
    - (iii) Country where issued
    - (iv) Place where issued

## Verification / Declaration

> I, hereby declare that I have no liability under the Act or the Income-tax Act, 1961 (43 of 1961) (as it existed prior to its repeal) or the Wealth-tax Act, 1957 (27 of 1957) or the Gift-tax Act, 1958 (18 of 1958) (as it existed prior to its repeal) or the Expenditure-tax Act, 1987 (35 of 1987) or the Black Money (Undisclosed Foreign Income and Assets) and Imposition of Tax Act, 2015 (22 of 2015), or that satisfactory arrangements have been made for the payment of all or any of such taxes which are or may become payable by me.
> Further, the information provided above is true and correct to the best of my knowledge. I have not concealed any relevant fact.

## Notes

1. In case of individual, the first, middle and last name shall be provided in full without any abbreviations. In any other case also, the name shall be provided in full.
2. The address shall contain (i) Country/Region, (ii) Flat/Door/Building, (iii) Road/Street/Block/Sector, (iv) PIN/ZIP Code, (v) Post Office, (vi) Area/locality, (vii) District, and (viii) State.
3. Some of the Information in the form would be pre-filled to the extent possible.
