## 2. Purpose

1. Systematic review, analysis, evaluation and interpretation of inspection reports for checking effectiveness of regulatory inspection programme – identify gaps, strengths, trends
2. Identify guidelines requiring development/updating, set up/amend inspection plans and resources, re-structure inspection process format

## 5. Analysis

Reports reviewed for type/category of deficiencies – QMS, production, documentation, QC, validation, effective monitoring of CAPA closure of previous observations, planning and conduct by zonal/sub-zonal offices, SLA officials, subject experts.

## 6. Conclusion

i. Most deficiencies / non-compliances falling under production, quality control & premises; smaller number in QMS and quality risk assessment
ii. Vaccine industries require more inspection related to CPP and routine inspection as per CIP
iii. To carry out more CPP and routine CIP inspections, more Inspectorate staff to be allocated
iv. Training should address compliance with new WHO guidelines, GMP guidelines of other countries vs National GMP guidelines

## 7. Recommendations

### a) For Inspectorate

i. Smaller deficiencies noted in QMS and QRA – impart more training to address in-depth QMS and QRA at earliest or no longer than Q2 2025
ii. Inspection planning per SOP QMS-INS-002 and QMS-INS-003
iii. Reports per uniform format in SOP QMS-INS-004
iv. Observations categorised as Critical, Major, Other as defined in SOP QMS-INS-004 with references to Drugs Act & Rules or WHO guidelines
v. Observations shall include Requirement (R), Evidences (E) and Deficiency (D) per SOP QMS-INS-004
vi. Biological / vaccine site inspection to be carried out by / in presence of qualified Lead Inspector with subject expert from CDL Kasauli
vii. Reports critically reviewed by zonal/sub-zonal heads for team composition, planning, overall compliance to QMS inspection SOPs with clear remarks
viii. Pending compliance of previous inspection to be considered in next inspection for closure

### b) For Industries

i. Highest deficiencies under production, QC & premises – improve facility / QC & premises within timelines not longer than Q4 2025
ii. Impart more training to address production, QC & premises issues
iii. Adhere to revised Schedule-M vide GSR 922(E) dated 28/12/2023 and current WHO guidelines specific to sterile products like WHO TRS 1044 Annexure-2
iv. QMS including change management, deviation control & change control, OOS investigation with timeline of complete CAPA closure, complaint handling
v. Material management including vendor qualification
vi. Premises and Utilities
vii. Production and aseptic control / practice, process validation, equipment qualification and computerised system validation
viii. QC including trend analysis and continuous improvement
