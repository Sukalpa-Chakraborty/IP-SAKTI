# Processing of TM Application in Trade Mark Registry

**Source Type:** Procedural Guidelines / Official Documentation
**Topic:** Trade Mark Application Processing Workflow

---

## 1. Overview of TM Application Processing

- **System Type:** Processing of Trade Mark (TM) applications is conducted entirely through the **TM electronic processing system**.
- **Filing Modes:**
  - **Hybrid Mode:** Applications can be filed both **online** and **offline**.
  - **Online Filing Rate:** Approximately **98%** of applications are filed online.
- **Digitization:** All offline applications are digitized at the initial stage. Thereafter, all file movement is conducted through a complete electronic mode.

---

## 2. Stage-Wise Processing in TMR (Trade Mark Registry)

### A. Pre-Examination Processing

1. **Filing of Application:**
   - Applications may be filed online or offline.
   - After digitization of offline applications, both online and offline applications are merged and proceed for processing through the TM system.

2. **VIENNA Codification:**
   - **Applicability:** If the applied mark consists of figurative elements.
   - **Process:** Codification of figurative elements is done as per the **VIENNA Agreement**.
   - **Exception:** Trade Marks applied as **word per se** directly proceed for examination without Vienna Codification.
   - **Expected Timeline:** All applications requiring Vienna Codification or otherwise should be processed **expeditiously and serially** as per priority based on the **date of filing**.

---

### B. Examination of Applications

- **Allotment:** Applications are allotted for examination by the automated system **serially** based on the **date of filing**.
- **Two-Stage Examination Process:**
  1. **Examiner:** Prepares the First Examination Report.
  2. **Examination Controller:** Evaluates the report.
     - If found proper: Approves it, and the examination report is issued to the applicant.
     - If deficiency noted: Report is reverted/referred back to the Examiner with suggestions for resubmission/reexamination.

- **Outcome of Examination:**
  - **Accepted:** Application is published in the TM Journal.
  - **Objection Raised:** Examination report is issued to the applicant containing office objections.
    - Applicant must reply within **30 days** from the date of receipt of the examination report.

- **Expected Timeline:** Examination should be done **expeditiously and serially** as per priority based on:
  - The **date of filing** of the application, or
  - The **date of last compliance**, if any.

---

### C. Post-Examination Processing

#### 1. Consideration of Reply

- **Deadline for Reply:** Applicant must submit a reply to office objections within **one month**.
  - **Consequence of Failure:** Application is **abandoned** for want of reply.
- **Processing of Reply:**
  - Replies submitted within the prescribed period are considered by authorized officers.
  - Allotment to authorized officers is done through the TM system **serially** based on the **date of filing of the response**.
- **Outcome:**
  - **Accepted:** Application is published in the TM Journal.
  - **Objections Not Met:** A hearing opportunity is offered to the applicant in all cases where a decision can adversely affect the applicant's interest, as per law.
- **Expected Timeline:** Consideration of reply should be done **expeditiously and serially** as per priority based on the **date of filing of response** to the examination report.

#### 2. Show-Cause Hearing

- **Trigger:** If objections are not met after consideration of reply.
- **Process:**
  - Hearing notices are issued **serially** to the applicant/agent through the system.
  - Allotment of cases to Hearing Officers is done by the TM system **automatically**.
- **Expected Timeline:** Scheduling of applications for hearing should be done **serially** based on the **date of consideration of reply** by the authorized officer.

---

### D. Post Advertisement Processing

- **Publication:** After acceptance, the trademark is published in the **TM Journal**.
- **Opposition Window:** **Four months** from the date of publication.
  - If no opposition is filed: The published trademark becomes eligible for registration.
- **Issuance of Registration Certificate:**
  - Done through the automated TM system.
  - Certificate is automatically issued if:
    - No opposition is filed within the prescribed period, or
    - The application has not been withdrawn by the Registrar at the applicant's request.
- **Validity and Renewal:**
  - **Validity:** 10 years from the date of registration.
  - **Renewal:** Can be renewed every 10 years indefinitely by paying the prescribed fee on the prescribed form.

---

### E. Opposition

- **Filing:** Any third party may oppose the trademark after publication.
- **Disposal:** Must be disposed of as per rules after giving a proper hearing opportunity to both parties.
- **Outcomes:**
  - **Opposition Dismissed:** Trade mark proceeds for registration; certificate issued to applicant.
  - **Opposition Allowed:** Application is refused as per law.
- **Expected Timeline:** Opposed applications should be disposed **serially** based on compliance or non-compliance by the party (Applicant or Opponent) as per the Trade Marks Act and Rules.
- **Rectification Proceedings:** Procedure for disposal is similar to opposition procedure, subject to the Trade Marks Act and Rules.

---

### F. Post Registration Trade Mark Management

- **Recordable Changes:**
  - Proprietor name, address, address for service, assignment, registered user, etc.
  - Filed via request on prescribed form with prescribed fee.
- **Objection Handling:**
  - If objections are raised and compliance is required, the request should be processed ordinarily within **30 days** from the date of compliance by the applicant.
- **Notice to Previous Proprietor:**
  - Registry sends a **one-month notice** to the previous registered proprietor for any change in proprietorship by way of assignment or transmission.
  - Request can be processed only after expiry of the one-month notice period.
- **Expected Timeline:** Post-registration requests shall be examined and processed **serially** based on:
  - The **date of filing** of the request.
  - The **date of compliance** of requirements, if any.
- **Renewal Requests:**
  - Processed through an automated TM system **serially** based on the **date of filing** of the request.

---

## Key Takeaways for RAG System

- **Serial Processing:** All stages emphasize serial processing based on priority dates (filing, response, compliance).
- **Automated System:** The TM system automates allotment, scheduling, and certificate issuance.
- **Timelines:**
  - Reply to examination report: **30 days**.
  - Opposition window: **4 months**.
  - Post-registration objection compliance: **30 days**.
  - Notice to previous proprietor: **1 month**.
- **Hearing Opportunity:** Mandatory where a decision can adversely affect the applicant.
- **Validity:** 10 years, renewable indefinitely.
