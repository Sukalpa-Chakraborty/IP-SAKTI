## 1. Introduction to Rasayana

### 1.1 Definition and Meaning

The word **Rasayana** literally means "the path that rasa takes" (rasa: the primordial tissue or plasma; ayana: path). It is considered the science which:
- Restores youth
- Alleviates suffering (diseases)
- Bestows longevity

### 1.2 Core Concept

In Ayurveda, the qualities of the **rasa-dhatu** (plasma tissue) influence the health of all other dhatus (tissues) of the body. Any medicine that improves the quality of rasa is called a **rasayana**, resulting in the strengthening or promoting of the qualities and health of all tissues of the body.

### 1.3 Properties of Rasayana Plants

Rasayana plants are said to possess the following properties:
- Prevent ageing
- Re-establish youth
- Strengthen life
- Strengthen brain and mind
- Prevent disease
- Promote healthy longevity

### 1.4 Historical Context

- Ayurveda dates back about **7,000 years**
- It has eight branches, one of which is **Rasayana Tantra**
- Rasayana Tantra was practiced as an independent clinical discipline primarily as a positive health medicine
- Comprehensive efforts are needed to revive this useful discipline for the welfare of humanity

### 1.5 Rasayana and Stress (Adaptation)

- The ability to adapt to a given habitat is a distinctive feature of all living organisms
- **Hans Selye** defined the non-specific response to any demand as **stress** and the demand as **stressor**
- Three stages of response to stress (General Adaptation Syndrome):
  1. Alarm reaction
  2. Resistance
  3. Exhaustion
- The branch of Ayurveda dealing with the science of adaptation is called **Rasayana Tantra**
- Modern research has proved that herbs listed as Rasayanas possess **adaptogenic properties**

### 1.6 Mechanism of Action

- Rasayana herbs non-specifically activate the **reticuloendothelial system (RES)** and other components of the immune system
- The central nervous system, endocrine system, and immune system participate in intense cross-talk
- By acting on the immune system, rasayanas exert broad-based effects by initiating a cascade of events involving neurotransmitters, hormones, and amines of the stress response cycle

### 1.7 Rasayana as:

- An adaptogen
- An immunostimulant
- An immunomodulator
- Pro-host probiotic
- Anti-mutagenic

### 1.8 Effects of Rasayana Therapy

According to Ayurvedic concept, rasayana therapy simultaneously affects the body and mind and brings about physical and psychic improvement. This therapy:
- Prevents the effects of early ageing
- Develops intelligence
- Increases body-resistance against diseases

In the words of **Charaka**: "With a rasayana, one obtains longevity, regains youth, vitality and vigour, gets a sharp memory, intellect and freedom from diseases, gets a lustrous complexion and the strength of a horse."

**Sushruta** describes a rasayana as one which is anti-ageing, increases life-span, promotes intelligence and memory, and increases resistance to disease.

### 1.9 Key Ayurvedic Concepts Related to Resistance and Immunity

1. The qualitative, quantitative, and functional balance of body-elements maintains strength, which causes resistance to diseases
2. **Ojas** is described as the essence of all dhatus and is considered an excellent body element. Resistance power of the body depends on the quality and quantity of ojas
3. **Bala** (strength or power) is classified as:
   - **Sahaja** (natural, hereditary)
   - **Kalakrita** (variable as per age and season)
   - **Yuktikrita** (acquired by good diet, drug, and exercise)
4. Bala is divided into:
   - **Vyadhi pratibandhakatva** — specific resistance against a specific disease
   - **Vyadhi bala virodhitva** — does not stop onset but minimises severity

### 1.10 Classification of Rasayana Therapy

Rasayana therapy is classified based on:

**Method of Administration:**
- **Kuti-pravesika** (indoor)
- **Vataatapika** (outdoor)

**Effect:**
- **Kaamya** (invigorating and vitalizing)
- **Medhya** (promoting intellectual factors)
- **Achara** (address psycho-somatic activities)
- **Naimittika** (used to promote resistance against disease following illness)
- **Vrishya** (virilitying or sex-stimulant)

### 1.11 Important Note

Before administering rasayana, the individual needs to undergo **Shodhana/Panchakarma** (purification therapy) process.

### 1.12 Rasayana and Vajikarna

- **Vajikarna** are Ayurvedic aphrodisiacs
- No clear-cut distinction between Rasayana and Vajikarna
- Vajikarna are used for production and purification of **shukra** (vital fluid/sperm)
- The vital fluid is considered the essence of all functions of the body which result in fame, beauty, strength, and power

## 2. What are Rasayana?

### 2.1 Definition

The agents which:
- Cure body and mental diseases
- Delay old age
- Increase mental power, generating power, vital energy, eyesight
- Impart intelligence, memory
- Aid proper digestion
- Clear complexion

These nourish the whole body by strengthening the primordial tissue **Rasa** — the essence of all food we take which the body can assimilate.

### 2.2 Mechanism of Action

- If this essence is well distributed in all systems, the body remains healthy
- By their physico-chemical action, Rasayana purify and promote **dhatus** (tissue)
- They augment the body's disease resistance capacity and the ability for restorative reaction
- Counteract all deleterious effects including ageing

### 2.3 Comparison to Alternatives

Rasayana may be compared to **alternatives** which work as blood cleansers by their diuretic and antihepatotoxic action. Alternatives also:
- Restore proper functions of the body
- Increase health and vitality
- Alter body's metabolism so tissues can best deal with functions from nutrition to elimination

### 2.4 Four Stages of Human Life (According to Ayurveda)

| Stage | Age | Characteristics |
|-------|-----|-----------------|
| 1 | Birth to 20 | All tissues of the body grow |
| 2 | 20-40 | Tissues continue to grow |
| 3 | 40-60 | Age of stagnation; mental activities expand, power of judgment increases |
| 4 | After 60 | Senescence starts; metabolism decreases, waste not excreted properly, bone joints become dry and fixed |

The body can be rejuvenated at stages 3 and 4 by Rasayana.

### 2.5 Rasayana Therapy Protocol

#### Pretreatment for Rasayana Therapy

**Samshodhana (Diet restriction):**
- One week before therapy: simple diet of steamed vegetables and fresh fruits
- No sugar, alcohol, or animal products
- Milk and honey allowed

**Purvakarma (Preparatory treatment):**
- Massage with warm oil
- Application of heat on the body
- Sesame oil generally used

**Panchakarma (Cleansing of internal organs):**
- **Virechana** — purging to clean small intestine
- **Basti** — to clean large intestine
- **Vamana** — emetics to clean stomach
- **Nasya** — nasal drops to clear respiratory passage
- **Raktamokshana** — bloodletting (optional)

#### Administration of Herbs

- Patient admitted into a hut (**kuti praveshika**) for administration
- Hut should be pollution-free, well-ventilated, facing north, painted with slaked lime
- Restricted diet along with medication
- Medicine should be fully utilized by body and not passed undigested
- No digestive disturbance (indigestion, hyperacidity, constipation)
- Given in early hours of morning (within three hours of sunrise)
- During mercurial compounds: pure vegetarian diet
- Avoid: excessive sleep, swimming, talking, anger, sadness, desire, excessive happiness, bathing, worry, irritation, depression, sex, fragrances

### 2.6 Post-Treatment

- Start with rice water
- Followed by rice gruel
- Rice and split beans
- Rice with beans
- Finally normal diet with a teaspoonful of ghee
- Daily whole body massage with warm sesame oil
- Makes arteries softer and smoother, changes blood constitution, dissolves clots, forms new cells

## 3. Tridosha

### 3.1 The Three Doshas

| Dosha | Equated With | Function |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Vata** | Air/Bios | Biomotor force, life or vital force |
| **Pitta** | Bile/Fire | Metabolic activity, generates and preserves body heat |
| **Kapha** | Phlegm/Water | Preservative principles, cooling and preservative property |

### 3.2 The Six Rasas (Tastes)

| Rasa | Main Sources | Effects |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Sweet** | Carbohydrates | Nourishes sapta dhatus; good for five senses; excess causes laziness, obesity, diabetes, high BP |
| **Acidic** | Acidic fruits (except amalaki), yogurt, fermented preparations | Stimulates saliva, increases digestion, imparts strength; excess causes hyperacidity, loss of sexual power |
| **Salty** | Minerals | Makes food tasty and digestible; excess causes thirst, heat, premature greying, wrinkles, hair loss, loss of libido |
| **Bitter** | Herbs | Cleans mouth, increases glandular secretion, digests food, strengthens senses, anti-inflammatory; excess causes inflammation, burning, impotence |
| **Pungent** | Spices | Antidote to toxins, kills microbes, causes diuresis; excess reduces power, energy, may cause sedation and hallucination |
| **Astringent** | Tannin-containing unripe fruits, barks, roots | Causes dryness and constipation; excess causes loss of virility, paralysis, nervous system diseases |

### 3.3 Relationship Between Ras and Dosha

| Dosha | Aggravated By | Subsided By |
|-------|---------------|-------------|
| **Vata** | Bitter, Pungent, Astringent | Sweet, Acid, Salt |
| **Pitta** | Acids, Salt, Bitter | Astringent, Sweet, Pungent |
| **Kapha** | Sweet, Acid, Salt | Astringent, Bitter, Pungent |

### 3.4 Vata (Detailed)

**Functions:**
- Differentiates cells into different structures
- Creates blood vessels, lymphatic system, nerves

**Aggravated By:**
- Light, dry, easily digestible food for long time
- Highly nutritious substances with little roughage
- Eating small quantities several times a day
- Excessive cold drinks, excessive work
- Stress from non-fulfillment of goal
- Worry, grief, panic, fear
- Excessive sex
- Sleeping late at night
- Taking bath for long time
- Indigestion and constipation

**Effects:**
- If stable: All five senses, body systems, and organs remain stable
- If corrupted: Arthritis, muscular pain, headache, chronic diseases, untimely death

### 3.5 Pitta (Detailed)

**Functions:**
- Generates and preserves body heat
- Digests food by separating ras, chyme, urine, faeces
- Liberates energy
- Works with liver and spleen to produce blood

**Aggravated By:**
- Hot, spicy, fried, sour, salty food
- Eating late in evening just before sleeping
- Remaining thirsty for long time
- Exposure to heat and sun
- Remaining irritable and angry

**Effects of Excess:**
- Excess body heat
- Hyperacidity
- Haemorrhage
- Loss of calcium
- Urinary diseases (incontinence, burning micturition)
- Headaches, restlessness, irritation, noise phobia

**To Control Pitta:**
- Use cold, soft, sweet, bitter, astringent substances
- Avoid fried, salty, spicy, acidic, non-vegetarian food, alcohol, tobacco, hard work, sunshine

### 3.6 Kapha (Detailed)

**Functions:**
- Cooling and preservative property
- Acts like water jacket of internal combustion engine
- Provides humidity required by various systems

**Aggravated By:**
- Sweet, sour, fatty, highly nutritious foods which are hard to digest

**Effects:**
- Diseases cold in nature: common cold, cough, bronchial troubles
- Loss of appetite, insomnia, laziness
- Increases mucus, saliva, perspiration
- Causes loss of virility

**To Control Kapha:**
- Avoid strong, cold, humid wind
- Avoid excess sleep, sex, greediness, lust

## 4. Rasayana Preparations

### 4.1 Methods of Administration

**Simplest Mode:**
- Take herb as it is, pound it, or use its juice after filtration

**Powder (Churna):**
- Properly dried herbs in powder form
- Mixed with salt or sugar for palatability
- Administered with fluid medium
- Common mediums: boiled lukewarm cow's milk, sugar, honey, cow's ghee, or mixture of milk and ghee

**Infusion:**
- Herb immersed in water (usually overnight), filtered, and used

**Decoction:**
- Herb boiled in water until water reduced to half or one-fourth
- Filtered through cheese cloth

**Distillation:**
- Fresh leaves/flowers/dry root/seed boiled in water in closed container
- Steam condensed
- Volatile active constituents dispersed in water (aqua)

**Ghanasatva:**
- Concentrated, sometimes standardized aqueous extracts
- Thick paste or dry powder form
- Preferred by pharmaceutical industry

**Asav and Arishta:**
- Fermented preparations with self-generated alcohol
- Dissolve ethanol-soluble constituents
- **Arishta** — prepared from decoctions
- **Asav** — made from dry powders
- Fermented in airtight earthen pitchers for 40-50 days
- Long shelf life, taste good, provide immediate energy

**Avleha or Lehya (Linctus):**
- Semi-solid preparations from dry powders, decoctions, pulp of herbs
- With sugary substances, honey, ghee and/or oil
- Sugar boiled in extract/decoction until thick
- Fine powder added to warm thick syrup
- Ghee or oil added when still hot
- Honey added after cooling

**Bhasam:**
- Minerals, gems, or hard animal products calcined at very high temperature
- Ash-like end product heated and powdered repeatedly
- **Shaptuti** — when repeated 100 times
- **Sahasraputi** — when repeated 1,000 times
- Do not degrade during storage

**Ghrita or Ghritam:**
- Preparations made from oil or ghee
- Contain fat-soluble constituents
- Prepared by heating juice, paste, decoction, infusion of herbs in ghee and oil until all water evaporates
- Internal use: 16 months shelf life
- External use: no expiry period

**Pak:**
- Preparations from milk, ghee, dry fruits, herbs, minerals, spices
- Mainly used as nutrients or aphrodisiacs
- Milk boiled until thick and solid
- Condensed milk fried in ghee
- Sugar powder/syrup, herbs, minerals, chopped dry fruits, spices added
- Made into chocolate or candy balls of about 25 g each

### 4.2 Purification of Poisonous Herbs

- **Mardan** (to kill) or **Shodhana** (to purify)
- Actually a process to reduce toxicity for safe therapeutic doses
- Toxic materials boiled or treated with herbs/chemicals in fluids: cow's urine, cow dung solution, water, milk
- After treatment, herb/mineral dried and processed further

### 4.3 Sugar Types

- **Misbri** — indigenous method of refining sugar; lumps of transparent, bold crystals
- Considered cooling effect
- Used in auspicious ceremonies and Ayurvedic medicaments

### 4.4 Salts in Ayurveda

- Five types recognized
- **Black salt** — man-made preparation; good carminative
- Prepared by fusing saltpetre (nitre), Terminalia chebula, and common salt
- In presence of organic matter at high temperatures, salt turns into deep violet amorphous mass

## 5. Herbal Monographs (Selected)

### 5.1 Aak (Calotropis spp.)

**Botanical Name:** Calotropis procera (Ait.) R.Br., C. gigantea (L.) R. Br. ex Ait.
**Family:** Asclepiadaceae

**Distribution:** Common, wild, latex-containing shrubs growing on wasteland in arid zones of India

**Parts Used:** Root bark, latex, flower

**Properties:**
- Root bark: Alterative, tonic, antispasmodic, expectorant, emetic in large doses
- Digitalis-like effect on heart
- Used as substitute of ipecacuanha for inducing vomit
- Helps in dysentery
- Increases glandular secretion, especially bile
- For syphilis, smoked like tobacco
- Antimicrobial agent
- Latex applied externally for chronic skin diseases (leprosy, eczema)
- Called "vegetable mercury"

**Therapeutic Indications:**
- Diarrhoea and dysentery
- Migraine headaches
- Hepatoprotective effect
- Inflammations
- Bronchial asthma
- Cytotoxic effect (anticancerous property)

**Chemical Studies:**
- Calactin, calotropin, calotoxin, uscharidin (all poisonous)
- Powerful bacteriolytic enzyme
- Non-toxic proteolytic enzyme calotropin (more proteolytic than papain and bromelain)

**Toxicological Studies:**
- Accidental instillation of herb juice → local poisoning
- Higher doses of root bark → nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, headache, burning micturition, leucorrhea
- Toxic constituents of latex have caustic effect on mucous membranes
- Calotropin more toxic than strychnine

- **As Rasayana:** Geriatric tonic for general debility, lack of disease resistance, memory loss

**Therapeutic Studies:**

**As Anabolic Agent:**
- Raises total protein level, increases body weight
- Enhanced cellular regeneration, increased lean body mass

**Antioxidant Effect:**

**As Antimicrobial:**

**As Immunomodulator:**

**As Cytoprotectant:**

### 5.44 Som Ras (Amanita muscaria)

**Botanical Name:** Amanita muscaria
**Family:** Amanitaceae
**English Name:** Fly Agaric Mushroom

**Distribution:** Western Himalayas at approximately 2,000 m in rainy season on rotting organic matter

**Description:**
- Crimson red fructification, looks like beautiful flower
- Insects attracted by colour, killed on contact (poisonous nature)
- Crimson umbrella with white scales, contains remains of dead insect bodies
- Fructification 7-20 cm diameter, central stalk 20-25 cm long
- Broad bulbous base with hair-like fibres
- Stalk off-white, fleshy when young, horny on maturity
- Base has white valves
- Ventral side of umbrella has gills enclosing spores

**Vedic Context:**
- Soma mentioned in Rig-Veda (120 hymns devoted exclusively)
- God-narcotic of ancient Indians
- Divine bliss, juice of immortality
- Comparable to tonics, ambrosia, nectar, elixir, panacea, adaptogens, smart drugs, mood elevators
- Use died out when culture changed from hunting to pastoralism and agriculture
- Source became mystery

**Identification:**
- Identified as dark coloured, leafless milky sap-containing creeper — resembles Periploca aphylla and Sarcostemma brevistigma
- These herbs lacked physiological effects ascribed to soma
- Some authors thought: Cannabis, Peganum harmala, Ephedra vulgaris
- Wasson (1972): Fly agaric mushroom (Amanita muscaria)
- Soma-like drink still consumed in Central Asia and Siberia

**Chemical Studies:**
- Hallucinogenic compounds: Muscimol, muscarin, ibotenic acid, tropane alkaloids
- During drying, ibotenic acid changes to muscimol

**Toxicological Studies:**
- Small doses: Relaxes body, imparts peace of mind
- After some time: Hallucinogenic effect begins
- Body becomes semi-conscious with stimulation of nervous system
- Vivid dreams, recollection of old memories, supernatural feelings
- Long-term use harmful

- **In Migraine Headaches:** Helps neurological disorders

- **Hypo-uricaemic Effects:** Significant fall in serum uric acid; alcohol extract more effective
