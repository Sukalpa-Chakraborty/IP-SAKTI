## 1. Preface to the Fourth Edition

### 1.1 Purpose and Recognition

The work reached its fourth edition, indicating it met a recognized want among Indian fellow-subjects for whose instruction and guidance it was originally issued.

### 1.2 Contributions

- **Dr. J. E. T. Aitchison, C.I.E.**, Surgeon-Major in H.M. Indian (Bengal) Medical Service, placed at the author's disposal an elaborate MS. commentary on the Third Edition, embodying his own professional experience in India (extending over many years), and supplying vernacular names of various drugs in Punjabi, Kashmir, and Leh languages.

### 1.3 New Articles Added

Five new articles added to the List of Drugs:

1. **Sugar** (suggested by Dr. Aitchison)
2. **Kerosine Oil** (suggested by Dr. Aitchison) — owing to extensive use for lighting purposes, now obtainable in nearly every bazaar
3. **Cinchona Febrifuge (Quinetum)**
4. **Petroleum**
5. **Rock Salt**

### 1.4 Additional Notices Introduced

- Indian Hemp (Gunjah) smoking in treatment of Tetanus
- Antiscorbutic properties of Amchur (dried Mangoes)
- Lactifuge powers of Sambac flowers (Jasminum Sambac)
- Emmenagogue virtues of Til or Jinjili Seeds (Sesamum Indicum)

## 2. Preface to the Second Edition

### 2.1 Original Purpose

The work was originally published for the use of the **District Vaccinators of Travancore**, whose sphere of action was far removed from regular medical aid.

- Originally published in English and Tamil on opposite pages
- Reprinted in Tamil by the London Missionary Society's Press at Nagercoil
- In 1868, a Malayalam translation by Dresser Ramswamy Rajoo was issued by the Travancore Government

### 2.2 Target Audience

1. **Missionaries** — whose stations are too far removed from regular medical aid
2. **European and Anglo-Indian officials** — attached to Public Works, Forests, Railway, Telegraph, or other Departments; employed in Tea, Coffee, or Cotton plantations; in commercial pursuits
3. **Educated Natives** — who readily avail themselves of knowledge drawn from trustworthy European sources
4. **Medical Officers** — especially at up-country stations, for emergencies such as failure of supply of European drugs

### 2.3 Selection Principles

About 80 medicines were selected based on:

1. **Safety of the drug** — Arsenic, Aconite Root (Bish), Nux Vomica, Indian Hemp, and other powerful medicines were omitted
2. **Acknowledged utility or efficacy** — as proved by European experience
3. **Generally well-known, recognized native name**
4. **Wide distribution and easy procurability** in all parts and bazaars of India

## 3. Introduction: Weights, Measures, and Preparations

### 3.1 Weights

| Mark | Weight |

| ℈ | 20 grains |
| ℈ | 30 grains |
| ℈ | 60 grains |
| ℈ | 90 grains |
| ℈ | 120 grains |

#### Improvised Weights Using Indian Coins

| Coin | Weight |
|------|--------|
| New rupee | 180 grains (3 drachms) |
| Half rupee | 90 grains (1.5 drachms) |
| Quarter rupee | 45 grains (0.75 drachm) |

**Method for Small Weights:**
- Beat a new quarter rupee into a long, thin, narrow plate
- Divide into three equal parts → three 15-grain weights
- Divide one 15-grain part into three → three 5-grain weights
- Divide one 5-grain part into five → five 1-grain weights

**Useful Combinations:**
- Two rupees + half rupee = 450 grains (slightly over 1 ounce)
- Quarter rupee (45 gr) + 15-gr weight = 60 grains (1 drachm)
- 15-gr weight + 5-gr weight = 20 grains (1 scruple)

### 3.2 Measures of Capacity

| Mark | Measure |

### 3.3 Preparations

#### Infusions
- Solid ingredients cut small, sliced, or bruised
- Water should be boiling
- Cover vessel to prevent evaporation
- Strain through muslin or thin rag when cold
- Should be freshly prepared every other day in hot climates

#### Decoctions
- Ingredients subject to boiling
- Boil in covered vessel for specified period
- Strain while hot
- Rapidly spoil in hot climates

#### Tinctures
- Macerate solid ingredients in spirit for 7+ days
- Shake occasionally
- Strain and keep in well-stoppered bottles
- Prevent evaporation with wax and leather cap
- In hot climates, evaporation increases strength over time

#### Powders
- Pulverize as finely as possible
- Compound powders must be uniformly and thoroughly incorporated
- Keep in well-stoppered or corked bottles

#### Pills
- Thoroughly incorporate all ingredients
- Use honey or jaggery for cohesion
- Should be moderately hard
- No pill should exceed 5 grains in weight
- Add Arrowroot or Rice Flour to box to prevent adhesion

#### Ointments
- Avoid animal fats (Lard) — become rancid in hot climates
- Avoid hog's fat due to religious prejudices
- **Allowable animal fat:** Freshly prepared Ghee (clarified butter)
- **Vegetable substitutes:** Kokum Butter, Piney Tallow (expressed Oil of Vateria Indica)
- **Ceromel:** Mixture of wax and honey

## 4. Alphabetical List of Bazaar Medicines and Indian Medical Plants

### 4.1 Abelmoschus, or Edible Hibiscus (Okra)

**Botanical Name:** Abelmoschus (Hibiscus) esculentus, Linn.
**Family:** Malvaceae

**Vernacular Names:**
- Hind.: Bhindi, Ram-turā
- Duk., Punj.: Bhēndī
- Beng.: Dhēras or Dhēnras, Rām-torā
- Tam.: Vendaik-kāy
- Tel.: Benda-kāya
- Mal.: Ventak-kāya
- Can.: Bendē-kāyi
- Mah.: Bhēndā
- Guz.: Bhīndu
- Cing.: Banda-kā
- Burm.: Youn-padi-sī
- Malay: Kachang-lindir

**Properties:**
- Abounds in copious, bland, viscid mucilage
- Valuable emollient and demulcent properties
- Independent of mallow and other European articles of that class

**Uses:**
1. **Decoction:** Boil 3 oz fresh capsules (cut transversely) in 1.5 pints water for 20 minutes; strain and sweeten. Taken as ordinary drink in:
   - Fevers
   - Catarrhal attacks
   - Irritable states of Bladder and Kidneys
   - Gonorrhea
   - All cases with scalding pain and difficulty passing urine
   - Dysentery (especially chronic form)
2. **Inhalation:** Vapour of hot decoction for hoarseness and dry irritable throat
3. **Poultice:** Fresh capsules bruised form efficient emollient poultice

### 4.2 Abrus, or Country Liquorice Root

**Botanical Name:** Abrus precatorius, Linn.
**Family:** Leguminosae

**Vernacular Names:**
- Hind., Duk.: Mulatthī-hindi, Gunj-ka-jar
- Beng.: Jai-shtomodhu-bengala, Kunch-ka-jar
- Punj.: Asl-assus, Mülathī
- Kash.: Shangir
- Tam.: Gundu-mani-vér
- Tel.: Guru-venda-véru
- Mal.: Kunni-kuru-vér
- Can.: Gul-ganji-béru
- Cing.: Olinda-mül
- Burm.: Yu-e-si-anú
- Malay: Akar-saga-merah

**Properties:**
- Possesses many sensible properties and medical qualities of true liquorice-root
- Yields extract similar to officinal liquorice (less sweet, more bitter)

**Uses:**
1. **Syrup for Coughs of Childhood:**
   - Take fresh Abrus roots (larger size, well bruised) 2 oz
   - Abelmoschus capsules sliced 1 oz
   - Boil in 1 pint water for 30 minutes; strain
   - Add 8 oz sugar-candy or honey; boil to syrup consistency
   - Dose: Tea to tablespoonful several times daily when cough troublesome
2. **Extract from dried leaves** (superior to root extract):
   - Pour boiling distilled water on dried leaves
   - Keep vessel on slow fire for 6 hours
   - Strain while hot through flannel
   - Evaporate on water bath to proper consistency

**Note:** Syrups in India readily undergo fermentation; prepare only small quantities.

### 4.3 Acacia, or Babul Bark

**Botanical Name:** Acacia Arabica, Willd.
**Family:** Leguminosae

**Vernacular Names:**
- Hind.: Babul-ka-chal, Kikar-ka-chal
- Duk.: Kali-kikar-ki-chilkā
- Beng.: Babul-sal
- Punj., Kash.: Sāk
- Tam.: Karu-vēlam-pattai
- Malay: Kulit-pokoh-bunga

**Properties:**
- Large thick pieces, coarsely fibrous, deep mahogany colour
- Astringent, bitterish taste
- Excellent astringent (less powerful than some others)
- Obtainable throughout India (fresh or dried)

**Uses:**
1. **Decoction:** 1.5 oz bruised bark in 1 pint water, boil 10 minutes, strain
   - Dose: 1.5 to 2 oz twice daily (or more in chronic diarrhoea)
2. **External/Local Applications:**
   - Injection in Leucorrhoea and other Vaginal Discharges
   - Enema in Piles and Prolapsus (Descent) of Anus
   - Gargle in Sore Throat and Sponginess/Ulceration of Gums
   - Generally used with alum and other agents

### 4.4 Acorus, or Sweet Flag Root

**Botanical Name:** Acorus Calamus, Linn.
**Family:** Araceae

**Vernacular Names:**
- Hind., Duk.: Bach or Vach
- Beng.: Bach, Safed Bach
- Punj.: Warch
- Kash.: Vai
- Tam.: Vashambu
- Tel.: Vasa, Vadaja
- Mal.: Vash-anpa
- Can.: Bajé
- Mah.: Vekhanda
- Guz.: Vaj, Vach
- Burm.: Lene or Linhe
- Malay: Jaringowe

**Properties:**
- Pieces of various lengths, thickness of thumb, flattened, spongy
- Numerous sheath-like, ringed appendages
- Odour peculiar and aromatic
- Taste bitterish, warm, somewhat acrid
- Tonic and stomachic of no small value

**Uses:**
1. **Infusion:** 1 oz bruised root to 0.5 pint boiling water
   - Dose: Wine-glassful twice or thrice daily
2. **Combined with Chiretta:** Equal parts of infusions — good formula for convalescence after fevers, dyspepsia with flatulence, loss of appetite, constitutional debility
3. **Dysentery of Natives (Dr. Evers):**
   - Bruised root-stock 2 oz
   - Coriander seed 1 drachm
   - Black Pepper 0.5 drachm
   - Water 1 pint; boil to 12 oz
   - Adult dose: Wine-glassful 3 times daily
   - Child dose: 1-3 teaspoonfuls sweetened, 2-3 times daily
   - Useful in dysentery, diarrhoea, and bronchitic affections of children
4. **Insect Repellent:** Fresh root drives away fleas and other insects

### 4.5 Aloes

**Botanical Name:** Aloe Socotrina, Linn. and other species
**Family:** Liliaceae

**Vernacular Names:**
- Hind.: Musabbar, Ilvā, Yalvā
- Duk.: Musanbar
- Beng.: Mōshabbar
- Punj.: Elwā
- Kash.: Müssbar, Sibar
- Tam.: Kariya-pōlam, Irakta-pōlam
- Tel.: Mūshāmbaram
- Mal.: Chenna-nāyakam
- Mah.: Musam-bara-bola
- Guz.: Yeliyo
- Cing.: Kalubolam, Kari-bolam
- Burm.: Mo
- Malay: Jadam

**Purification of Bazaar Aloes:**
- Take bazaar Aloes (small fragments) 1 lb
- Boiling water 1 gallon
- Stir well, set aside 12 hours
- Pour off clear liquor, strain remainder
- Mix liquors, evaporate to dryness in sun or over gentle fire

**Properties:**
- Principal use as purgative (dose: 3-6 grains)
- If administered alone, apt to cause griping, nausea
- Generally given with aromatics
- Ill adapted for children, persons subject to piles, pregnant females

**Uses:**
1. **Irregular or Suspended Menstrual Discharge:**
   - Purified Aloes 24 gr
   - Sulphate of Iron 24 gr
   - Cinnamon powder 60 gr
   - Honey sufficient to make mass
   - Divide into 24 pills; take 2 twice daily
   - Do not give during pregnancy or while menstrual discharge present

2. **Hysterical Fits and Flatulent Distension:**
   - Aloes 20 gr
   - Assafoetida 20 gr
   - Beat into mass with honey; divide into 12 pills
   - Take 1 twice daily
   - Useful for headaches from sudden stoppage of menstrual discharge or bleeding piles

3. **Habitual Constipation:**
   - Purified Aloes 18 gr
   - Sulphate of Iron 30 gr
   - Beat into mass with honey; divide into 24 pills
   - Take 1 three times daily after meals
   - Reduce gradually as bowels act gently

### 4.6 Alum

**Vernacular Names:**
- Hind., Beng.: Phitkari
- Punj.: Fatkari
- Kash.: Fatkar, Phatkar
- Tam., Tel.: Pati-karam
- Mal.: Chinik-karam
- Can.: Pati-kara
- Mah.: Patikar, Turati
- Cing.: Sina-karam
- Burm.: Keo-khin
- Malay: Twas

**Properties:**
- Colourless, transparent, crystalline masses
- Acid, sweetish, astringent taste
- Valuable astringent (external and internal)
- Dose: 10-20 grains for adults

**Uses:**
1. **Country Sore Eyes (Ophthalmia):**
   - Children: 3 grains to 1 oz water
   - Adults: Double strength
   - Wash eyes 4-5 times daily
   - If eyelids swollen, bathe with warm milk first

2. **Native Treatment for Sore Eyes:**
   - Finely powdered alum on heated iron plate
   - Add lemon/lime juice until black, soft mass forms
   - Apply hot around orbit (not beneath eyelids)
   - One or two applications (12 hours each) usually suffice

3. **Alum Poultice for Blows on Eye:**
   - 30 grains powdered alum
   - White of egg
   - Rub to coagulum; apply between thin rags

4. **Hemorrhage (Lungs, Stomach, Kidneys, Uterus):**
   - Alum 10-12 grains thrice daily (with or without opium)
   - **Alum Whey:** Boil 2 drachms powdered alum in 1 pint milk for 10 minutes; strain
   - Dose: 1.5-2 oz thrice daily

5. **Excessive Menstrual Discharges and Bleeding Piles:**
   - Alum whey internally
   - External: Cloths saturated with solution of alum in decoction of Galls or Babul bark (2 drachms to 8 oz)

6. **Prolapsus of Anus (especially children):**
   - Same external application as above

7. **Bleeding from Nose:**
   - Injections of alum solution (20 grains to 1 oz water) into nostril

8. **Bleeding from Leech-bites, Cuts:**
   - Powdered alum or saturated solution on compress

9. **Chronic Diarrhoea of Natives:**
   - Alum 10 gr
   - Infusion of Acorus root 1.5 oz
   - Laudanum 5 drops
   - Three or four times daily

10. **Diarrhoea preceding Cholera:**
    - Alum, Catechu, Cinnamon (each powdered) 10 gr
    - Mix with honey; give at a dose
    - Repeat every 1-2 hours

11. **Hopping Cough (after acute stage):**
    - Alum 3-4 grains every 4-6 hours (child 2-3 years old)
    - Or solution: Alum 25 gr, Omum Water 3 oz
    - Dose: Dessertspoonful every 4-6 hours (child 2-4 years old)

12. **Relaxed or Ulcerated Sore Throat, Ulceration/Sponginess of Gums, Salivation:**
    - Gargle: 2 drachms alum in 1 pint decoction of Galls or Babul bark
    - Sweeten with honey

13. **Aphthae or Thrush (infants and young children):**
    - 20 grains finely powdered alum
    - 1 oz honey
    - Apply twice daily with fingertip

14. **Ulcerative Stomatitis:**
    - Alum in fine powder or strong solution

15. **Gonorrhea (false — discharge from sore between prepuce and head of penis):**
    - Solution: Alum 20 gr in 1 oz water
    - Use twice or thrice daily
    - Enforce strict cleanliness

16. **Gleet:**
    - Injection: Alum 3 gr in 1 oz water, twice daily

17. **Leucorrhoea and Vaginal Discharges:**
    - Injections of alum in decoction of Galls or Babul bark

18. **Chronic Spreading and Gangrenous Ulcers:**
    - Finely powdered Alum 4 drachms
    - Finely powdered Catechu 1 drachm
    - Opium 0.5 drachm
    - Ceromel/Kokum butter/Ghee 1 oz
    - Rub opium with ceromel first, then incorporate other ingredients
    - Apply on soft rag night and morning

19. **Bed-Sores (Dr. Aitchison):**
    - Burnt alum 30 grains
    - White of egg
    - Mix well; paint over part

20. **Enlargement of Joints (Knee), Swellings from Blows, Bruises, Sprains:**
    - Alum 4 drachms
    - Vinegar and Arrack, each 1 pint
    - Dissolve; keep cloths wet with lotion constantly applied

21. **Scorpion Bites:**
    - Alum moistened with water; apply locally (often instant relief)

### 4.7 Assafoetida

**Vernacular Names:**
- Hind., Duk., Beng., Punj., Mah., Guz.: Hing
- Kash.: Yang
- Tam.: Kāyam, Perun-gāyam
- Tel.: Ingura
- Mal.: Perun-gāyam, Kāyam
- Cing.: Perun-kāyam
- Burm.: Shinkhu or Shingu
- Malay: Hingu

**Properties:**
- Moist and strongly smelling kinds best for medical purposes
- Given as pill (5-10 grains) or mixture
- **Mixture:** Rub down 5 drachms Assafoetida in 1 pint hot water; strain
- Dose: 1-2 tablespoonfuls

**Uses:**
1. **Hysterical Fits, Fainting, Nervous Palpitations:**
   - Liquid form when symptoms urgent
   - Solid form (with aloes) to combat tendency

2. **Flatulence, Flatulent Colic, Spasmodic Affections of Bowels:**
   - Enema: 30 grains in 4 oz water
   - Or by mouth in liquid form
   - **Children's Flatulent Colic:** Teaspoonful of mixture with Omum water
   - **Convulsions of pale weakly children:** May be tried

3. **Thread Worms (Rectum and Lower Bowel):**
   - Assafoetida enema

4. **Obstinate Coughs of Childhood (remaining after inflammation):**
   - Mixture: Teaspoonful 4-5 times daily

5. **Advanced Stages of Hooping Cough:**
   - Same as above

6. **Chronic Bronchitis and Asthma of Adults:**
   - Recommended (pill form to avoid disagreeable smell/taste)

### 4.9 Atis, or Atees

**Botanical Name:** Aconitum heterophyllum, Wallich
**Family:** Ranunculaceae

**Vernacular Names:**
- Hind.: Atis
- Duk.: Atvikā
- Punj.: Atis, Batis, Patis
- Kash.: Mohand-i-guj-safed, Hong-i-safed
- Tam.: Ati-vadayam
- Tel.: Ati-vasa

**Properties:**
- Small tuberous roots, tapering to point
- 1-1.5 inches long, 3/8 to 1/4 inch thick
- Grey externally, slightly wrinkled, rootlet scars
- Internally white, farinaceous, inodorous
- Pure bitter taste (devoid of acidity or astringency)
- **Distinguishing character:** Pure bitter taste distinguishes from all other roots sold under same name

**Important Testing:**
- Break every root across
- Discard any not pure white with short, starchy fracture and pure bitter taste
- If chewing causes tingling or numbness — **do not use**

**Active Principle:** Atisine (discovered by Mr. Boughton)

**Uses:**
1. **Intermittent Fever and Periodical Fevers:**
   - Dose: 30 grains mixed with water every 4-6 hours during intermissions
   - Commence during or towards close of sweating stage
   - Children: Reduce by one-half to three-fourths according to age

2. **Debility after Fevers and Other Diseases:**
   - Excellent tonic: 5-10 grains thrice daily

**Properties:**
- Half-ripe fruit best for medical use

**Uses:**
1. **Diarrhoea and Dysentery:**

2. **Irregularity of Bowels (alternating Diarrhea and Constipation):**
   - One draught early morning often regulates bowels
   - If debility and weak stomach, may disagree — try smaller doses or at bedtime

### 4.30 Datura

**Botanical Name:** Datura alba, Linn. and Datura fastuosa, Linn.
**Family:** Solanaceae

**Vernacular Names:**
- Hind., Duk., Beng., Punj., Guz.: Dhatūrā
- Tam.: Ummattai

- Tel.: Ummetta, Duttūramu
- Mal.: Ummatta
- Can.: Ummatte
- Cing.: Attana
- Burm.: Padāyin
- Malay: Kachubung

**Properties:**
- White and purple varieties common throughout India
- Same medicinal properties (purple generally regarded as more powerful but no evidence)
- Valuable medicine — much caution necessary
- Over-doses = powerful narcotic poison

**Preparation — Tincture of Datura:**
- Macerate 2.5 oz bruised Datura seeds in 1 pint Proof Spirit for 7 days (closed vessel, occasional shaking)
- Press, filter, measure
- Add sufficient Proof Spirit to make 1 pint
- Produces sedative and narcotic effects similar to Opium (great saving — Opium expensive)
- **Dose:** Commence with 10-12 drops in water; increase to 20-30 drops
- **General rule:** 20 drops ≈ 1 grain Opium
- **Effect on pupil:** Dilates pupil — examine eye occasionally
- If pupil very large and dilated = sign medicine carried as far as safe

**Uses:**
1. **Cataract and Other Diseases of Eye (substitute for Belladonna):**
   - Watery extract of leaves applied around eyes (dilates pupils)
   - Alcoholic extract of seeds: Macerate 0.5 oz seeds in 4 oz country spirit; evaporate to dryness; dissolve residue in 1 oz water
   - Solution ≈ 4 grain (to oz) solution of Atropine

2. **Asthma (smoking):**
   - Dried leaves and stem cut small; smoked like tobacco in pipe
   - Benefit immediate and striking in some; little effect in others; injurious in few
   - **Dose:** 10-20 grains dried plant to commence; may increase to 30 grains
   - **Discontinue immediately** if giddiness, sickness, or unpleasant symptoms
   - **Preventive:** Smoke pipe last thing at night
   - Keep pipe filled by bedside for immediate use on attack
   - **Note:** Serious/fatal consequences have followed incautious use

3. **Chronic Coughs (violent paroxysms, hard, dry, scanty expectoration):**
   - Smoking Datura beneficial

4. **Rheumatic Swellings of Joints, Lumbago, Painful Tumours, Nodes:**
   - **Poultice:** Bruise fresh leaves into pulp; mix with equal weight rice flour + water to poultice consistency
   - **Epithem:** Steep entire leaves in arrack/spirit; place wet over seat of pain; secure with bandage
   - **Fomentation:** Infuse leaves in boiling water (1 oz to 1 pint fluid); apply as directed
   - **Liniment:** Macerate 1 oz bruised seeds in 1 pint Sesamum/bland oil for 7 days; strain
   - **Additional uses:** Painful/difficult Menstruation, painful Uterine affections (place over lower abdomen), Neuralgic pains (especially face)

5. **Tetanus or Lock-jaw (consequent on wound):**
   - Poultices of leaves renewed 3-4 times daily kept constantly to wound
   - Cleanse wound by irrigation of tepid water if covered with discharge/slough
   - **Tincture of Datura:** 20-30 drops in water 3-4 times daily internally
   - Regulate by effect: Continue till full pupil dilatation with some giddiness, drowsiness, confusion of ideas
   - If spasms abate (recur at more distant intervals, less severe), continue smaller doses at longer intervals till cease
   - If no abatement after specific effects, discontinue
   - Datura liniment well rubbed along spine several times daily
   - Confine to darkened room; protect from cold draughts
   - Open bowels with turpentine enemas if necessary
   - Support strength with strong beef-tea, mutton-broth, eggs beaten with milk, brandy-mixture or stimulants
   - If cannot swallow, give in enemas (not more than 4 oz at a time — larger quantities not retained)

6. **Guinea Worm:**
   - Datura poultice most useful in relieving pain and hastening expulsion of worm

### 4.44 Leeches

**Vernacular Names:**
- Hind.: Jok
- Duk., Beng., Punj.: Jonk
- Kash.: Drik
- Tam.: Attai
- Tel.: Attalu, Jela-galu
- Mal.: Atta
- Can.: Jigani
- Guz.: Jala
- Cing.: Kudallu, Pundal
- Burm.: Miyon, Minyon
- Malay: Lintah

**Properties:**
- Procurable during monsoon near tanks and swamps
- Blood-extracting capacity proportional to size
- **Small leeches:** Abstract 2.5 times own weight
- **Small middle-sized:** 4 times
- **Large middle-sized:** 5.5 times
- **Large:** Nearly 6 times
- **Middle-sized (1.5-2 inches at rest):** Preferable in all cases
- **Very small leeches:** Objectionable (many bites, long application, indefinite oozing, difficulty arresting flow)
- **Very large leeches:** Objectionable (large gaping wound, ugly scar — especially childhood/infancy)

**Application:**
- One or two: Hold in hand to desired spot
- Several: Put in wine-glass; hold to surface till all take

**Making Leeches Bite:**
1. Thoroughly cleanse skin with soap and water; dry
2. Remove from water; keep in dry, warm cloth for 10 minutes
3. Smear skin with cream or sugared milk
4. Apply small mustard poultice over spot
5. After cleansing with hot water, apply leeches (bite more readily, flow greater)
6. Make small puncture/scratch; smear blood over surface
- **Note:** Fumes of sulphur, vinegar, or tobacco often prevent biting

**For Particular Spots:**
- Take blotting-paper; make as many small holes as leeches
- Holes correspond to spots for application
- Cover with wine-glass/tumbler
- Leeches creep to holes; bite exposed skin
- Blotting-paper easily removed by moistening

**Removing Leeches:**
- Generally drop off in 15 minutes or less
- If not: Sprinkle with salt or vinegar, or touch with piece of onion (old Bengali practice)

**Promoting Bleeding:**
- Hot fomentations

**Arresting Bleeding:**
- Burnt rag; firm pressure with finger
- Piece of tobacco leaf, spider's web, or nap off a hat (sometimes better than burnt rag)
- If fail: Powdered Alum or Sulphate of Copper

**If Leeches Get into Rectum/Nostrils/Other Passages:**
- Injection of vinegar or salt solution
- Or simply touch with vinegar or salt solution

**Contraindications for Application:**
- Not immediately over large prominent vein
- Not to eyelids
- Not to bosom of woman (especially during pregnancy)
- Not to loose skin of penis or scrotum (bites apt to be followed by infiltration/inflammation)

**Additional Care for Young Children:**
- Bleed more freely than adults
- Place where bone near surface (pressure may be made against it)
- Morning best time
- Evening: Bites may burst out bleeding during sleep — child may die from haemorrhage (cases on record)
- **General rule:** 1 leech per year of age up to 6; after that up to adolescence, latter number continues sufficient in ordinary cases

**Therapeutic Applications:**
1. **Fevers with Much Headache:**
   - Early stages only
   - Young and vigorous: 4-6 leeches to each temple
   - Sometimes most relief at nape of neck (head joins spine)
2. **Severe Pain in Chest or Abdomen During Fevers:**
   - 8-10 leeches immediately over seat of pain
3. **Severe Headache/Fulness of Head (from stoppage of bleeding piles):**
   - Leeches close to anus
   - Great care to prevent creeping into rectum
   - From sudden stoppage of Menstrual Discharge: Apply to inner part of thighs
4. **Acute Dysentery:**
   - 6-9 leeches to verge of anus
   - Relieves pain and straining at stool
   - Also beneficial in Congestion of Liver (may place over liver region, but fewer number afford greater relief at verge of anus)
5. **Local Inflammations of Skin, Incipient Abscesses, Boils, Bruises, Sprains, Blows (much pain and heat):**
   - 6-8 leeches; follow with hot fomentations
   - Relieve pain, cause subsidence of inflammatory action
6. **Obstinate Vomiting:**
   - Few leeches to pit of stomach (after ordinary means failed)

### 4.46 Lime

**Vernacular Names:**
- Hind.: Chunah, Chun
- Duk.: Chunnah

- Mal.: Nira

- Guz.: Chun

- Malay: Kapor

**Lime Water Preparation:**
- Add 2 oz Slaked Lime to 1 gallon Water (stoppered bottle)
- Shake well 2-3 minutes

- Otherwise: 12 hours at least before use
- Only clear water (holding Lime in solution) employed
- Keep supply ready; remains good long time in well-stoppered bottles
- **Dose:** 1-3 oz twice or thrice daily (best in milk)

**Saccharated Solution of Lime:**
- Carefully mix: Slaked Lime 1 oz + Powdered White Sugar 2 oz
- Add to 1 pint Water (as above)
- Keep in well-stoppered bottle
- **Dose:** 15-20 drops/minims in milk 2-3 times daily
- Better adapted for diseases of childhood and infancy

**Uses:**
1. **Acidity of Stomach, Heartburn, Indigestion (connected with acidity):**
   - Lime Water 1-2 oz
   - Speedily and permanently effectual
   - Especially when urine scanty/high coloured, vomiting/acid eructations prominent
   - Best given in milk

2. **Diarrhoea (arising from Acidity):**
   - Best in solution of gum arabic or other mucilage
   - **Obstinate cases:** 10 drops Laudanum with each dose
   - May combine with Omum water
   - **Chronic Dysentery:** Same treatment sometimes useful
   - **Enemas:** Lime Water diluted with equal part tepid milk/mucilage
   - **Especially adapted for:** Diarrhoea and Vomiting of Infants/young children from artificial feeding

3. **Obstinate Vomiting:**
   - Few doses Lime Water in milk
   - Try in Vomiting attendant on advanced stages of Fever
   - Thought to arrest black vomit of yellow fever
   - Valuable in Pyrosis (Waterbrash)

4. **Irritation of Genital Organs (Pruritus Pudendi):**
   - Bathe parts well with tepid Lime Water 3-4 times daily
   - **Leucorrhoea and Vaginal Discharges:** Injections of 1 part Lime Water + 2-3 parts Water

5. **Scrofula:**
   - Lime Water 0.5 oz in Milk 3-4 times daily
   - Especially when abscesses and ulcers continually forming
   - Requires perseverance
   - **Scrofulous and Other Ulcers (much discharge):** Lime Water as local application
   - **Syphilitic Ulcers/Chancres:** Black Wash (Lime Water 0.5 pint + Calomel 30 grains) — keep constantly applied on lint/rag

6. **Skin Diseases (much secretion, irritation/burning):**
   - Lime Water pure or conjoined with oil

7. **Sore or Cracked Nipples:**
   - Very serviceable

8. **Discharges from Nose and Ears (scrofulous and other children):**
   - Diluted with equal part water or milk — useful injection

9. **Consumption:**
   - Lime Water and milk strongly recommended as ordinary beverage

10. **Diabetes:**
    - Same diet-drink advised (little dependence as cure; may produce temporary benefit)

11. **Thread Worm:**
    - Enemas of 3-4 oz Lime Water, repeated 2-3 times

12. **Poisoning by Mineral Acids:**
    - Lime Water plentifully in milk — antidote of no mean value
    - Also in Poisoning by Arsenic

13. **Burns and Scalds:**
    - **Lime Liniment:** Equal parts Lime Water + bland oil (Olive, Linseed, or Sesamum)
    - Shake thoroughly; apply freely over burnt surface
    - Keep parts covered with rags constantly wetted
    - **Smallpox:** Lime Liniment on cotton-wool applied to pustules — said to prevent pitting

- Require thorough grinding with water
- Activity increased if previously deprived of fixed oil by expression
- Long keeping loses pungency — use fresh seeds when possible

**Uses:**
1. **Emetic (English Mustard):**
   - Full teaspoonful (piled up) in tumblerful warm water
   - Produces free vomiting (if not, repeat in 5-10 minutes; third dose after similar interval)
   - If fail, try other emetic
   - **Indicated in:** Drunkenness, Narcotic and other Poisoning, stomach overloaded with hard indigestible food or intoxicating drinks
   - Simply unloads stomach without depressing effect
   - **Doubtful:** Country Mustard safe as internal medicine

   - **Preparation:** Mustard flour mixed to poultice consistency with water or vinegar; spread on stout brown paper/rag; apply to skin

     - Some skins very susceptible: Remove at once if great pain

     - Fevers/acute disease: Morning or early part of day preferable to evening

   - Mustard poultices to feet and calves
   - Relieve affection of head
   - **Mustard foot bath:** Ordinary foot bath + handful Mustard (more effectual)
   - Water as hot as can be borne; higher up leg, better
   - **Delirium Tremens:** Use every night before bedtime

4. **Head Affections (early stages of Insanity, Delirium Tremens with determination of blood to head):**
   - Envelope legs and lower abdomen in clothes steeped in Mustard + hot water
   - Cold wet towel round head
   - Calming effect; occasionally productive of sleep
   - Mustard foot bath also worthy of trial (repeated every night before bedtime)

5. **Dropsy:**
   - **Whey:** Boil 0.5 oz bruised seed in 1 pint milk; strain
   - Give daily in divided doses

6. **Cholera, Colic, Spasms of Bowels (unattended by inflammation):**
   - Mustard poultice over abdomen — considerable relief

7. **Vomiting (especially accompanying Fevers):**
   - Mustard poultice to pit of stomach
   - **Cholera (patient very low):** Place over heart or left side of chest

8. **Coughs (much difficulty of breathing):**
   - Mustard poultices to chest — relief
   - Also on back between shoulder-blades
   - For children as well as adults
   - **Hooping Cough:** Occasionally much relieved by Mustard poultices along spine

9. **Toothache, Faceache, Neuralgic Pains of Head and Face:**
   - Mustard poultice over seat of pain — frequently relieved

- Mal.: Kashakasha-karappá
- Can.: Afimu
- Mah.: Afin
- Guz.: Afim
- Cing.: Abin
- Burm.: Bhain, Bhin
- Malay: Afiun

**Important Cautions:**
- **One of most valuable medicines when properly employed** — very powerful; may cause great mischief if used without care/caution or in unsuitable cases
- Bazaar Opium always more or less adulterated — uncertainty of operation
- **Recommendation:** Out-stations should always be provided with genuine Smyrna or Turkish Opium in:
  - 1 grain pills (impermeable covering)
  - Tincture (Laudanum) — 15 minims contain 1 grain Opium (recently prepared)
  - **Caution:** Evaporation of spirit increases strength in hot climates
  - Old Laudanum: 10 minims or less may contain 1 grain Opium
- **Keep preparations under lock and key** — Natives have little moral courage to resist

**Kinds of Opium:**
1. **Patna Garden Opium:** Prepared exclusively for medicinal purposes; square packages 2-4 lbs; covered with talc and brown wax; solid, brittle in cold, brown, fine smell; yields 7-8 (or 10) % Morphia
2. **Malwa Opium:** Flat circular cakes ~1.5 lbs; no exterior covering; dull opaque blackish brown; odour like Smyrna but less powerful with slight smoky smell; intensely bitter; yields 3-5% Morphia
3. **Superior Malwa Opium:** Smaller balls/cakes ~10 oz; covered with coarse dust of broken poppy petals; dark brown; homogeneous; yields 7-8% Morphia
4. **Other varieties:** Himalayan/Hill Opium, Kandeish, Kutch — less applicable (varying strength)

**Points to Keep in Mind:**
- **Idiosyncrasy:** Some persons very intolerant — smallest dose produces great nervous excitement, violent headache, vomiting; avoid if known
- **Infants and young children:** Bear Opium badly — 3 drops Laudanum have proved fatal; use only under professional advice
- **Pregnancy:** Avoid as far as possible — habitual use prejudicial effect on foetus
- **Previous habits:** Confirmed opium eater requires far larger dose
- **Inability to swallow:** Give in enema — larger dose (or even half larger) required than by mouth
- **Whenever in doubt:** Take safer course and avoid it

**Therapeutic Uses:**
1. **Rheumatism, Pains of Different Kinds, Cancer, Carbuncles (Rajah Boils), Abscesses, Ulcers (Leprous/Syphilitic/Scrofulous):**
   - Pain (especially at night) banishes sleep — Opium valuable
   - Adult: Commence 10-15 drops Laudanum ~0.5 hour before bedtime
   - If succeeds in giving sleep: Repeat nightly
   - If not: Double second night; double again third night
   - **Not advisable:** Go beyond this quantity except under professional advice
   - After week or two: Doses lose power — cautiously increase
   - **Improvement:** Decrease quantity gradually rather than leave off entirely

2. **Sleeplessness and Restlessness of Delirium Tremens:**
   - Opium as above
   - Combine each dose with 4-5 grains Camphor (pill)
   - Camphor alone 2-3 grains every 3 hours — most soothing influence
   - **Opium at bedtime:** Give alone

3. **Spasmodic Affections of Bowels, Colic, Passage of Gall Stones (pain violent):**
   - Full dose: 20 drops Laudanum in wineglassful Omum or Infusion of Sweet Flag root
   - If no relief in 0.5 hour: Repeat; third dose after another 0.5 hour if pain continues
   - **External:** Hot water fomentations, turpentine stupe, or mustard poultice
   - **After subsidence:** Dose of Castor Oil advisable (especially if attack from crude/indigestible food)

4. **Cholera:**
   - Large and repeated doses (especially solid form) in all stages — fraught with danger
   - **Judicious administration** at proper time/cases: Much good
   - **Indiscriminate use:** Worst consequences
   - **Outset:** Few remedies more useful (combined with Acetate of Lead)
   - **Dr. Ayre's Calomel Treatment:** 1-2 grains Calomel + 1-5 drops Laudanum every 5-15 minutes till Laudanum reaches 60-80 drops — discontinue
   - **Essential part:** Free use of cold water as drink

5. **Stone in Bladder, Gravel, Irritable/Painful Affections of Kidneys:**
   - Full doses as above
   - **More effectual:** Introduced into rectum as suppository (2 grains Opium + 4 grains Soap) or enema (30-40 drops Laudanum in 2 oz thin conjee water)

6. **Retention of Urine (Spasmodic Stricture of Urethra):**
   - Hot bath + full dose Opium (25-30 drops Laudanum); follow with Castor Oil
   - Recent cases of no great severity following debauch, exposure to wet
   - **Enema:** 2-3 oz rice conjee sometimes succeeds when fails by mouth

7. **Diabetes (especially old cases in aged):**
   - Occasionally most beneficial
   - Full doses, persevere, watch effects
   - Diminish/discontinue if headache or bad symptoms
   - Persons with this disease take large doses with impunity

8. **Painful Affections of Uterus:**
   - Suppository or enema
   - **Camphorated Opium Liniment:** Warm, rub into loins
   - Hot rice poultice sprinkled with Laudanum over lower abdomen
   - **Internal:** Full doses; combine with Camphor
   - **After-Pains:** 15-20 drops Laudanum in wineglassful Camphor julep, Omum Water, or simple conjee — speedy relief
   - **Threatened Abortion (from fall, over-exertion):** Same dose Laudanum + perfect rest recumbent; repeat if great restlessness/pain

9. **Dysentery (Natives):**
   - Full and repeated doses (1-2 grains 3-4 times daily) — formerly great repute
   - Fallen into disuse since Ipecacuanha treatment reintroduced
   - **Natives:** Seems to answer better than Ipecacuanha
   - **Preliminary dose:** 25-30 drops Laudanum — enables stomach to bear Ipecacuanha, prevents emetic operation
   - **Local pain/bearing down/straining:** Small enema (2 oz) of conjee + 30-40 drops Laudanum — more relief than anything else
   - Valuable adjunct to Catechu and other astringents in Diarrhoea

10. **Vomiting:**
    - Few drops Laudanum (5-10) in effervescing draught or little Omum Water — speedy relief
    - Combine with Infusion of Cloves and other remedies

11. **External/Local Diseases:**
    - **Simple Opium Liniment:** Rub down 1 drachm bazaar Opium in 2 oz Cocoa-nut/Sesamum/bland Oil
    - **Camphorated Opium Liniment:** Equal quantity Camphor Liniment — excellent application for many painful external affections
    - Shake well before use; apply night and morning (or oftener)
    - **Caution:** Do not apply to abraded/sore surface; only for sound skin; not if pain attended with much heat and redness (use cooling lotions)
    - **Hooping Cough:** Camphorated Liniment well rubbed along spine — occasionally very useful
    - **Stiff Neck:** Warm Laudanum rubbed in

12. **Ophthalmia (great intolerance of light):**
    - Fumigate eye with vapour of boiling water + teaspoonful Laudanum or couple grains Opium
    - **Eyewash:** Laudanum + Vinegar + Brandy (each 1 part) + Water 4 parts

13. **Toothache (decayed tooth):**
    - Grain of Opium in hollow; do not swallow saliva

14. **Earache:**
    - Mixture equal parts Laudanum + bland oil; insert into outer passage on cotton wool (do not push too far)

15. **Painful Piles (much swelling and heat):**
    - Soft rice poultice sprinkled with Laudanum, or smeared with simple Opium Liniment

### 4.59 Physic Nut Plant
