## 1. Introduction

### 1.1 Global Health Challenge

Age-related onset of pathogenesis presents a substantial healthcare and economic challenge before the global society.

- By 2050: 2 billion people aged 60+ and 2 billion under age 15 (each 21% of world population)
- 63% of all deaths worldwide emanate from non-communicable diseases (cardiovascular diseases, cancers, chronic respiratory diseases, diabetes)
- Cumulative output loss of US $47 trillion over next two decades (75% of global GDP in 2010)

### 1.2 Research Hypothesis

**Central Question:** Can Ayurveda—the world's most comprehensive, personalized, holistic and sustainable healthcare system—provide solutions to substantially delay age-related pathogenesis?

**Caveats:**
- Drug is NOT intended as anti-aging
- Aimed at extending **health span** (healthy and disease-free years), not life-span
- Must be accessible to rich and poor alike (crude: INR 3.00/day; extract capsules: INR 10.00/day)

### 1.3 Methodology

**Step 1:** Studied ancient Ayurveda texts (Brihattrayi):
- Charaka Samhita (900 BCE – 600 BCE)
- Sushruta Samhita
- Vagbhatta's Ashtanga Hridayam

**Step 2:** Identified candidate herbs for target diseases:
- Cardiovascular diseases
- Cancers
- Chronic respiratory diseases
- Diabetes

**Step 3:** Assembled database of scientific research on Ayurveda, phytomedicine, and ethnomedicine

**Step 4:** Compared and found concordance between ancient texts and modern research

**Step 5:** Assessed efficacy and safety through in vitro, in vivo, and clinical studies

### 1.4 The JVN-8 Formulation

**Composition:** 8 medicinal plant species in specific proportions:

| # | Botanical Name | Common Name |
|---|----------------|-------------|
| 1 | Terminalia bellirica (Gaertn.) Roxb. | Bibhitaki |
| 2 | Terminalia chebula Retz. | Haritaki |
| 3 | Phyllanthus emblica Gaertn. | Amalaki |
| 4 | Piper longum L. | Pippali |
| 5 | Piper nigrum L. | Maricha |
| 6 | Zingiber officinale Roscoe | Shunthi |
| 7 | Tinospora cordifolia (Thunb.) Miers | Guduchi |
| 8 | Withania somnifera (L.) Dunal | Ashwagandha |

**Approach:** Reverse pharmacology — safety established by ethnomedicinal use, long-term Ayurvedic use, and clinical studies of drugs containing one or more of the species.

**Pilot Study:** Nonrandomized, uncontrolled, single group, open-label observational clinical study for 90 days among 18 human volunteers.

### 1.5 Potential Benefits

Fresh interpretation of available studies suggests JVN-8 can be:
- Cost-effective
- Safe
- Useful in a large number of conditions of clinical relevance

### 1.6 Future Research Needs

- In vitro and in vivo studies
- Robust clinical trials (randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multi-centre, crossover trials)
- Biomarker identification for geriatric problems
- Comparison with Metformin trial model (US$50 million to test health span extension)
