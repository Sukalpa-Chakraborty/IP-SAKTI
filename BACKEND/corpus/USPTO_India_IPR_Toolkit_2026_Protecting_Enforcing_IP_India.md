## 1. India’s IP Landscape

US rights do not extend to India; systems differ. Toolkit overview + tips + enforcement strategy.

**Bodies:**
- **DPIIT** (Ministry Commerce & Industry) – central IP policy, administers patents/copyrights/trademarks/designs/GIs/semiconductor layouts; oversees CGPDTM
- **CGPDTM / Indian IP Office** (HQ New Delhi, offices Delhi/Kolkata/Mumbai/Chennai/Ahmedabad) – registration/grant; incentives (NIPAM education); oversees: Patent Offices (Kolkata/Chennai/Delhi/Mumbai), Copyright Office Delhi, Trade Marks Registries (Mumbai/Kolkata/Chennai/Ahmedabad/Delhi), Designs Wing Kolkata, GI Registry Chennai, Semiconductor Registry Delhi, PIS + RGNIIPM Nagpur
- **CIPAM** – DPIIT professional body for National IPR Policy 2016 – promotion, awareness (youth/academia/SMEs/startups/enforcement/judiciary), streamlining
- **CBIC** (Dept Revenue, Finance) – border protection; record copyright/trademark/design/GI; no patents
- **Police** (central/state) – search/seize
- **District/High Courts** – adjudication; Commercial Courts (2015 Act) for IP; Supreme Court apex/final appeal

**General considerations:**
- Customize strategy (business/IP type/plans); long-term early; background checks; define IP use in licences/contracts; IP clauses in employment, educate team; enforcement-minded strategy + local attorney (filing/registration/monitoring/investigation/enforcement); use USPTO + South Asia Attaché New Delhi (on-ground help, providers, counterpart issues) + US Export Assistance Centers / Commercial Service (80+ countries incl India).

## 2. Trademarks

**Definition:** Identifier distinguishing goods/services; forms: heading/label/name/device/numeral/shape/packaging/color-combination. Act: Trade Marks Act 1999. Sec 2(1)(zb): mark graphically representable + distinguishing, incl shape/packaging/colors.

**Types:** Word, Device (label/sticker/monogram/logo/geometric), Service, Collective (association/institution/cooperative, regional characteristics), Certification (meets standards), Well-known (famous), Unconventional/non-traditional (color/sound/shape/smell/motion/packaging).

**Registrable:** Graphically representable + distinguishing + no earlier conflict. Sec 9 absolute grounds – even non-distinctive registrable if acquired distinctiveness/secondary meaning via prolonged exclusive use, or well-known (e.g. personal name with ≥5 yrs extensive exclusive recognition).

**Who:** Any “person” using/intending to use – natural, company, partnership, HUF, trust, society, govt, association, joint proprietor – Sec 18(1) proprietor claim. No domicile attorney required (unlike US) but recommended.

**Where:** Jurisdiction of principal business place; if outside India – agent/lawyer office jurisdiction.

**First-to-use:** Like US priority based on first use; unregistered prior use protected common law; unlike US, first use anywhere globally counts – transborder reputation if goodwill/reputation in India evidenced. Unlike US (use in interstate commerce needed), India allows “proposed to be used” registration without use.

**Steps:**
1. **Classes:** Nice Agreement classification.
2. **Search:** Advisable (no conflict/pending) to avoid issues.
3. **Filing:** Copy of mark/logo; applicant name/address/nationality; incorporation state; local address if non-domiciled; goods/services list + classes; first-use date in India if pre-filing use; POA if via agent. From US consider Madrid Protocol via WIPO (alternative to direct). Paper or online e-filing (ipindiaonline); online 10% cheaper (2017 Rules). ipindia.gov.in.
4. **Examination:** ~1+ year; accept absolutely/conditionally/refuse. Absolute → Trade Marks Journal (search.ipindia.gov.in/IPOJournal/Journal/Trademark). Conditional → examination report, 1 month to comply; if acceptable → publication; if not/hearing requested → Registrar hearing → if allowed → publication. Guides: ipindia filing-process, workflow.
5. **Publication:** If satisfies, Journal – notice for opposition; if none in 4 months → registration.
6. **Opposition:** Any person within 4 months of publication/re-publication; grounds stated + evidence: Sec 9 absolute (e.g. devoid distinctive, customary), Sec 11 relative (similar earlier mark + identical/similar goods/services), other (well-known, copyright, GI conflict). Registrar hearing decides; appeal to High Court (ecourts highcourts).
7. **Registration:** Certificate under seal; effective as of filing date; ® only after certificate. Term 10 yrs from filing (Sec 47(b) non-use cancellation – registration date = register entry date).
8. **Renewal:** Indefinite 10-yr renewals; apply ≤1 yr before expiry; 6-month grace with fee+surcharge. Default Sec 25(4): after 6 mo but within 1 yr – restore with fee + Form; Registrar republishes for objections; if none → re-enter for 10 yrs (2017 Rules).
9. **Priority:** Paris Convention (India+US) – home filing → same filing date in other member if within 6 months.

**Infringement Sec 29:** Unauthorized use of identical/deceptively similar registered mark re registered goods/services. Cases: confusion/incorrect association; use as trade name/business for registered goods/services; advertising creating unfair advantage/harming distinctiveness/reputation; applying to packaging/labelling/business paper/advertising without authorization with knowledge/ reason to know. Sec 102 falsification: unauthorized application/deceptively similar, or falsifying genuine (alter/add/efface); falsely applying to goods/package/services, or using package bearing identical/deceptively similar for packing other goods.

**Non-infringement Sec 30:** Honest industrial/commercial practices; no undue advantage/detriment; indicating kind/quality/quantity/purpose/value/origin/time/characteristics; outside registration scope/limitations; implied consent (continued beyond permitted use not removed); parts/accessories; exercising registered right to use identical/similar mark.

**Passing off:** Common-law tort for unregistered marks – prevents misrepresentation.

**Remedies:** Civil – permanent/interim injunctions, damages/account of profits, delivery/destruction, costs; interim – local commissioner (Anton Piller search/seize/preserve/inventory), restrain asset disposal harming recovery. Criminal – 6 mo–3 yrs + INR 50k–200k (~US$580–2324 as of 2025).

**Landmarks:** Yahoo! v Akash Arora 1999 – domain = trademark, cybersquatting; D.M. Entertainment v Baby Gift House 2010 – first celebrity merchandising/publicity; N.R. Dongre v Whirlpool 1996 – well-known + transborder reputation; Milmet Oftho v Allergan 2004 SC – foreign well-known protected even if unused/unregistered in India.

**Tips:** Register early (prima facie proprietorship; unregistered limited); intent-to-use allowed; search; Madrid since 2013 (guidelines link); well-known list (tmrsearch list as of 10.02.2025) – no clear formal procedure but Form TM-M + statement/evidence (other registrations, enforcement – court/Registrar declarations) – SOP link; avoid class headings (list goods/services, Nice but headings prohibited); multi-class allowed; licence recordal not mandatory (written agreement suffices, use accrues); assignment deed mention goodwill inclusion, consideration, effective date; attorney advisable though not required; registration not prerequisite for civil/criminal (combined infringement+passing off possible); 10-yr renewable; maintain use – cancel if 5 yrs continuous non-use (from register entry); Sec 115(4) – search/seizure by ≥DSP with prior Registrar favourable opinion; IPAB abolished 2021 – appeals to High Court.

## 3. Patents

**What:** Statutory limited right to prevent making/using/offer/sale/import without consent. Authority: Indian Patent Office, CGPDTM (Chennai/Mumbai/Kolkata/Delhi HQ). Like US utility – novel + inventive step + industrial application. Term 20 yrs from filing; no extension. PCT national phase – 20 yrs from international filing date (considered India filing if enters within 31 mo from priority).

**Ineligible Sec 3-4 (even if novel/inventive/industrial):** Frivolous/contrary natural laws; contrary public order/morality or prejudicial to life/health/environment; mere scientific principle/abstract theory/living/non-living natural discovery; mere new form of known substance without enhanced efficacy / new property/use of known substance / mere use of known process/machine unless new product or ≥1 new reactant (salts/esters/ethers/polymorphs/metabolites/pure form/particle size/isomers/mixtures/complexes/combinations/derivatives = same unless significantly differ in efficacy); mere admixture aggregating properties; mere arrangement/duplication independently functioning; agriculture/horticulture method; medicinal/surgical/curative/prophylactic/diagnostic/therapeutic treatment of humans/animals (disease-free/economic value); plants/animals (except microorganisms) incl seeds/varieties/species + essentially biological production; literary/dramatic/musical/artistic incl films/TV; maths/business method/computer program per se/algorithms; scheme/rule/mental act/game method; IC topography; traditional knowledge/aggregation/duplication of known traditional components; atomic energy.

**Eligible examples:** GM microorganisms/vaccines; new compound/formulation; synergistic admixture; improvement/combination new/better/cheaper result; surgical/therapeutic/diagnostic instrument; diagnostics on removed tissues/cells/fluids; computer-implemented with technical effect/contribution.

**How to obtain:**
- Territorial – need Indian patent; lengthy (years); non-resident must via registered Indian agent or India service address.
1. **Attorney:** Early advice (disclosure anywhere may kill patentability); PCT (US+India members) streamlines multi-country, more time; e.g. US resident files USPTO RO, 31 mo to India national phase; US attorney can recommend Indian agent (tech-familiar); non-residents must via registered agent.
2. **Priority:** Paris – first filing → subsequent same subject in member within 12 mo claims first date, avoids intervening-act rejection.
3. **Prepare/file:** English, complete spec (abstract/description/claims/drawings) clearly disclosing; physical or e-filing (ipronline); foreign applicants – office per India service address.
4. **Examination:** Filing check → substantive only if published + Form 18 request + fee within 31 mo from priority/filing (earlier) (for apps filed ≥15 Mar 2024); else deemed withdrawn. PCT national phase not processed before 31 mo unless express Form 18 request+fee. Expedited (Form 18A e-filing only) for ISA/IPEA India PCT, startups/small/female – grant/disposal ~1 yr. FER issued → comply within 6 mo (+3 mo extension); else abandoned (no appeal, but High Court may revive in extraordinary – agent negligence without applicant fault + positive intent). If in order + no pre-grant opposition pending → grant (date = Controller order).
5. **Review/appeal:** Controller rejection → review (restrictive) vs appeal to jurisdictional High Court (broader, incl pre-grant opposition orders); writ for natural-justice violations.

**Pre-grant opposition:** Any person (researchers/NGOs) on Form 7A+fee after publication but before grant, Sec 25(1) grounds + statement/evidence/hearing request. 2024 Rules – Controller decides prima facie maintainability within 1 mo with reasons; notifies applicant → 2 mo statement/evidence; then reject / require amendment then grant / refuse with speaking order. Opposed apps expedited examination. Misused to delay – courts condemned frivolous/serial as abuse.

**Invalidation:** Post-grant – any “person interested” (research same field) within 1 yr from grant publication; same grounds; Controller notifies patentee → Opposition Board recommendation → maintain/amend/revoke; appeal to High Court. Revocation petition to High Court anytime during subsistence (generally grant jurisdiction; 2022 Delhi – also where commercial interest affected). Also counterclaim in infringement suit; cannot pursue separate simultaneous actions. Revocation extra grounds vs opposition (indefinite/unsupported claims, fraud/misrepresentation, not entitled/no foreign filing permission). 

**Tips:**
- **Novelty:** Absolute novelty; pre-filing disclosures = prior art except grace: (i) public working in India ≤1 yr before priority for reasonable trial by/with applicant consent; (ii) public display ≤12 mo before application (from exhibition opening/paper reading/publication) – display/use with inventor consent at Gazette-notified exhibition + consequent publication/use during exhibition without consent, or paper before learned society/with consent in transactions. 2024 Rules – formal request+fee for display grace. No grace for other disclosure/commercial use – file priority first.
- **Trade secret:** Patent publication without grant or later invalidation → public → trade secret lost.
- **Foreign filing:** Resident must wait 6 weeks after India filing with no secrecy direction (or revoked) before filing abroad; or seek foreign filing licence (brief description); defence/atomic – need govt consent; residency (not invention origin) triggers (unlike US). Normally 21 days disposal (from govt consent if defence/atomic). Non-compliance – up to 2 yrs imprisonment + fine; India app abandoned, granted patent revocable.
- **Spec/disclosure:** Fully/particularly describe operation/use/method + best method known/entitled; claims single invention/single inventive concept, clear/succinct/fairly based.
- **Amendments:** By disclaimer/correction/explanation; no new matter beyond unamended; amended claims must fall wholly within unamended claim scope + directly derivable. PCT national phase entry – may delete claims without amendment application; no add/revise/modify. 2023 Delhi Single Judge – may amend non-patentable→patentable if basis in original spec; consider whole spec not just textual claims. No double-patenting provision but two patents for one invention barred. Markush/selection (genus/species) allowed but selection must show substantial/unexpected advantage vs non-selected. Patents of addition – improvement/modification of main (applied/obtained), not before main, expires with main. No continuations; divisionals restrictive – anytime before grant to remedy Controller objection or multi-invention claims; no advance grant notice may close window; must be patentably distinct; Controller may require amendment to avoid overlap. Practice – must be based on parent claims not mere disclosure; addition beyond pending parent scope disallowed; cancelled parent claims considered disclaimed – file early before cancellation/grant. 2023 Division Bench relaxed – maintainable if plurality reflected in provisional/complete disclosure (not only claims); 2024 Rules aligned.
- **Sec 3(d) efficacy (chem/pharma):** New form of known efficacy → enhanced efficacy + novelty/inventive/industrial. *Novartis v UoI* (2013) 6 SCC 1: curative claim → therapeutic efficacy; only efficacy-related properties count (not flow/stability/hygroscopicity); does not bar all incremental innovation.
- **Computer:** Programs per se Sec 3(k) barred, but technical effect/contribution patentable.
- **Compulsory licence:** After 3 yrs from grant, interested person to Controller – reasonable public requirements unsatisfied, not affordable, not worked in India (working statements). Special – national emergency/extreme urgency/public non-commercial on govt notification; pharma export in exceptional cases.
- **Working Form 27:** Every patentee/licensee (exclusive or not) – once per 3 fiscal years (Apr-Mar) starting FY after grant FY; due within 6 mo after every 3rd FY (e.g. grant 2023-24 → period from 2024-25, window 1 Apr–30 Sep 2027); extendable to 9 mo total; failure → penalty + compulsory licence basis; false → penalty.
- **Sec 8 foreign activity:** Form 3 particulars (country/date/number/status/publication/grant) at India filing or within 6 mo; update in writing other same/substantially same foreign filings within 3 mo from FER; updated Form 3 within 2 mo if Controller directs (recorded reasons). Controller may use databases. Delay/extension up to 3 mo on request. Non-compliance = pre/post opposition + revocation grounds, but jurisprudence limits revocation to material non-disclosure with deliberate/wilful suppression. Can use WIPO CASE/DAS; Manual requires Examiner/Controller to use CASE (search/exam reports incl India/US).
- **Enforcement:** Customs does not record patents (like US CBP); border enforcement only via court injunction vs import.

## 4. Copyrights

**What:** Original works of authorship fixed tangible – literary (incl programs/compilations/databases), dramatic, musical, AV, artistic, sound recordings. Original = originates from author, not copied. India – Berne/TRIPS/WCT/WPPT – US works protected.

**Protected (Copyright Act 1957):** Literary (novels/poems/databases/programs), Dramatic (plays/choreo), Musical (sheet music/compositions – not lyrics – lyrics = literary, unlike US), Artistic (paintings/sculptures/photos), Film (videos/TV/movies/DVDs), Sound recordings (CDs/LPs/downloads/streams). Fixation limits: unlike US (video/phonorecord fix choreo/musical), India excludes phonorecords for musical, films for choreo; musical written, lyrics separate literary, choreo written notation.

**Moral (author’s special):** Paternity + integrity (restrain/claim damages for distortion/mutilation prejudicial to honour/reputation); independent, inalienable, perpetual.

**Unprotected:** Ideas only (not expression) – ideas/themes/plots/facts/procedures/methods/maths; e.g. business-model description protectable, model itself not.

**Registration:** Not prerequisite; voluntary via Registrar (New Delhi, under CGPDTM/DPIIT); only prima facie proof (rebuttable presumption). If registered, subsequent transfers/agreements (e.g. software licences) must also be registered. Musical written, choreo notation.

**Designs:** Designs Act – shape/config/pattern/ornament/line-color composition applied industrially, judged by eye; excludes Copyright Act artistic works – no dual protection. Copyright ceases for registrable-but-unregistered design if >50 industrially-produced copies by owner/licensee – may lose protection. Supreme Court two-prong test – purely artistic (Copyright) vs design lacking functional utility (Designs) – case-specific, need counsel.

**Economic rights:** Literary/dramatic/musical – reproduce (incl electronic storage), issue unpublished copies, perform/communicate/make available/broadcast, make film/recording, translate, adapt/derivative + same on translation/adaptation; Programs + sale/commercial rental; Artistic – reproduce (incl electronic, 2D↔3D), communicate/make available/broadcast, issue unpublished copies, include in film, adapt + same on adaptation; Film – copy (incl photo/storage), sell/commercial rent/offer, communicate/make available/broadcast; Sound recording – make other recordings embodying (incl storage), sell/rent/offer, communicate/make available/broadcast. Once-sold = in circulation. Plus broadcast reproduction (broadcasters), performer’s, resale share (painting/sculpture/drawing original, literary/dramatic/musical manuscript).

**Term (generally < US):** Published literary/dramatic/musical/artistic – life+60 yrs from next calendar year after death; anonymous/pseudonymous (non-photo) – 60 yrs from next year after publication; posthumous/engraving – 60 yrs after publication; film/sound – 60 yrs after publication; govt/public undertaking/international org – 60 yrs after publication; broadcaster – 25 yrs after broadcast; performer – 50 yrs after performance.

**Inalienable royalties:** Literary/musical in film – royalties for any exploitation except theatre exhibition, shared equally with film owner; literary/musical in non-film sound recording – royalties shared equally with recording owner; assignable only to heirs or copyright society.

**Assignments/licences:** Written, signed by assignor/agent; identify work, rights, duration/territory, royalty/consideration; if unspecified – 5 yrs, India only; if assignee non-use within 1 yr – lapse. Licence – must be written but need not be signed.

**Statutory/compulsory:** Withheld works, sound recordings of musical works, broadcasting literary/musical/recordings.

**CMOs (copyright societies):** IPRS (musical/lyrics), RMPL (sound recordings), Cinefil Producers (films), SRAI/Screenwriters (dramatic + associated literary), IRRO (reprographic literary). Awaiting (Mar 2025): ISAMRA (singers+musicians, ex-ISRA vocalists only, applied Sep 2023 as Performers’ Society) for performer rights, PPL (public performance/broadcast – Sony/Warner/Universal). Only registered societies may licence/collect for literary/dramatic/musical/artistic in recordings/films (though some publishers direct-license). US users – ensure US CMO agreements with IPRS (performance/mechanical), ISAMRA (performers), PPL/RMPL (producers if not direct).

**International:** Berne, UCC, Geneva Phonograms, Multilateral Double-Taxation Royalties, TRIPS, WPPT, WCT. Three Berne principles: national treatment, automatic (no formalities), independence. Foreign (Berne/UCC) protected via Copyright Act + International Copyright Order 1999; automatic even if created outside India if author from member. Shorter-term rule applied – not exceed origin term unless India law provides. Sound recordings – Berne N/A, but TRIPS/WPPT national treatment (except equitable remuneration for broadcast/communication) – India protects foreign (incl US) public performance/broadcast. SoundExchange–PPL India agreement for digital/broadcast/communication/public performance of US recordings; performers same right – ISAMRA + IMI labels agreement – ISAMRA % from PPL collections; ISAMRA–SoundExchange reciprocal for Indian performers in US.

**Enforcement:** Civil + criminal + border; foreign (Berne) can sue in India. Civil – injunction, damages, accounts, delivery/destruction; District/High Court with jurisdiction; Commercial Courts/Divisions (2015 Act) expedited; Delhi dynamic injunction (2019 batch piracy – extend via Joint Registrar to mirror/redirect/alphanumeric variants without new suit; 2023 dynamic+ for future works; also for well-known trademarks; 2025 superlative – real-time irrespective of mode). Criminal – 6 mo–3 yrs + INR 50k–200k (US$580–2330); seizure/delivery; enhanced on subsequent convictions. Offences: knowing infringement/abetment, infringing program use, plates, TPM circumvention with intent, RMI removal/alteration/distribution/import/broadcast/communication knowing removal, false Register entries. Unlike US – direct police complaint or Magistrate complaint directing investigation; seek counsel; state IP units e.g. MIPCU. Customs – record online (ipr.icegate.gov.in) under IPR (Imported Goods) Enforcement Rules 2007 (Customs Act 1962) to block import/export pirated; suo moto suspension on prima facie; detention as prohibited goods on owner notice+evidence; release if no court disposal order within 14 days.

**Safe harbor (IT Act 2000):** Intermediary exempt for third-party info if conditions; copyright complaint to Grievance Officer – acknowledge 24h, resolve 7 days. Actual knowledge (court order or reasoned written govt intimation specifying basis/provision/nature/URL) → remove/disable within 3h. Copyright Act exempt for transient/incidental storage for links/access/integration unless prohibited by owner and responsible person knows/has reason infringing; on written complaint – refrain 21 days or until court order; else may restore (Copyright Rules 2013 procedure).

**Tips – Courts:** High Court – rectification/appeals vs Registrar (correction/expungement/error; appeal within 3 mo); Commercial Courts – assignment, compulsory/statutory licences, tariff schemes, etc. **Infringement:** Unlicensed/contra-licence exercise of exclusive rights; also import of infringing copies. **Fair use:** No codified fair use – exhaustive Sec 52 non-infringing incl fair dealing. **Copyright+trademark:** Logo dual – copyright as prior right to oppose/cancel bad-faith trademarks, takedown on e-commerce; US note – logo trademark registration needed for enforcement, copyright automatic. **Authorship:** Author = first owner except: employment contract of service/apprenticeship (employer owns unless contrary); commissioned – creator owns (need written assignment) except photo/painting/portrait/engraving/film commissioned for payment – commissioner owns unless written otherwise; independent contractor – contractor owns, need assignment. Consult counsel. **Joint:** Cannot licence/grant to third party without co-owner consent (unlike US – each may exploit accounting profits).

## 5. Trade Secrets / Unfair Competition

No specific statute (unlike US); protected via contract/equity/common-law breach of confidence. National IPR Policy 2016 recognized importance but no standalone law. Enforcement civil + criminal only (no admin). US concern – uncertainty, wants standalone law.

**What (TRIPS Art 39):** Undisclosed info protected vs disclosure/acquisition/use contrary to honest practices if: secret (not generally known/readily accessible to usual circles), commercial value from secrecy, reasonable secrecy steps. Courts: formula/process/device/business info kept confidential for advantage; confidential info = formula/pattern/compilation/program/device/method/technique/process deriving independent economic value from secrecy + reasonable efforts. Factors: extent known outside/inside (employees), precautions, savings/value vs competitors, effort/money to develop, time/expense to duplicate. Three elements: confidential nature, disclosed under confidence (contractual or not), actual/threatened unauthorized use.

**Enforcing:** No admin; Criminal – no specific misappropriation offence – use breach of trust/theft/cheating + computer-resource provisions per facts; Civil – no specialist courts, CPC 1908; may be commercial disputes (Commercial Courts Act 2015) though “trade secret/confidential” not named as IP, but Delhi/Madras/Calcutta/Himachal IPR Division Rules 2022-23 expressly include. Remedies – preliminary/final injunction, damages/accounts (liquidated/actual/punitive/aggravated – aggravated for mala fide/wanton, deterrence), costs/fees discretionary, return orders, commissioners for search/seizure.

**Issues:** Need proper documentation/evidence; specify secret sought to restrain; discovery/interrogatories/interim/commissioner available; confidentiality during litigation – sealed covers, counsel-only disclosure, confidentiality clubs (external counsel/experts/representatives), in camera review; Commercial Courts strict timelines/penalties → expeditious if followed. Contract Act 1872 – restraints of trade void – cannot restrain ex-employee joining competitor/starting business, but can prohibit disclosure if clearly in employment contract. Inevitable disclosure doctrine not recognized; general knowledge/routine affairs ≠ secret; independent fair discovery not misappropriation; rare protection without contract under confidence duty. No special trial – like other IP; ADR available – arbitration clause → interim under Arbitration & Conciliation Act 1996.

**Tips (business/contractual):** Limit access, physical (locks/server rooms) + technical (MFA/MDM/DLP); identify/categorize prioritize valuable; HR trust; background checks + NDAs (non-disclosure/non-use); need-to-know; written policies + training; track flows/transfers/devices; select/monitor manufacturers/partners – due diligence (trade.gov/india, FCS), brand/reputation/IP experience, multi-personnel relationships, secure IP before exploring, compartmentalize production/design/packaging/equipment, keep vital/latest tech in US, classify/mark sensitivity/access levels, overt/covert anti-copy (chemicals/films/foils/codes/holograms/inks/labels/papers/odors), DRM + anti-reverse-engineering in object code, NDAs pre-negotiation with secrecy/non-use/audit/post-relationship, governed within Indian law.

## 6. Geographical Indications

**What (TRIPS Art 22(1)):** Indication identifying good as originating in member territory/region/locality where quality/reputation/characteristics essentially attributable to origin. Art 1 – members determine implementation method, no more extensive than TRIPS required if not contravening.

**India – sui generis (vs US trademark-based certification/collective):** GI Act 1999 + GI Rules 2002 (amendment simplifying in process).

**Benefits:** Consumers – quality/distinctiveness assurance, source identification; Holders – positive association/reputation, marketing/premium for authentic.

**Sec 2(e):** GI = indication identifying agricultural/natural/manufactured goods originating/manufactured in country/region/locality where quality/reputation/characteristics essentially attributable to origin, and for manufactured – ≥1 of production/processing/preparation there. **Sec 8:** Registrable re any/all classes as Registrar classifies (international classification). **Sec 9 ineligible:** Likely deceive/confuse; contrary to law; scandalous/obscene; offending religious susceptibilities; otherwise disentitled in court; generic/common (ceased protected/disused in origin)*; literally true but falsely represents other origin. *Generic = originally geographic but become common kind/nature/type – consider origin + consumption area.

**Additional:** Manufactured – production/processing/preparation in origin; Handicraft – human factors essential Sec 11(2)(a); written statement linking uniqueness to geo/environment/nature/human factors.

**Who:** Any association/producers/organization/authority representing producers’ interest. Authorized user Sec 17 – producer of registered GI applies to record as authorized owner. No assignment/licensing Sec 24 – cannot assign/transmit/licence/pledge/mortgage/agree. Office – Chennai (CGPDTM supervised).

**Steps (multi-class, fee per class):**
1. File – determine GI definition fit, represent interest, India service address, affidavit of representation, signed by applicant/agent + statement of case (relevant info, special characteristics/quality maintenance), 3 copies + 3 certified region maps + inspection structure if any + all applicant addresses if association.
2. Scrutiny/exam – examiner deficiencies → respond 1 mo; consultative group studies statement → report.
3. Show-cause – Registrar objections → respond 2 mo or hearing.
4. Publication – if accepted, within 3 mo in GI Journal (search.ipindia.gov.in/IPOJournal/Journal/GIR).
5. Opposition – any person within 3 mo (+1 mo extension if requested before expiry); Registrar serves applicant → 2 mo counter (else abandoned) → serve opponent → affidavits/evidence → hearing.
6. Registration/renewal – accepted → registered from filing date; 10 yrs renewable 10-yrly (fee); if not renewed – removed; no assignment etc.
7. Appeal – aggrieved to High Court within 3 mo.

**Protection:** Registered – owner/authorized users can sue infringement; suits (infringement registered or passing off unregistered) in District Court with jurisdiction (not below). Unregistered Sec 20(1) – no proceeding to prevent/recover damages for infringement.

## 7. IP Enforcement

Judicial (courts civil/criminal + police) + admin (Customs cross-border). Concerns: inconsistent decisions, police/Customs unfamiliarity, no central coordinator. Progress: IPDs Delhi/Madras/Calcutta/Himachal, state units (MIPCU etc.). Check for new IPDs.

**Civil:** Greater for patents/designs (no criminal). Three-tier – Supreme (Delhi apex/final), High Courts (state highest, superintendence, writ vs public authorities incl CGPDTM/Patent/Copyright/Trademark for natural-justice/constitutional violations), District (generally unlimited pecuniary civil). Five High Courts (Delhi/Bombay/Calcutta/Madras/Himachal) have original first-instance for IP above threshold (e.g. Delhi >US$254k). Till Apr 2021 IPAB (2003) heard trademark/patent/copyright appeals; Tribunals Reforms Ordinance 4 Apr 2021 abolished – to High Courts (patents/trademarks/GIs/plant varieties) and Commercial Courts/High Courts (copyright). No specialized courts but Commercial Courts Act 2015 – district Commercial Courts + High Court Commercial Divisions fast-track commercial (incl IP – registered/unregistered trademarks/copyrights/patents/designs/domains/GIs/semiconductors) disputes ≥INR 300k (~US$3510 2025); amended CPC to reduce time; summary judgments. Delhi IPD Jul 2021 (post-IPAB) – all IPR, IPD benches per roster (recently 2 exclusive judges) + IPR Appellate Division; Rules Feb 2022 (practice/procedure original/appellate/misc) – broad “IPR subject matter” (patents/copyrights/trademarks/GIs/plant varieties/designs/semiconductors/traditional knowledge/trade secrets/confidential/common-law related). Madras Apr 2023 (Rules 2022) second, Calcutta Sep 2024 (Rules 2023) third, Himachal Oct 2024 (Rules 2022) fourth; Bombay/Gujarat nomenclature for IPR.

**Dynamic injunctions Delhi:** After blocking order, fast block subsequent content without new suits – submit URLs → ISP block administratively. 2019 batch piracy – Joint Registrar extends granted rogue-site injunction to mirror/redirect/alphanumeric variants providing same access (application + affidavit/evidence). 2023 dynamic+ – future works of holders vs recurring sites; also for well-known trademarks; 2025 superlative – extended dynamic+ real-time irrespective of mode (copyright + broadcast reproduction suit).

**Civil pros/cons:** Pros – compensatory damages, specialized commercial benches, injunctions, Anton Piller fear (with uniformed police), appeal, easy settlement exit. Cons – low/insufficient deterrent damages, rare punitive, collection difficulties, delays.

**Criminal:** Via IP laws + Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023 (new code 2024, replaced IPC 1860 – counterfeiting/cheating etc.) + IT Act 2000; procedure Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita 2023. Complaint to police or Magistrate. Trademark/Copyright/GI – 6 mo–3 yrs + INR 50k–200k (~US$585–2340 2025); cognizable – FIR mandatory, investigation/arrest without warrant. Pros – deterrence, cheaper than civil, targets large-scale/persistent infringers. Cons – no injunction/damages, low priority, lengthy trials, low convictions, high evidence, lack resources/expertise, police liaison needed, corruption/influence risk, hard exit (state case).

**Customs (Customs Act 1962 + IPR (Imported Goods) Enforcement Rules 2007):** Prohibit import/export infringing; import prohibition covers trademarks/copyrights/designs/GIs only (no patents). Holder must record each right separately via notice + ownership proof; single application covers multiple ports; online (ipr.icegate.gov.in). Conditions – bond (protect importer/consignee/owner/authorities, bear destruction/demurrage/detention) + indemnity (liabilities/expenses for suspension) with surety/security as Customs deems; inform of amendment/cancellation/revocation/suspension. Two bonding: Consignment-specific (110% detained value + 25% security – low volume/granular) vs Centralized single running (covers all ports, debited/credited – high volume/convenient). Recordation 5 yrs or IP expiry (earlier). Suo moto suspension on prima facie/reasonable belief even if unrecorded; holder must join within time else release; if infringement found – confiscate/destroy/dispose + penalties. Parallel/grey-market generally not prohibited except certain cases. Pros – stops at border, prohibits import/export, deters parallel in cases; designs recordable (unlike US) + registered copyrights/trademarks. Cons – training needed, bond/costs expensive, transit excluded (only India sale/use imports), not root cause. Nov 2023 CGPDTM–NCTC (Customs risk management) working group (5 NCTC + 3 CGPDTM, reports to CGPDTM director case-wise) vs counterfeits/piracy.

**E-commerce notice-takedown:** Varies per platform; generally need Indian registration certificates; no national e-commerce policy. IT Act 2000 + Intermediary Guidelines/Digital Media Ethics Code Rules 2021 (amended) govern safe harbor. Marketplace facilitator between identifiable buyers/sellers = intermediary. Evolving jurisprudence. Safe harbor if: limited to access/transmission/temporary storage/hosting; does not initiate/select receiver/select/modify; observes due diligence + guidelines. Loses if: conspires/abets/aids/induces unlawful act, or fails to expeditiously remove/disable on knowledge/govt notification. Grievance Officer – acknowledge 24h, dispose 7 days. Actual knowledge (court order or reasoned written authorized govt intimation specifying basis/provision/nature/URL) → remove/disable within 3h. Must inform users of rules/privacy/agreement + reasonable efforts to prevent illegal hosting/display/upload/modify/publish/transmit/store/share; significant social media (>5M Indian users) must deploy tech incl automated tools to proactively identify identical previously removed/disabled per court/govt order. Discuss regulations before enforcing.
