# WIPO Treaty on Intellectual Property, Genetic Resources and Associated Traditional Knowledge – GRATK/DC/7

**Source PDF:** `gratk_dc_7.pdf`
**Original:** English, Date May 24 2024
**Event:** Diplomatic Conference to Conclude International Legal Instrument Relating to IP, Genetic Resources and Traditional Knowledge Associated with Genetic Resources, Geneva May 13-24 2024
**Status:** Adopted by Diplomatic Conference

## Preamble

Parties desiring promotion of efficacy, transparency, quality of patent system re genetic resources (GR) and traditional knowledge (TK) associated with GR; emphasizing patent offices need appropriate information to prevent erroneous grants for non-novel/non-inventive inventions; recognizing patent system's role in protection; disclosure requirement contributes to legal certainty/consistency benefiting system, providers, users; mutual supportiveness with other instruments; IP role in innovation/transfer/dissemination/economic development; acknowledging UNDRIP and including Indigenous Peoples and local communities.

## Article 1 – Objectives

(a) enhance efficacy, transparency, quality of patent system re GR and TK associated with GR
(b) prevent erroneous patent grants for non-novel/non-inventive GR/TK inventions

## Article 2 – List of Terms

- **Applicant:** person records of Office show as applying/filing/prosecuting per applicable law
- **Application:** patent grant application
- **Contracting Party:** State or IGO party
- **Country of origin of GR:** country possessing GR in in-situ conditions
- **Based on:** GR and/or TK must have been necessary for claimed invention, invention depends on specific properties of GR and/or TK
- **Genetic material:** any plant, animal, microbial or other origin material containing functional units of heredity
- **Genetic resources:** genetic material of actual or potential value (footnote: per CBD, not intended to include human genetic resources)
- **In situ conditions:** where GR exist within ecosystems/natural habitats, and for domesticated/cultivated species surroundings where distinctive properties developed
- **Office:** authority entrusted with patent granting
- **PCT:** Patent Cooperation Treaty 1970
- **Source of GR:** any source applicant obtained GR – research center, gene bank, Indigenous Peoples/local communities, Multilateral System of ITPGRFA, other ex-situ collection/depository
- **Source of TK associated with GR:** any source obtained – scientific literature, public databases, patent applications/publications

## Article 3 – Disclosure Requirement

3.1 Where claimed invention based on GR, require disclosure of:
- (a) country of origin, or
- (b) if unknown or (a) inapplicable, source of GR

Agreed Statement: if >1 country of origin, disclose country from which actually obtained.

3.2 Where based on TK associated with GR, require disclosure of:
- (a) Indigenous Peoples or local community, as applicable, who provided TK, or
- (b) if unknown or inapplicable, source of TK

Agreed Statement: "as applicable" in 3.2(a) not flexibility to avoid requiring disclosure; implemented without effect on scope.

3.3 If none of 3.1/3.2 info known, require declaration to that effect affirming true/correct to best knowledge.

3.4 Provide guidance on meeting requirement + opportunity to rectify failure to include minimum info or correct erroneous disclosures.

3.5 No obligation on Offices to verify authenticity.

3.6 Make disclosed info available per patent procedures, without prejudice to confidential info protection.

## Article 4 – Non-Retroactivity

No obligations for applications filed prior to entry into force for that Party, without prejudice to existing national disclosure laws applying to such applications.

## Article 5 – Sanctions and Remedies

5.1 Appropriate, effective, proportionate legal/administrative/policy measures for failure to provide Art 3 info.
5.2 Opportunity to rectify before sanctions/remedies, subject to 5.2(bis).
5.2(bis) May exclude rectification opportunity where fraudulent conduct/intent per national law.
5.3 No revocation/invalidation/unenforceability solely on basis of failure to disclose Art 3 info, subject to 5.4.
5.4 May provide post-grant sanctions/remedies where fraudulent intent re Art 3 disclosure per national law.

## Article 6 – Information Systems

6.1 May establish databases of GR/TK, in consultation where applicable with Indigenous Peoples/local communities, stakeholders, considering national circumstances.
6.2 Should with safeguards (consultation) make systems accessible to Offices for search/examination; access may need authorization.
6.3 Assembly may establish technical working groups on systems, accessibility, safeguards.

## Article 7 – Relationship with Other International Agreements

Implemented mutually supportive with other relevant agreements.

Agreed Statement: Request PCT Union Assembly to consider amendments to PCT Regulations/Administrative Instructions to allow PCT applicants designating State requiring GR/TK disclosure to comply via formality either upon international filing (effect for all such States) or subsequently at national phase.
Footnote: Nothing derogates/modifies other international agreement.

## Article 8 – Review

Review scope/contents 4 years after entry into force – e.g. possible extension to other IP areas and derivatives, new/emerging technologies.

## Article 9 – General Principles on Implementation

9.1 Adopt necessary measures.
9.2 May determine appropriate method within own legal systems/practices.

## Article 10 – Assembly

10.1 Contracting Parties have Assembly: one delegate (+ alternates/advisors/experts) each; expenses borne by Party; WIPO IB may assist developing/transition countries; encourage effective participation of Indigenous Peoples/local communities as accredited observers; invite financial arrangements.

10.2 Functions:
- (a) maintenance/development/application/operation
- (b) admission of certain IGOs per Art 12.2
- (c) Art 8 review
- (d) decide Diplomatic Conference for revision per Art 14, instruct DG
- (e) establish technical WGs
- (f) adopt amendments to present Article (excl 10.1, 10.2(c)(d)(f), 10.3) and Art 11
- (g) other implementation functions including cooperation, technical assistance/capacity building via IB

10.3 Endeavour consensus; if vote: each State 1 vote; IGO votes equal to its Member States party, no double voting.
10.4 Meets on DG convocation, normally same period/place as WIPO General Assembly.
10.5 Own rules – extraordinary sessions, quorum, majorities.

## Article 11 – International Bureau

11.1 WIPO IB admin tasks, prepare meetings, secretariat for Assembly/WGs.
11.2 DG/staff participate without vote; DG/staff ex officio Secretary.
11.3 IB prepares Diplomatic Conferences per Assembly directions; DG/persons participate without vote.

## Article 12 – Eligibility

12.1 Any WIPO Member State.
12.2 Assembly may admit IGO competent with own legislation binding all Members on Treaty matters, duly authorized.
12.3 EU may sign/ratify/accede, making Art 12.2 declaration at that time.

## Article 13 – Ratification and Accession

Deposit ratification (if signed) or accession (if not) with DG; effective date = deposit date.

## Article 14 – Revision

Only by Diplomatic Conference per Vienna Convention; convocation decided by Assembly.

## Article 15 – Amendment of Articles 10 and 11

15.1 May be amended by Assembly per 10.2(f).
15.2 Proposals by Party or DG, communicated by DG ≥6 months before Assembly consideration.
15.3 Adoption requires 3/4 votes cast.
15.4 Entry into force 1 month after DG receives acceptances (per constitutional processes) from 3/4 Parties at adoption time; binds all then or later Parties.

## Article 16 – Signature

Open at Geneva Diplomatic Conference and thereafter at WIPO HQ for 1 year after adoption for eligible parties.

## Article 17 – Entry into Force

3 months after 15 eligible Art 12 parties deposit ratification/accession.

## Article 18 – Effective Date to Become Party

- (a) 15 Art 17 parties from entry into force date
- (b) others 3 months after deposit

## Article 19 – Denunciation

By notification to DG; takes effect 1 year after receipt; not affecting pending applications / in-force patents for denouncing Party at effective time.

## Article 20 – Reservations

No reservations permitted.

## Article 21 – Languages

21.1 Single original Arabic, Chinese, English, French, Russian, Spanish – equally authentic.
21.2 Official texts in other Assembly-designated languages established by DG after consultation with interested Parties (official language concerned).

## Article 22 – Depositary

DG of WIPO.
