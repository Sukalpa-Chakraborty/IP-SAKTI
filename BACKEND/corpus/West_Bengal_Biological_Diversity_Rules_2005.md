# West Bengal Biological Diversity Rules, 2005

## 1. Short Title

These rules may be called the **West Bengal Biological Diversity Rules, 2005**.

## 2. Definitions

In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:

- **"Act"** means the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 (18 of 2003).
- **"Biodiversity Management Committee"** means the Biodiversity Management Committee constituted by a local body under sub-section (1) of section 41.
- **"bio-survey and bio-utilisation"** shall have the same meaning as in clause (d) of section 2.
- **"Board"** means the West Bengal Biodiversity Board established under sub-section (1) of section 22.
- **"Chairperson"** means the Chairperson of the Board.
- **"Form"** means a form annexed to these rules.
- **"member"** means a member of the Board and includes the Chairperson.
- **"rule"** means a rule of these rules.
- **"Secretary"** means the Secretary to the Board appointed under rule 11.
- **"section"** means a section of the Act.
- **"State Government"** means the Government of West Bengal in the Department of Environment.
- **"sub-rule"** means a sub-rule of the rule in which the word occurs.

Words and expressions used but not defined in these rules and defined in the Act shall have the meanings respectively assigned to them in the Act.

## 3. Manner of Selection and Appointment of Chairperson

- The State Government may appoint the Chairperson on deputation from amongst officers not below the rank of a Secretary to the Government of West Bengal or by selection (direct recruitment).
- Provided that the State Government may, if it considers necessary, appoint the Chairperson on part-time basis by selection (direct recruitment).
- The selection of the Chairperson, either for part-time or full-time basis, shall be done by a selection committee constituted by the State Government.

### Selection Committee Composition

- Chief Secretary – Chairman
- Secretary, Department of Environment – Member
- Secretary, Department of Finance – Member
- One expert on conservation of Biodiversity / Natural resources – Member
- One person nominated by the State Government – Member

The Committee shall select the Chairperson from a panel of eminent persons having adequate knowledge and experience in conservation and sustainable use of biodiversity as well as equitable sharing of benefits, prepared by the State Government.

## 4. Term of Office of Chairperson

- The Chairperson shall hold office for a term of **three years** from the date he assumes office or until he attains the age of **sixty-five years**, whichever is earlier.
- Shall be eligible for re-appointment.
- Provided that a government officer holding the post of Chairperson may, on attaining the age of superannuation, be re-appointed to the post till he attains the age of 65 years.

## 5. Pay and Allowances of Chairperson

- The Chairperson shall be entitled to such remuneration as may be fixed by the State Government.
- When an officer of the State Government is appointed to the post of Chairperson, he shall be entitled to the grade pay for the post till he attains the age of superannuation.
- The Chairperson shall be entitled to such benefits as leave, house rent, provident fund, pension and such other benefits as may be determined by the State Government.
- A Chairperson appointed on part-time basis shall be entitled to travelling allowance, daily allowance and such other allowances as a Secretary to the Government of West Bengal is entitled to for performing any official duty.

## 6. Term of Office of Members

- Every member, other than the Chairperson and an ex officio member, shall hold office for a term of **three years** from the date he assumes office.
- Shall be eligible for re-appointment, till he attains the age of **sixty-five years**.

## 7. Pay and Allowances of Members

- Every member, other than the Chairperson and ex officio member, shall be entitled to such travelling allowance, daily allowance and such other allowances to which a State Government officer drawing a pay of **Rs. 16,400/- p.m. or above** is entitled, for attending a meeting of the Board.

## 8. Filling Up of Casual Vacancy

- If the office of any member becomes vacant, the vacancy shall be filled up by nomination by the State Government.
- The person so nominated shall hold office as a member for the **remaining term** of the member in whose place he is so nominated.

## 9. Resignation of Member

- A member may resign by a letter under his hand addressed to the State Government.
- Such resignation shall take effect from the date on which it is accepted by the State Government or on expiry of **thirty days** from the date of tendering the resignation, whichever is earlier.

## 10. Removal of Member

- A member may be removed from his office only after a due and proper enquiry held by an officer, not below the rank of Secretary to the Government of West Bengal, appointed by the State Government.
- After giving the member a reasonable opportunity of being heard.

## 11. Secretary to Board

- The Board shall appoint the Secretary on deputation from amongst officers, not below the rank of an Assistant Secretary to the Government of West Bengal, having experience in day-to-day office administration including accounting matters.
- The terms and conditions of service of the Secretary shall be such as may be specified by the Board by regulations made under sub-section (2) of section 14 read with section 25.
- The Secretary shall be responsible for:
  - (a) the day-to-day administration of the Board;
  - (b) coordinating and convening the meeting of the Board;
  - (c) maintaining the records of the proceedings and the accounts of the Board;
  - (d) such other matters as may be assigned or delegated to him, from time to time, by the Board.

## 12. Meetings of Board

- The Board shall meet at least **four times a year** at its headquarters or at such places as may be decided by the Chairperson.
- The Chairperson shall, upon a written request from not less than five members of the Board, call a special meeting.
- Members shall be given at least **seven days' notice** for an ordinary meeting and at least **three days' notice** for a special meeting, specifying the purpose, time and place.
- The Chairperson may, in consultation with the members, invite any person working in conservation of biological diversity or any related field.
- In every meeting the Chairperson shall preside over, and in his absence, by a member elected for the purpose by the members present from amongst themselves.
- Decisions shall be taken by a **simple majority** of members present and voting. In the event of equality of votes, the Chairperson or the member presiding shall have a second or casting vote.
- Each member shall have **one vote**.
- The quorum at every meeting shall be **five**.
- No member shall bring forward any matter of which he has not given at least seven days' notice, unless the Chairperson permits.
- Notice of the meeting may be given by messenger, registered post, or in such other manner as the Secretary may think fit.

## 13. General Functions of Board

### Duty

It shall be the duty of the Board to advise the State Government on all matters relating to the conservation of biological diversity, sustainable use of its components and equitable sharing of the benefits arising out of the utilisation of biological resources.

### Functions

The functions of the Board shall be:

- (a) to lay down the procedure governing the activities specified in section 23;
- (b) to regulate commercial utilisation and bio-survey and bio-utilisation of any biological resources in the State;
- (c) to coordinate the activities of the Biodiversity Management Committees;
- (d) to provide technical assistance and guidance to the Biodiversity Management Committees;
- (e) to give directions to the Biodiversity Management Committees for effective implementation of the Act;
- (f) to commission studies and sponsor investigations and research concerning conservation and sustainable use of biological diversity, bio-survey and bio-utilisation and other relevant subjects;
- (g) to engage consultants, each for a specific period not exceeding three years, for providing technical assistance to the Board in effective discharge of its functions:
  - Provided that if it is necessary to engage a consultant beyond three years, the Board shall seek prior approval of the State Government.
- (h) to collect, compile and publish technical and statistical data, manuals, codes and guidelines relating to conservation of biological diversity, sustainable use of its components and fair and equitable sharing of benefits;
- (i) to organise through mass media programmes regarding conservation of biological diversity, sustainable use of its components and fair and equitable sharing of benefits and to plan and organise training of personnel;
- (j) to prepare the annual budget of the Board;
- (k) to create the posts of officers and other employees of the Board, with prior approval of the State Government;
- (l) to build up data base and to create information and documentation system for biological resources and associated traditional knowledge through biological diversity registers and electronic media;
- (m) to report to the State Government about the functioning of the Board and the implementation of the Act;
- (n) to sanction grants for specific purposes to the Biodiversity Management Committees;
- (o) to undertake physical inspection of any area in connection with the implementation of the Act;
- (p) to assist the National Biodiversity Authority in opposing grant, outside India, of any intellectual property right on any biological resources or associated knowledge obtained from the State in an unlawful manner;
- (q) to do such other functions as may be assigned or directed by the State Government.

## 14. Powers and Duties of Chairperson

- The Chairperson shall have the overall control over the day-to-day activities of the Board.
- Shall have the power of general superintendence over the officers and other employees of the Board and to issue directions for the conduct and management of the affairs of the Board.
- Shall be in charge of all confidential documents of the Board and responsible for their safe custody.
- All orders and instructions of the Board shall be issued under the signature of the Chairperson or such officer authorised by the Chairperson.
- The Chairperson may sanction and disburse all payments against the approved budget.
- Shall have power to grant administrative and technical sanction to all estimates.
- Shall convene the meetings of the Board and preside over the meetings and ensure that all decisions taken are implemented properly.
- Shall exercise such other powers and perform such other functions as may be delegated by the Board or the State Government.

## 15. Procedure for Access to Biological Resources

- Any person who is a citizen of India, or any body corporate created by law in India, or any association or organisation registered in India, intending to undertake any activity referred to in section 7, shall give an intimation by an application in **Form** to the Board.
- On receipt of the application, the Board shall, after consultation with the concerned local bodies and collection of such other information, dispose of the application within a period of **one month** from the date of its receipt.
- The Board may grant approval or prohibit/restrict the activity if it is detrimental or contrary to the objectives of conservation and sustainable use of biological diversity or equitable sharing of benefits.
  - Provided that no order for prohibiting or restricting shall be made without giving an opportunity of being heard.
- If the Board grants approval, it may enter into a written agreement with the applicant allowing access to biological resources and associated knowledge. The agreement shall be signed by an officer authorised by the Board and the applicant.
- The form of the agreement shall be laid down by the Board and shall include, inter alia:
  - (a) general objectives and purpose for seeking approval;
  - (b) description of the biological resources and the traditional knowledge including accompanying information;
  - (c) intended uses (research, breeding, commercial utilisation, etc.);
  - (d) quantum of monetary and other incidental benefits (including a commitment to enter into a fresh written agreement in case any of the biological resources is taken up for research and is to be used later for commercial purpose or in case there is a change in use);
  - (e) restriction to transfer the accessed biological resources and associated traditional knowledge to any third party without prior approval of Board;
  - (f) obligation of the applicant to adhere to a limit set by the Board on the quantity and specification of quality of biological resources;
  - (g) guarantee to deposit with the repositories designated under section 39 a reference sample of the biological resources sought to be accessed;
  - (h) submission of regular status report of research and other development to the Board;
  - (i) commitment to abide by the provisions of the Act, and the rules, regulations and orders made thereunder;
  - (j) commitment to take measures for conservation and sustainable use of biological resources sought to be accessed;
  - (k) commitment to minimise environmental impacts on account of the collection activities;
  - (l) other matters such as duration of the agreement, notice in case of rescission, independent enforceability of individual clauses, survival of the benefit sharing clause in case of rescission, liability in case of natural calamities, arbitration of disputes and confidentiality clause.
- The agreement may include conditions relating to measures to be taken by the applicant for conservation and protection of biological resources and such other restrictions as the Board may deem fit.
- The Board shall widely publicise, through print and electronic media, the approvals granted and shall regularly monitor compliance of the conditions.

## 16. Revocation, etc. of Approval for Access to Biological Resources

- If the Board is satisfied, on the basis of any complaint or suo motu, that:
  - (a) there exists overriding public opinion for protection of environment or conservation of biological diversity which necessitates revocation; or
  - (b) the person granted approval has violated any provisions of the Act or rules, or failed to comply with terms and conditions of the agreement, or is destroying biological diversity while accessing,
- it may, after giving the person an opportunity of being heard, by order in writing, revoke the approval and rescind the written agreement.
- The Board shall send a copy of the order to the concerned Biodiversity Management Committee or local body for prohibiting access or assessing and recovering damage.

## 17. Restriction or Prohibition on Access to Biological Resources

In case the request for access:

- (a) is for any endangered taxa, or taxa likely to become threatened due to such access;
- (b) is for any endemic and rare species;
- (c) may likely result in adverse effect on the livelihoods of the local people;
- (d) may result in adverse environmental impact difficult to control and mitigate;
- (e) may cause genetic erosion affecting the ecosystem;
- (f) may cause destruction of any biological resources;
- (g) may be for use of resources for purposes contrary to national interest and other related international agreements entered into by India,

the Board may, after consultation with the concerned Biodiversity Management Committee or local body and after an enquiry and giving an opportunity of being heard, by order, restrict or prohibit access.

## 18. Operation, etc. of State Biodiversity Fund

- The State Biodiversity Fund shall be operated jointly by the Chairperson, or by the member to whom power has been delegated under sub-rule (8) of rule 14, and such other officer of the Board as may be authorised.
- The State Biodiversity Fund shall have the following heads of accounts:
  - (a) receipts from the Central Government;
  - (b) receipts from the State Government;
  - (c) other receipts.
- The Fund shall be applied for the purpose specified in sub-section (2) of section 32.

## 19. Annual Report and Audit of Accounts of Board

- The Board shall prepare its annual report for each financial year, giving a full account of its activities, and submit the same to the State Government.
- The Board may, subject to section 33, lay down the procedure for keeping accounts.
- The accounts of the Board shall, subject to section 34, be audited annually by a Chartered Accountant appointed by the Board.
- The Board shall furnish to the State Government the annual report together with the audited statement of accounts by the end of **September** each year.

## 20. Notification, etc. of Biodiversity Heritage Sites

- The Board shall, in consultation with the concerned Biodiversity Management Committee, local body and other authorities, take necessary steps to facilitate setting up of biodiversity heritage sites comprising areas of biodiversity importance.
- The State Government may, on recommendation of the Board, issue notification relating to biodiversity heritage sites.

## 21. Constitution, etc. of Biodiversity Management Committee

- Every local body shall constitute a Biodiversity Management Committee within its area of jurisdiction.
  - Provided that in panchayat area, a BMC may be constituted at the block level and at the district level.
- Every local body shall nominate **seven persons** as members for the BMC.
  - Provided that not less than **one third** of the members shall be women.
  - Provided further that not less than **eighteen per cent** of the members shall belong to the Scheduled Castes or the Scheduled Tribes.
- The Chairperson of the BMC shall be elected from amongst the members in a meeting presided over by the Chairperson of the concerned local body.
- BMCs may co-opt persons having expertise and knowledge in biodiversity conservation as special invitees.
- The tenure of office of the members of the BMC shall be **three years**.
- The concerned Member of Legislative Assembly and the concerned Member of Parliament shall be special invitees to the meetings of a BMC.
- The BMC shall prepare and update, in consultation with local people, a **People's Biodiversity Register** containing comprehensive information on the availability of local biological resources, their medicinal and other uses and other traditional knowledge associated thereto.
- The BMC shall advise on any matter concerning grant of approval referred to it by the State Biodiversity Board or National Biodiversity Authority and maintain data about local vaids and hakims.
- The People's Biodiversity Registers shall be prepared, updated and maintained by the BMCs in such manner as may be directed by the Board.
- The BMC shall also maintain a register containing information about details of access to biological resources and traditional knowledge, details of collection of fees and details of benefits derived and mode of sharing.
- The BMC shall take initiative to generate awareness regarding biological resources available in the locality, their ecological and economic importance and the need to conserve them, and shall prepare action plans.
- The BMC shall determine and periodically update the status of regeneration of all important species and quantum available within its area, by making inventory, under technical guidance of the Board.
- The BMC shall set up and maintain germplasm banks and seed centers or other centers of similar nature for promoting conservation of cultivars, folk varieties and landraces under technical guidance of the Board.
- The BMC shall carry out such other functions as may be assigned to it by the Board.

## 22. Local Biodiversity Fund

- Every Biodiversity Management Committee shall have a Local Biodiversity Fund.
- The Local Biodiversity Fund shall be operated jointly by the Chairperson, or by the member to whom power has been delegated, and such other officer of the BMC as may be authorised.
- The Local Biodiversity Fund shall have the following heads of accounts:
  - (a) receipts from the National Biodiversity Authority or the Central Government;
  - (b) receipts from the Board or the State Government;
  - (c) other receipts (including collection of fees).
- The Local Biodiversity Fund shall be used for the purpose specified in sub-section (2) of section 44 of the Act.
