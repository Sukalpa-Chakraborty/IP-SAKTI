## 8. How to use these links in your documentation

In your report / README / PPT appendix, you can have a section like:

### Data Sources and Citation

> The prototype uses publicly available government and intergovernmental sources:
> - Indian patent and trademark data: IP India ([https://ipindia.gov.in/](https://ipindia.gov.in/), [https://iprsearch.ipindia.gov.in/PublicSearch/](https://iprsearch.ipindia.gov.in/PublicSearch/), [https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/](https://tmrsearch.ipindia.gov.in/tmrpublicsearch/)).
> - Traditional knowledge context: CSIR TKDL ([https://www.csir.res.in/en/tkdl/about-tkdl](https://www.csir.res.in/en/tkdl/about-tkdl)).
> - Biological Diversity and ABS: National Biodiversity Authority ([https://www.nbaindia.org/](https://www.nbaindia.org/), [https://absefiling.nbaindia.in/](https://absefiling.nbaindia.in/)).
> - Statutory text: India Code ([https://indiacode.gov.in/](https://indiacode.gov.in/)), including the Patents Act, 1970; Biological Diversity Act, 2002; Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940.
> - International patents and treaties: WIPO PATENTSCOPE ([https://patentscope.wipo.int/](https://patentscope.wipo.int/)) and GRATK Treaty ([https://www.wipo.int/en/web/treaties/ip/gratk/index](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/treaties/ip/gratk/index)).
> - AYUSH sector information: Ministry of AYUSH ([https://ayush.gov.in/](https://ayush.gov.in/)).

Then, for each specific claim in your report, you can add inline citations like:

- “Patentability criteria are based on the Patents Act, 1970 as published on India Code [indiacode.gov.in].”
- “ABS obligations are derived from the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 and NBA portal guidance [indiacode.gov.in][nbaindia.org].”
- “Traditional‑knowledge protection context follows CSIR’s description of TKDL [csir.res.in].”
- “International disclosure requirements reference the WIPO GRATK Treaty text [wipo.int].”
