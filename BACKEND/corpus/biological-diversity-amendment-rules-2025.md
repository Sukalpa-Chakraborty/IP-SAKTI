# Biological Diversity (Amendment) Rules, 2025

## 1. Document Overview

This is a notification from the **Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change**, Government of India, amending the **Biological Diversity Rules, 2024**. The amendment is made in exercise of powers conferred by section 62 of the **Biological Diversity Act, 2002**.

The amendment introduces new procedures for obtaining a **certificate of origin for cultivated medicinal plants**, and substitutes Rule 19, First Schedule entries, and Forms 11 and 12.

Published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II, Section 3, Sub-section (i), vide G.S.R. 295(E), dated 6th May 2025.

## 2. Key Amendments

### 2.1 Substitution of Rule 19
**New Rule 19: Procedure for obtaining a certificate of origin for cultivated medicinal plants**

- (1) Books containing details of cultivated medicinal plants shall be maintained by the Biodiversity Management Committee in **Form 11** on the web portal.
- (2) An application for obtaining certificate of origin under sub-section (2) of section 7 of the Act shall be made to the Biodiversity Management Committee along with the fee in **Form 11A** on the web portal.
- (3) On the basis of entries made in the books maintained under sub-rule (1), the certificate of origin shall be generated in the web portal in **Form 12**.

## 3. RAG Q&A Extraction

### Q: What is the purpose of this amendment?
**A:** To amend the Biological Diversity Rules, 2024, by substituting Rule 19, First Schedule entries, and Forms 11 and 12, to provide a procedure for obtaining a certificate of origin for cultivated medicinal plants.

### Q: Who maintains the books containing details of cultivated medicinal plants?
**A:** The Biodiversity Management Committee (BMC), in Form 11 on the web portal.

### Q: What is the fee for applying for a certificate of origin?
**A:** ₹200, as per entry 11A of the First Schedule.

### Q: What is the fee for the certificate of origin issued to the applicant?
**A:** Nil.

### Q: What is Form 11A?
**A:** Application for obtaining certificate of origin for cultivated medicinal plants.

### Q: What does Form 12 contain?
**A:** Certificate of Origin, including a table of cultivated medicinal plants and quantities, issued by the Biodiversity Management Committee.

### Q: When were the principal rules published?
**A:** 22nd October 2024, vide G.S.R. 665(E).
