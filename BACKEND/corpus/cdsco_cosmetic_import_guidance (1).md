# Comprehensive Guidance on CDSCO Cosmetic Import Registration in India

## 1. Overview & Regulatory Framework

In India, the import of cosmetics is regulated by the **Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO)** under the **Central Licensing Authority**, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India. 

The primary legislation governing cosmetic imports is the **Cosmetics Rules, 2020**, framed under the **Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940**.

* **Mandate:** No cosmetic product can be imported into India without obtaining an Import Registration Certificate from the Licensing Authority.
* **Governing Body:** CDSCO (Headquarters: FDA Bhawan, Kotla Road, New Delhi).
* **Online Portal:** Applications are processed through the **SUGAM Portal** (`cdscoonline.gov.in`).

---

## 2. Key Entities & Roles

### A. Foreign Manufacturer
The legal or actual manufacturing entity located outside India responsible for producing the cosmetic product.

### B. Authorized Agent / Authorized Indian Agent (AIA)
A foreign manufacturer must appoint an Authorized Agent in India who will act as the legal representative.
* **Eligibility:** Must be an Indian entity possessing a valid **Wholesale Drug License** or **Cosmetic Manufacturing License**.
* **Authorization:** Appointed via a duly executed **Power of Attorney (PoA)**.

---

## 3. Key Regulatory Forms

| Form Name | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Form COS-1** | Application for issue of Registration Certificate for import of cosmetics into India. |
| **Form COS-2** | Import Registration Certificate granted by the CDSCO Licensing Authority. |
| **Form COS-3** | Import license for import of cosmetics for testing, evaluation, and demonstration purposes. |
| **Form COS-4** | Application for import of cosmetics for personal use. |

---

## 4. Government Fee Structure (Schedule III)

The official fees payable to the Central Drugs Standard Control Organization via the Bharatkosh online payment portal are as follows:

1. **Manufacturing Site Registration:** USD $1,000$ (or equivalent in INR) per manufacturing site.
2. **Category Registration:** USD $500$ (or equivalent in INR) for each category of cosmetic product.
3. **Variant Registration:** USD $50$ (or equivalent in INR) for each additional variant within a category.
4. **Duplicate Certificate:** USD $500$ (if lost or damaged).

## 5. Required Document Checklist

To file Form COS-1, the following documents must be prepared, signed, and uploaded:

### 1. Administrative Documents
* **Covering Letter:** Issued by the Authorized Indian Agent introducing the application.
* **Form COS-1:** Duly filled and signed by the Authorized Agent.
* **Power of Attorney (PoA):** Duly executed by the foreign manufacturer authorizing the Indian Agent. Must be:
  * Notarized and Apostilled (for Hague Convention signatory countries), OR
  * Authenticated by the Indian Embassy/Consulate in the country of origin.
* **Free Sale Certificate (FSC):** Issued by the National Regulatory Authority (e.g., US FDA, EU Authority) of the country of origin, certifying that the product is freely sold in that country.
* **Manufacturing License / ISO / GMP Certificate:** Proof of valid manufacturing operating license or ISO 22716 / Good Manufacturing Practices certification.

### 2. Product Technical Documents
* **Product Master File (PMF):** Detailed product descriptions, composition, manufacturing process flow charts, and specifications.
* **Quantitative & Qualitative Formula:** List of ingredients with concentration levels, function, and CAS numbers. Ingredients must comply with **IS 4707** (Bureau of Indian Standards).
* **Finished Product Specifications & Test Methods:** Analytical testing standards and Certificates of Analysis (CoA) for representative batches.
* **Non-Animal Testing Declaration:** A self-declaration certifying that the cosmetic product and its ingredients have not been tested on animals on or after November 12, 2014.
* **Heavy Metals & Microbiological Test Reports:** Testing reports confirming limits for heavy metals (Lead, Arsenic, Mercury, Cadmium) and microbial limits.

### 3. Packaging & Labeling Documents
* **Original Artwork / Specimen Labels:** Primary and secondary artwork reflecting foreign packaging.
* **Proposed Indian Label Artwork:** Artwork showing compliance with Indian labeling norms (including Importer details, Reg No space, Veg/Non-Veg symbol).

## 6. Indian Labeling Compliance Requirements

Every imported cosmetic pack arriving at Indian ports must bear a label with the following mandatory information in English:

1. **Brand & Product Name**
2. **Name & Address of Foreign Manufacturer**
3. **Name, Address, & Wholesale License Number of the Authorized Importer**
4. **Import Registration Certificate Number:** Format: `RC/COS-XXXXXX`
5. **Net Content / Volume**
6. **Batch / Lot Number**
7. **Manufacturing Date & Expiry / Best Before Date** (or PAO - Period After Opening if applicable)
8. **Ingredients List** (as per International Nomenclature of Cosmetic Ingredients - INCI)
9. **Origin Indicator:** 
   * Green symbol (dot in a square) for 100% vegetarian cosmetics.
   * Red/Brown symbol for cosmetics containing ingredients of animal origin (e.g., carmine, lanolin, gelatin).
10. **Maximum Retail Price (MRP):** Inclusive of all taxes in INR ($\text{₹}$).

```
[Step 1: Appointment of Authorized Agent]

                  ▼

                  │
                  ▼

                  │
                  ▼
[Step 4: Form COS-1 Draft & Document Upload]
                  │
                  ▼
[Step 5: Fee Payment via Bharatkosh]
                  │
                  ▼
[Step 6: Submission & Processing by CDSCO (Approx. 90 Days)]
                  │
        ┌─────────┴─────────┐
        ▼                   ▼
 [Query / Deficiency]    [Approval]
        │                   │
        ▼                   ▼
[Response Submission]   [Form COS-2 Issued]
```

1. **Preparation:** Finalize product categories, variants, and gather apostilled/consularized legal documents.
2. **SUGAM Portal Registration:** The Indian Agent registers their firm details and wholesale license on the portal.
3. **Form COS-1 Application Filing:** Fill in product details, variants, ingredients, and upload all requisite files.
4. **Fee Payment:** Calculate total fees (Site + Categories + Variants) and pay online via Bharatkosh.
5. **CDSCO Scrutiny:** CDSCO evaluates the application. If deficiencies are found, a query letter is issued.
6. **Query Resolution:** The applicant must respond to query letters within the stipulated timeframe.
7. **Grant of Certificate:** Upon satisfactory evaluation, CDSCO issues **Form COS-2** (Import Registration Certificate).

---

## 8. Validity & Post-Grant Rules

* **Validity:** Form COS-2 is valid perpetually, provided a retention fee is paid every **5 years** from the date of issue.
* **Post-Approval Changes:**
  * **Minor Changes** (e.g., change in pack size, minor label artwork design): Requires notification to CDSCO.
  * **Major Changes** (e.g., addition of new variants, change in formulation, change in manufacturing site): Requires filing an amendment or fresh application.
