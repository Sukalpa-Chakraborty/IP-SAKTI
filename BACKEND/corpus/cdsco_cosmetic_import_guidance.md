# CDSCO Guidance Document: Registration and Import of Cosmetics into India

**Issued by:** Central Drugs Standard Control Organization (CDSCO)  
**Division:** Cosmetics Division  
**Date:** 19.01.2021  
**Title:** Guidance Document on Application Submission for obtaining Import Registration Certificate for import of cosmetics into India  

---

> **Note:** This Guidance Document is aimed only for creating public awareness about regulation on import of Cosmetics by CDSCO and is not meant to be used for any legal or professional purposes. Readers are advised to refer to the statutory provisions of the *Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940* & Rules made thereunder, Notifications in the Official Gazette, and Guidelines/Clarifications issued by CDSCO from time to time.

---

## Preface

* **Definition of Cosmetic:** Under **Section 3(aaa)** of the *Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940*, a cosmetic is defined as:
  > *"Any article intended to be rubbed, poured, sprinkled or sprayed on, or introduced into, or otherwise applied to, the human body or any part thereof for cleansing, beautifying, promoting attractiveness, or altering the appearance, and includes any article intended for use as a component of cosmetic."*

* **Regulatory Framework:** Import of cosmetics into India is regulated through a registration system by the CDSCO under the *Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940* and the *Cosmetics Rules, 2020*. Any cosmetic article must be registered along with its pack size, variants, and manufacturing premises prior to import.
* **Mandatory Registration:** As per **Rule 12(1)** of the *Cosmetics Rules, 2020*, no cosmetic shall be imported unless registered by the Central Licensing Authority (Drugs Controller General (I), CDSCO HQ, New Delhi).
* **Application Mode:** Online application in **Form COS-1** submitted via the **SUGAM Portal** of CDSCO with requisite fees and documentation.
* **Quality & Safety Standards:** As per **Rule 39**, products must comply with specifications prescribed under the **Ninth Schedule** or applicable standards in the country of origin if not listed under the Ninth Schedule.
* **Prohibition of Misleading Claims:** As per **Rule 36**, no cosmetic may claim or convey any false or misleading information.

---

## Guidance for Submission of Application in Form COS-1

1. **PURPOSE:** Provide guidance to stakeholders for submission of application in **Form COS-1** to CDSCO for grant of Import Registration Certificate in **Form COS-2**.
2. **SCOPE:** Applicable to manufacturers or their authorized agents in India.
3. **MODE OF SUBMISSION:** Online submission on the **SUGAM portal** of CDSCO (`cdscoonline.gov.in`).

---

### Key Requirements Checklist

#### 4.1 Covering Letter
* State the purpose clearly: Fresh Registration, Endorsement (of Products / Manufacturing site / Pack Size / Sourcing Location), or Re-Registration.
* Reference details of previously issued Import Registration Certificates (if any) and product categories.
* Include correlation section numbers matching product serial numbers in Form COS-1 with Free Sale Certificates (FSC) and Authorization letters.

#### 4.2 Authorisation from Manufacturer (First Schedule)
* Executed and authenticated by a First Class Magistrate in India, or equivalent authority in the country of origin, or attested by the Indian Embassy, or **Apostilled** under the Hague Convention.
* Must explicitly state full legal names and addresses of both the overseas manufacturer (and its actual manufacturing sites) and the Authorized Indian Agent.
* Product list and variants categorized per the **Fourth Schedule** of the *Cosmetics Rules, 2020*.
* Jointly signed, stamped, and dated by both the authorized Indian agent and the foreign manufacturer.
*(Pro forma available in Annexure-I)*

#### 4.3 Part-I of Second Schedule
* Information and undertaking furnished by the manufacturer or authorized agent.
*(Pro forma available in Annexure-II)*

#### 4.4 List of Ingredients / Composition
* Full ingredient list with standard nomenclature (INCI names) and exact percentages, signed and stamped by an authorized representative of the manufacturer.
* **Prohibited Raw Materials:** Ingredients listed under Annex A of Bureau of Indian Standards **IS: 4707 (Part 2)** shall not be added.
* **Dyes & Pigments:** Must strictly comply with **IS: 4707 (Part 1 or Part 2)** and the **Tenth Schedule**. Permitted synthetic/natural organic colors must not exceed:
  * **Arsenic ($As_2O_3$):** $\le 2\text{ ppm}$
  * **Lead ($Pb$):** $\le 20\text{ ppm}$
  * **Heavy Metals (other than lead):** $\le 100\text{ ppm}$ total
* **Hexachlorophene Restriction:** Prohibited, except in soaps at concentrations $\le 1\%\text{ w/w}$ with mandatory warning label: *"Contains hexachlorophene not to be used on babies"*.
* **Mercury Limits:**
  * Eye cosmetics: Max $70\text{ ppm}$ ($0.007\%$) of mercury as a preservative.
  * Other finished products: Unintentional mercury $\le 1\text{ ppm}$.
* **Lead & Arsenic:** Lead and arsenic compounds are strictly prohibited as coloring agents.

#### 4.5 Product Labels
Legible original labels for each proposed product and variant complying with **Chapter VI** of *Cosmetics Rules, 2020*:
* Product name, brand name, and complete address of actual manufacturing site ("Made in [Country]").
* For small containers ($\le 30\text{ g}$ solid/semi-solid or $\le 60\text{ ml}$ liquid), manufacturer name and principal place of manufacture with pin/zip code.
* Expiry Date / "Use before" / "Expiry XX months from MFD".
* Distinctive Batch Number or Lot Number.
* Manufacturing License Number (if mandatory in country of origin).
* Declaration of Net Content.
* Directions for safe use, hazardous warnings, and cautions where applicable.
* Import Registration Certificate Number preceded by **"RC No."** or **"Reg. Cert. No."** along with name and address of the Indian importer (stickering permitted at bonded warehouses prior to release).
* Full ingredient list in descending order of concentration (for ingredients $> 1\%$).

#### 4.6 Specification and Testing Methods
* Laboratory specification sheets and analytical testing methods signed and stamped by a competent technical representative. Compliance with **Ninth Schedule** or standards of origin country.

#### 4.7 Pack Insert
* Mandatory if provided with the product.

#### 4.8 Manufacturing Licenses / Authorizations
* Authenticated copy of manufacturing license, registration, or marketing authorization issued by the National Regulatory Authority (NRA) in the country of origin. If no such system exists, an official undertaking is required.

#### 4.9 Free Sale Certificate (FSC)
* Original FSC issued by the National Regulatory Authority or competent trade association in the country of origin.
* Must be authenticated via Indian Embassy, or Chamber of Commerce + Apostille, or Notary Public + Apostille.
* Must explicitly state that the specific products are freely sold/marketed in the country of origin.

#### 4.10 Non-Animal Testing Declaration
* No animal testing is permitted for cosmetics.
* Products tested on animals after **November 12, 2014** are strictly prohibited from import into India.
* A signed manufacturer declaration certifying non-animal testing after the cutoff date must be provided.

#### 4.11 Heavy Metal and Hexachlorophene Declaration
* Test reports or manufacturer declarations confirming limits for $Pb$, $As$, $Hg$, heavy metals, microbiological loads, and hexachlorophene content comply with Indian standards.

#### 4.12 Form COS-1 Application
* Formal online application submitted via SUGAM portal, fully aligned with details present in Authorisation letters and FSCs.

#### 4.13 Fee Structure (Via Bharatkosh Portal)
Fees must be paid under Head of Account `0210041040000-00-1` as per the **Third Schedule**:
* **Grant or retention of Registration Certificate:** $1,000\text{ USD}$ per product category
* **Registration of each Manufacturing Site:** $500\text{ USD}$
* **Registration of each Variant:** $50\text{ USD}$
* **Additional Category of Cosmetic:** $1,000\text{ USD}$

## Annexure-II: Second Schedule [Rules 12(4), 23(2), 23(4)]
### Part-I: Information and Undertaking

1. **Particulars of Manufacturer & Premises:**
   * Name, address, telephone, fax, and email of manufacturer and actual manufacturing sites.
   * Names and addresses of Partners/Directors.
   * Name and address of Authorized Agent/Importer in India and its Partners/Directors.
   * Brief corporate profile of manufacturer’s business activities (domestic and global).

2. **Particulars of Cosmetics:**
   * Names, brand names, categories, pack sizes, and variants proposed for import.
   * Manufacturing licenses, registration certificates, or FSCs issued in the country of origin.
   * List of countries where marketing authorization/import permission is granted.

3. **Chemical Information:**
   * Complete ingredient declaration with standard reference names and percentages.
   * Testing methods and finished product specifications.
   * Labeling copies and package inserts.

4. **Manufacturer Undertaking Declarations:**
   * Compliance with all conditions of the Registration Certificate under *Cosmetics Rules, 2020*.
   * Reporting any changes in manufacturing premises or distribution of functions.
   * Strict adherence to Chapter III and Ninth Schedule (BIS standards).
   * Guarantee that **no cosmetic imported has been tested on animals**.
   * Notification to Licensing Authority within **30 days** regarding any change in labeling, composition, or testing specifications.
   * Immediate notification and cessation of dispatch/marketing in case of adverse reaction reports, market recalls, regulatory restrictions, or "Not of Standard Quality" reports in any country.
   * Permission for the Licensing Authority to sample products for testing.
