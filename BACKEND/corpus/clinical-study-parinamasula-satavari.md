# Clinical Study of Parinamasula and Treatment with Satavari (Asparagus racemosus)

## 1. Document Overview

This is a clinical study from the **Department of Kayachikitsa, Institute of Medical Sciences, Banaras Hindu University, Varanasi**. It investigates the effect of **Satavari (Asparagus racemosus Willd.)** root powder on **Parinamasula** (duodenal ulcer).

Received: 10 October 1994; Accepted: 2 January 1995.

Authors: Mrs Bharti, C.M. Maheshwari, S.K. Tewari.

## 2. Abstract

<!-- chunk_id: parinamasula-abs-001 -->

Twenty cases of Parinamasula (duodenal ulcer) were selected. Diagnosis based on barium meal, X-ray, gastric analysis, clinical history, and physical examination. All cases were ulcer positive or had duodenal bulb deformity. Group A (15 cases) had hyperchlorhydria and hyperacidity; Group B (5 cases) had normal gastric acidity. All patients received **Satavari tuberous root powder 20 g/day** in three divided doses with milk for one month. Results assessed.

## 3. Introduction

<!-- chunk_id: parinamasula-intro-001 -->

Parinamasula is characterized by pain during digestion at epigastrium (Kuksi), flank (Jathara Parsva), umbilical region (Nabhi), inter-mammary region (Stannatar), back (Prsth Mula), hypogastrium (Vasti), or all sites. Pain subsides after vomiting/meals or digestion, and aggravates during digestion or after food. Duodenal ulcer is an exact clinical correlate.

Signs and symptoms not mentioned in Caraka Samhita, Susruta Samhita, Astanga-Hridya, and Astanga Samgraha.

Satawari Mandura is popularly used by Ayurvedic physicians for Parinamasula. Satawari is Madhura and Tikta Rasa, Madhura Vipaka, Sita Virya, hence Pittahara. Parinamasula is a disease with dominance of Pitta dosa. Therefore Satawari is beneficial.

## 4. Materials and Methods

<!-- chunk_id: parinamasula-methods-001 -->

- Radiologically confirmed 20 cases of duodenal ulcer.
- Some cases had gastroscopy.
- Group A: 15 cases with hyperacidity.
- Group B: 5 cases with normal hydrochloric acid.
- Patients were chronic, leading to duodenal bulb deformity.
- Examined clinically (pain, tenderness in epigastrium) with lab and radiological investigations.
- **Treatment:** 20 g Satavari (tuberous root) powder in three divided doses with milk for one month.
- Free hydrochloric acid and total acidity estimated by fractional test meal examination (Ehrman Alcohol meal) before and after treatment.
- Response rated: excellent, good, poor, unchanged.

## 5. Observations

<!-- chunk_id: parinamasula-observ-001 -->

Satawari caused significant improvement in symptomatology of duodenal ulcer patients.

**Symptoms before and after one month of treatment with Satavari (root powder 20 g/day):**

| S. No. | Symptoms | Present before treatment | Improved after treatment | Improvement % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Epigastric pain | 20 | 17 | 85 |
| 2 | Burning sensation in epigastric region | 16 | 15 | 63.7 |
| 3 | Loss of appetite | 10 | 8 | 80 |
| 4 | Nausea | 8 | 8 | 100 |
| 5 | Constipation | 7 | 5 | 71.4 |
| 6 | Flatulence | 6 | 6 | 100 |
| 7 | Vomiting | 6 | 6 | 100 |
| 8 | Malena | 4 | 3 | 75 |
| 9 | Mental symptoms | 3 | 1 | 33.3 |
| 10 | Loose motion | 2 | 2 | 100 |

Nausea and vomiting relieved within 4–6 days. Burning sensation and pain in epigastric region relieved within 10–15 days. Loss of appetite and flatulence improved within 20–25 days.

## 7. Summary and Conclusion

<!-- chunk_id: parinamasula-concl-001 -->

1. Parinamasula and duodenal ulcer are clinically similar entities.
2. Treatment with Satavari (root powder) shows symptomatic improvement.
3. Satavari is effective in reducing total acids and free hydrochloric acid of gastric juice to significant levels.
4. In chronic cases, Satavari reduced ESR significantly.
5. Satavari exhibited excellent response in 50% cases and good response in 30%.

## 9. RAG Q&A Extraction

### Q: What is Parinamasula?
**A:** A condition characterized by pain during digestion, clinically correlated with duodenal ulcer.

### Q: What was the treatment given?
**A:** Satavari (Asparagus racemosus) tuberous root powder 20 g/day in three divided doses with milk for one month.

### Q: What was the effect on total acidity?
**A:** Significant reduction in total acidity in Group A (hyperacidity) patients.

### Q: What was the effect on ESR?
**A:** ESR decreased from mean 23.15 ± 9.73 to 13.25 ± 4.78 (highly significant).

### Q: What was the overall response rate?
**A:** Excellent in 50%, good in 30%, poor in 15%, unchanged in 5%.

### Q: What is the conclusion?
**A:** Satavari is effective in treating Parinamasula (duodenal ulcer) by reducing acidity and improving symptoms.
