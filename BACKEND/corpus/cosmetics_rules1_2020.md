# Cosmetics Rules, 2020

**Notification:** G.S.R. 763(E) dated 15th December, 2020
**Ministry:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (Department of Health and Family Welfare)
**Authority:** Central Government, in exercise of powers conferred by section 12 and section 33 of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 (23 of 1940).

---

## CHAPTER I: PRELIMINARY

### Rule 1: Short title and commencement
(1) These rules may be called the **Cosmetics Rules, 2020**.
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

### Rule 2: Application
These rules shall be applicable to the cosmetic as defined in clause (aaa) of section 3 of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 (23 of 1940).

### Rule 3: Definitions
In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:
- **(a) "Act"** means the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 (23 of 1940).
- **(b) "Actual manufacturer"** in relation to import of cosmetics, means a person who manufactures cosmetics at his own manufacturing site in a country other than India approved by National Regulatory Authority or any authorised competent authority in that country for that purpose.
  - *Explanation:* "Person" includes a company or a unit or a body corporate or any other establishment.
- **(c) "Appendix"** means the appendix appended to these rules.
- **(d) "Authorised agent"** means a person in India authorised by the manufacturer. The authorised agent shall be responsible for the business activities of the manufacturer in India including compliance to the provisions of the Act and rules made thereunder.
  - *Explanation:* "Person" includes a company or a unit or a body corporate or any other establishment.
- **(e) "Bureau of Indian Standards"** means the Bureau of Indian Standards established under section 3 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016 (11 of 2016).
- **(f) "Central Licensing Authority"** means the Drugs Controller General of India, appointed by the Central Government.
- **(g) "Change in the constitution of a licensee"** means in relation to:
  - (i) a firm, a change from proprietorship to partnership including Limited Liability Partnership or vice versa;
  - (ii) a company: (A) its conversion from a private to a public company, or from a public to a private company; or (B) any change in the ownership of shares of more than fifty per cent of the voting capital in the body corporate or in case of a body corporate not having a share capital, any change in its membership; and where the managing agent, being a body corporate is a subsidiary of another body corporate, includes a change in the constitution of that other body corporate within the meaning of this clause.
- **(h) "Director"** means the Director of the Central Cosmetics Laboratory appointed by the Central Government under sub-rule 3 of rule 11.
- **(i) "Form"** means a Form set out in Appendix to these rules.
- **(j) "Government Analyst"** means a Government Analyst appointed by the Central Government or the State Government under section 20 of the Act.
- **(k) "Import registration certificate"** means a certificate issued under rule 13 by the Central Licensing Authority for registration of cosmetics manufactured for import into and use in India.
- **(l) "Inspector"** means and includes an Inspector appointed by the Central Government or a State Government respectively under section 21 of the Act.
- **(m) "Laboratory"** means the Central Cosmetics Laboratory established or notified for carrying out analysis or test of cosmetics by the Central Government under rule 11.
- **(n) "Legal manufacturer or brand owner"** in relation to import of cosmetics, means a person, who authorise the other manufacturer from India or overseas countries for the manufacture of cosmetics, by way of an authorization referred under rule 12.
  - *Explanation:* "Person" includes a company or a unit or a body corporate or any other establishment.
- **(o) "Licence"** means a licence granted by the State Licensing Authority under rule 25.
- **(p) "Loan licence"** means a licence granted by the State Licensing Authority under rule 25, for manufacturing a cosmetic, to a person who intends to utilize the manufacturing site of another licensee for manufacturing the cosmetic manufactured by the licensee at that site.
- **(q) "Manufacturer"** in relation to import of cosmetics means the actual manufacturer or the legal manufacturer.
- **(r) "New cosmetic"** means a cosmetic which contains a novel ingredient which has not been used anywhere in the world or is not recognised for use in cosmetics in any National or International literature.
- **(s) "Schedule"** means a Schedule appended to these rules.
- **(t) "State Government"** in relation to a Union Territory means the Administrator thereof.
- **(u) "State Licensing Authority"** means the state drugs controller by whatever name called and authorised by the Government of a State or a Union territory, as the case may be, to perform the duties of Licensing Authority under these rules in the State or Union territory and includes any person to whom powers of the Licensing Authority may be delegated by the Government of the State or Union territory.
- **(v) "Subsidiary"** means an entity in India owned by the manufacturer.
- **(w) "Use before" or "date of expiry"** means the date recorded on the container, label or wrapper as the date upto which the cosmetic shall retain its characteristics as per standards at proposed storage condition stated on label.
- **(x)** The words and expressions used in these rules and not defined herein but defined in the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 (23 of 1940) and the Drugs Rules, 1945 shall have the same meaning as assigned to them in that Act and rules respectively.

## CHAPTER II: LICENSING AUTHORITIES, OFFICERS AND LABORATORIES

### Rule 4: Licensing Authorities
(1) The **Central Licensing Authority** shall be the competent authority for enforcement of these rules in matters relating to:
  - (i) import of all categories of cosmetics;
  - (ii) co-ordination with the State Licensing Authorities.
(2) The **State Licensing Authority** shall be the competent authority for enforcement of these rules in matters relating to:
  - (i) manufacture for sale or distribution of all categories of cosmetics;
  - (ii) sale, stock, exhibit or offer for sale or distribution of all categories of cosmetics;
  - (iii) grant of approval to the laboratory which applies for carrying out tests on cosmetics and their raw materials under Chapter VIII.

### Rule 5: Delegation of powers of Licensing Authorities
(1) The Central Licensing Authority may, with the prior approval of the Central Government, delegate all or any of its powers to any other officer of the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation not below the rank of Assistant Drugs Controller.
(2) The officer to whom powers are delegated shall exercise the powers under the name and seal of the Central Licensing Authority.
(3) The State Licensing Authority may, with the prior approval of the State Government, delegate all or any of its powers to any officer not below the rank of Assistant Drugs Controller or equivalent.
(4) The officer to whom powers are delegated shall exercise the powers under the name and seal of the State Licensing Authority.

### Rule 6: Controlling officer
Any officer not below the rank of Assistant Drugs Controller shall be the controlling officer to supervise and give instructions to subordinate officers to exercise powers and functions under these rules for areas and purposes specified by an order of the Drugs Controller General of India or the State Drugs Controller.

### Rule 7: Government Analyst
The Central Government or a State Government may appoint by notification in the Official Gazette, the Government Analyst for the purpose of these rules as provided in section 20 of the Act and rules made thereunder.

### Rule 8: Functions of Government Analyst
(1) The Government Analyst shall cause to be analysed or tested such cosmetics as may be sent to him by an Inspector or any person under the provisions of Chapter IV of the Act and shall furnish reports of the results of test or analysis within a period of sixty days of the receipt of the sample.
  - *Provided* that where it is not possible to test or analyse the sample within the specified period, the Government Analyst shall seek extension of time from the concerned Government giving specific reasons for delay.
(2) A Government Analyst shall, from time to time, forward to the Central Licensing Authority, reports giving the result of analytical work and research with a view to their publication at the discretion of the Central Government.

### Rule 9: Powers, duties and functions of Inspectors specially Authorised to inspect manufacture and sale of cosmetics
(1) Subject to the instructions of the controlling officer, it shall be the duty of an Inspector appointed under section 21 of the Act by the State Government, authorised to inspect the manufacture of cosmetics:
  - (i) to inspect not less than once in a three year, all premises licensed to manufacture cosmetics within the area allotted to him.
  - (ii) to send a detailed report after each inspection to the controlling officer.
  - (iii) to institute prosecution in respect of breach of the Act and Rules thereunder.
  - (iv) take samples of cosmetics manufactured or imported for sale, or stocked or exhibited for sale in respect of which the inspector has reason to suspect contravention.
  - (v) maintain a record of all inspections undertaken, drawing of samples, seizure of stocks and action taken.
  - (vi) make such enquiries and inspections as may be necessary to detect the manufacture or sale of cosmetics in contravention of any provision of the Act and these rules.
  - (vii) investigate any complaint made in writing relating to cosmetic.
  - (viii) review technical dossier of cosmetic furnished with the application under these rules.
(2) Without prejudice to the duties and functions assigned to the Inspector appointed by the State Government, such duties and functions may be discharged by the Inspector appointed by the Central Government under section 21 of the Act, as directed by the Central Licensing Authority or Drugs Controller General of India.

### Rule 10: Prohibition of disclosure of information
Except for the purposes of official business or when required by a court of law, an Inspector shall not, without the sanction in writing of his official superior, disclose to any person any information acquired by him during the course of his official duties.

### Rule 11: Establishment and functions of the Central Cosmetics Laboratory
(1) The Central Government may, by notification, establish Central Cosmetics Laboratory for the purpose of:
  - (a) to analyse or test such samples of cosmetics as may be sent to it under sub-section (2) of section 11, or under sub-section (4) of section 25 of the Act;
  - (b) functioning as an appellate laboratory;
  - (c) to carry out any other function as may be specifically assigned to it.
(2) Without prejudice to sub-rule (1), the Central Government may also designate or notify any laboratory under its control and the Director of such laboratory having facility for carrying out test and evaluation of cosmetics as Central Cosmetics Laboratory.
  - *Provided* that no Laboratory shall be so designated unless it has been duly accredited by the National Accreditation Body for Testing and Calibration Laboratories.
(3) The Central Cosmetic Laboratory shall be headed by a Director who shall be appointed or designated by the Central Government.

## CHAPTER III: IMPORT OF COSMETICS

### Rule 12: Import of cosmetics
(1) No cosmetic shall be imported into India unless the product has been registered in accordance with these rules by the Central Licensing Authority or by any officer to whom such powers may be delegated under sub-rule (1) of rule 5.
(2) An application for registration of a cosmetic product intended to be imported into India shall be made through the online portal of the Central Government in **Form COS-1** either by the manufacturer himself or by his authorised agent or the importer in India or by the subsidiary in India authorised by the manufacturer.
(3) An authorisation by the manufacturer to his agent in India shall be duly authenticated either in India before a first class Magistrate or in the country of origin before the authority competent under the laws of that country or by an authority specified in the First Schedule.
(4) The applicant referred to sub-rule (2) above shall furnish along with the application such other information and documents as specified in Part I of the Second Schedule.
  - *Provided* that in the event of application for import of bulk finished formulation ready to fill, the following additional documents shall also be required:
    - (i) a valid manufacturing license for the finished formulation of the cosmetic ready to fill in finished form from the State Licensing Authority; and
    - (ii) details of registered brand owner of the finished product in India.
(5) The application for registration shall be accompanied by a copy of the receipt of fee having been deposited as specified in the Third Schedule.
(6) The fee shall be such for each category of cosmetic along with each manufacturing site with additional fee for each category of cosmetic and variant specified in the Fourth Schedule.
(7) Till such time, the online portal becomes operational, offline application in Form COS-1 may be made.
(8) The applicant shall be liable to pay testing fees directly to the testing Laboratory approved by the Central Government referred in rule 11, for examination, test and analysis of imported cosmetics as specified in the Fifth Schedule.
(9) The applicant shall pay the fee as specified in the Third Schedule for inspecting or visiting the manufacturing premises of cosmetics approved in foreign countries by officers authorised by Central Licensing Authority.

### Rule 13: Grant of import registration certificate
(1) After examination of documents furnished with the application under sub-rule (2) of rule 12, the Central Licensing Authority may, on being satisfied, grant import registration certificate in **Form COS-2** or may reject such application for which reasons shall be recorded in writing within a period of six months from the date of application.
(2) In the event of rejection, the applicant may appeal to the Central Government within a period of forty-five days and that Government may pass orders within ninety days from the date of appeal.
(3) In case of a new cosmetic, the applicant shall obtain prior permission in **Form COS-3** from the Central Licensing Authority before registration of import.
(4) A single application may be made and a single registration certificate in Form COS-2 may be issued for import of one or more cosmetics manufactured by the same manufacturer.
  - *Provided* that the cosmetics are manufactured at one factory or more than one factory functioning conjointly as a single manufacturing unit.
(5) A fee as specified in the Third Schedule shall be paid for a duplicate copy of the registration certificate, if the original is defaced, damaged or lost.

### Rule 14: Validity of import registration certificate
(1) A registration certificate granted under rule 13 shall remain valid in perpetuity, subject to payment of registration certificate retention fee as specified in the Third Schedule before completion of the period of five years from the date of its issue, unless suspended or cancelled.
(2) If the licensee fails to pay the retention fee on or before the due date, the registration certificate holder shall be liable to pay a late fee calculated at the rate of two per cent of the retention fee for every month or part thereof within one hundred and eighty days. In the event of non-payment, the registration certificate shall be deemed to have been cancelled.

### Rule 15: Fresh application in case of change in constitution
(1) In case of change in constitution of a registration holder or overseas manufacturer, an application shall be made for grant of fresh registration within a period of one hundred and eighty days from the date of such change.
  - *Provided* that the existing registration shall be deemed to be valid till such time fresh registration is issued or application is rejected.
(2) In case of any change in respect of labelling or composition or testing of registered product or its specifications, the Central Licensing Authority shall be informed within fifteen days.
(3) In case of change in name or address of a registration holder or overseas manufacturer, an application for amendment shall be made in online portal of Central Government for prior approval within sixty days from the date of such change.

### Rule 16: Suspension and cancellation of Registration Certificate
If the manufacturer or authorised agent or importer fails to comply with any of the conditions of the Registration Certificate, the Central Licensing Authority may, after giving an opportunity to show cause, suspend or cancel the registration certificate.
  - *Provided* that a person aggrieved by the order may appeal to the Central Government within thirty days.

### Rule 17: Import of cosmetics already registered for import
(1) A cosmetic manufactured in a foreign site and already registered under rule 13 for import and sale in India, may be imported by any person or entity by making an application in **Form COS-4**.
(2) The Central Licensing Authority may grant import registration number in **Form COS-4A**, or reject the application within six months.
(3) An import registration number granted under sub-rule (2) shall remain valid for a period of three years from the date of its issue.
(4) If the importer fails to comply with any of the conditions, the Central Licensing Authority may suspend or cancel the import registration number.

### Rule 18: Prohibition of import of certain cosmetic
(1) No cosmetic, the manufacture, sale or distribution of which is prohibited in the country of origin, shall be imported under the same name or under any other name except for the purpose of examination, test or analysis.
(2) No cosmetics shall be imported unless the "Use Before or use by" date shown on the label is later than six months from the date of import.
(3) No cosmetic containing hexachlorophene shall be imported.
(4) No cosmetic that has been tested on animals after the 12th day of November 2014 shall be imported into the country.

### Rule 19: Documents to be supplied to the Commissioner of Customs
Before any cosmetics are imported, a declaration signed by manufacturer or importer that the cosmetics comply with the provisions of Chapter III of the Act and the rules made thereunder, shall be supplied to the Commissioner of Customs.

### Rule 20: Procedure for import of cosmetics
(1) If the officer appointed at the port of entry has reason to believe that any cosmetic contravenes any provisions, he may take sample of the cosmetic from the consignment for inspection.
(2) If defects are noticed, the officer shall advise the Commissioner of Customs about further action.
(3) If the suspected contravention requires a test, the officer shall send the sample to the Laboratory. The consignment shall be detained till the test report is received.
  - *Provided* that if the importer gives an undertaking not to dispose of the cosmetic without the consent of the Commissioner of Customs, the consignment may be made over to the importer.
(4) If required to return the consignment, the importer shall do so within ten days of receipt of the notice.
(5) If the Director of the Laboratory reports that the sample contravenes provisions and cannot be remedied, the Commissioner of Customs shall communicate the report to the importer who shall within two months send back all the cosmetic to the country of manufacture or hand it over to the Central Government for destruction.
(6) If the contravention can be remedied, the Commissioner of Customs shall permit the importer to import the cosmetic on giving an undertaking not to dispose of it without permission.

### Rule 21: Import of cosmetic for personal use
Small quantities of cosmetics may be imported for personal use subject to:
  - (i) The cosmetics shall form part of a passenger's baggage and shall be the property of and intended for the bonafide use of the passenger; and
  - (ii) The cosmetics shall be declared to the Customs authorities, if they so direct.

### Rule 22: Import through points of entry
No cosmetic shall be imported into India except through the points of entry as specified in rule 43A of the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945.

## CHAPTER IV: MANUFACTURE OF COSMETICS FOR SALE OR DISTRIBUTION

### Rule 23: Application for grant of license or loan license
(1) Any person who intends to manufacture cosmetics shall make an application for grant of a licence or loan licence to manufacture for sale or for distribution to the state Licensing Authority.
(2) Application shall be made through an identified online portal in **Form COS-5** for licence or in **Form COS-6** for loan licence accompanied with a fee as specified in the Third Schedule.
(3) In case of a new cosmetic, the applicant shall obtain prior permission in **Form COS-3** from the Central Licensing Authority.
(4) The applicant shall furnish a self-declaration in **Form COS-7** conforming compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices, requirements of premises, plants and equipment as specified in the Seventh Schedule.
(5) The State Licensing Authority shall grant a licence or loan licence within forty-five days from the date of application after scrutiny.
(6) On receipt of the licence, the applicant may manufacture cosmetics for sale or distribution, after uploading a copy on the website of the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation.
(7) The State Licensing Authority shall inspect the site within thirty days from the date of grant of licence to verify the information given in self-certificate.
(8) If the State Licensing Authority fails to inspect within the period, the licence shall be deemed to be valid for all purposes.
(9) If false information is found, the Licensing Authority may cancel the licence after giving an opportunity to show cause.
(10) In case of defacement, damage, or loss, a duplicate copy may be requested on payment of fee.

### Rule 24: Manufacture at more than one premises
If cosmetics are manufactured at more than one premises, a separate application for each of such premises shall be made and a separate license obtained for each such premises.

### Rule 25: Form of license or loan licence
A license or loan licence to manufacture cosmetics for sale or distribution shall be granted in **Form COS-8** and loan licence in **Form COS-9**.

### Rule 26: Conditions of license or loan licence
A license in Form COS-8 shall be subject to the following conditions:
- (a) Manufacture shall be conducted under the direction and personal supervision of competent technical staff consisting at least one person who is a whole-time employee and who possesses qualifications like Diploma in Pharmacy, registered Pharmacist, Intermediate with Chemistry, or Bachelor degree in Cosmetic Technology.
- (b) The factory premises shall comply with the requirements specified in the Seventh Schedule.
- (c) The manufacturer shall provide and maintain adequate staff, premises and laboratory equipment for testing, OR make arrangements with an approved laboratory.
- (d) The applicant shall maintain documentary evidence for ownership, constitution of the firm, etc.
- (e) The licensee shall comply with the provisions of the Act and rules.
- (f) The licensee shall keep record of each batch of cosmetic manufactured and raw materials used as per Eighth Schedule. Records shall be retained for three years.
- (g) A license in Form COS-9 shall be deemed cancelled if the license in Form COS-8 is cancelled.
- (h) The licensee shall test each batch of raw materials and final product and maintain records for three years.
- (i) The licensee shall allow an Inspector to enter and inspect the premises and take samples (Form COS-10).
- (j) The licensee shall allow an Inspector to inspect all registers and records.
- (k) The licensee shall maintain an Inspection book in **Form COS-11**.
- (l) The licensee shall inform the Licensing Authority in writing of any change in the constitution of the firm. The current license shall be valid for a maximum of six months from the date of change.
- (m) In case of change in name or address of a manufacturer, an application for amendment shall be made within sixty days.
  - *Provided* that clauses (c) and (d) shall not apply to the manufacture of soap.

### Rule 27: Grant or refusal of license
(1) If the Licensing Authority is satisfied, he shall grant a license in **Form COS-8** or **Form COS-9**.
(2) If not satisfied, he shall reject the application and inform the applicant of the reasons.

### Rule 28: Further application after rejection
If within six months from rejection, the applicant informs the State Licensing Authority that requirements have been fulfilled and deposits the fee, the Authority may issue the license.

### Rule 29: Appeal to the State Government
Any person aggrieved by an order refusing to grant a license may appeal to the State Government within ninety days.

### Rule 30: Validity of licence
(1) A licence issued in Form COS-8 or Form COS-9 shall remain valid in perpetuity, subject to payment of retention fee before completion of five years.
(2) If the licensee fails to pay the retention fee, a late fee at 2% per month up to six months shall be paid.

### Rule 31: Inspection for verification of compliance
(1) The premises licensed for manufacturing cosmetics shall be inspected by Inspector appointed by the Central Government and State Government not less than once in three years.
(2) The inspectors appointed by the Central Government shall carry out special inspection of identified manufacturing facilities.

## CHAPTER V: PERMISSION FOR IMPORT OR MANUFACTURE OF NEW COSMETIC

### Rule 32: Permission for import or manufacture of new cosmetic
(1) Any person who intends to import or manufacture a new cosmetic, shall apply to the Central Licensing Authority in **Form COS-12** along with requisite fee and data on safety and effectiveness.
(2) If satisfied, the Central Licensing Authority may issue a prior permission in **Form COS-3**.
(3) The prior permission obtained in Form COS-3 shall be furnished along with the application for import or manufacture.
(4) Methods of test or analysis to be employed for safety evaluation shall comply with IS 4011: 2018.

## CHAPTER VI: LABELLING, PACKING AND STANDARDS FOR SALE OR DISTRIBUTION

### Rule 33: Prohibition of sale or distribution
No person shall sell or distribute any cosmetic unless the cosmetic, if of Indian origin, is manufactured by a licensed manufacturer and labelled and packed in accordance with these rules.

### Rule 34: Manner of labelling
(1) A cosmetic shall carry on both the inner and outer labels:
  - (a) the name of the cosmetics;
  - (b) the name of the manufacturer and complete address of the premises;
  - (c) use before or date of expiry (month and year).
(2) A distinctive batch number (preceded by "B" or "Batch No.") and Manufacturing licence number (preceded by 'M' or 'M. L. No') shall be carried on the inner or outer labels.
(3) The outer label shall carry a declaration of the net contents.
(4) Where a hazard exists, every inner label shall clearly indicate safe use directions, warnings, and names of hazardous ingredients.
(5) Imported cosmetics shall mention import registration certificate number preceded by "RC".
(6) Where a package has only one label, it shall contain all information.
(7) The list of ingredients present in concentration of more than one percent shall be listed in descending order.
(8) The cosmetic shall comply with labelling requirements specified in the Ninth Schedule.
(9) No cosmetic shall be imported unless packed and labelled in conformity with these rules.
(10) For export, labels shall meet the requirements of the destination country, but must include name, batch number, expiry date, manufacturer details, and licence number.

### Rule 35: Prohibition against altering inscription
No person shall alter, obliterate or deface any inscription or mark made by the manufacturer on the container, label or wrapper.

### Rule 36: Prohibition against false or misleading claims
No cosmetic may purport or claim to convey any idea which is false or misleading.

### Rule 37: Labelling of hair dyes
Hair dyes containing Paraphenyleneamine or other dyes shall be labelled with a caution in English and local languages: "Caution.- This product contains ingredients which may cause skin irritation... This product should not be used for dyeing the eyelashes or eyebrows; as such a use may cause blindness." Instructions for preliminary testing must be included.

### Rule 38: Special provisions relating to toothpaste containing fluoride
(i) Fluoride content shall not be more than 1000 ppm and shall be mentioned on the tube and carton.
(ii) Date of expiry should be mentioned on tube and carton.

### Rule 39: Standards of cosmetics
(1) No cosmetic shall be imported or manufactured unless it complies with the specifications prescribed under the Ninth Schedule.
(2) Raw materials specified in ANNEX A of IS: 4707 Part 2 shall not be added.
(3) No Cosmetic shall contain Dyes, Colours and Pigments other than those specified by BIS (IS: 4707 Part 1 or Part 2).
(4) No cosmetic containing hexachlorophene shall be manufactured (except soaps in concentrations not exceeding 1% w/w).
(5) Mercury limits: Eye area cosmetics - not exceeding 70 ppm; Other products - unintentional mercury not exceeding 1 ppm.
(6) Use of lead and arsenic compounds for colouring is prohibited.
(7) No person shall use any animal for testing of cosmetics.

## CHAPTER VII: PROCEDURE OF SAMPLING FOR TEST OR ANALYSIS, SEIZURE AND REPORT

### Rule 40: Testing of Cosmetic from a purchaser
An application from a purchaser for test or analysis under section 26 of the Act shall be made to the Government Analyst in **Form COS-13** and the report shall be supplied in **Form COS-14**.

### Rule 41: Procedure for sampling
The Inspector while exercising powers under section 22 of the Act shall follow the procedure provided in section 23 of the said Act.

### Rule 42: Forms of receipts for seized cosmetic
A receipt for the stock of any cosmetic or for any record, register, document or any other material object seized shall be given in **Form COS-15**.

### Rule 43: Manner of certifying copies of seized documents
The Inspector shall return the documents seized within a period of twenty days.

### Rule 44: Form of intimation of purpose of taking samples
(1) When an Inspector takes a sample, he shall intimate such purpose in writing in **Form COS-10**.
(2) If the fair price tendered is refused, the Inspector shall record the fact in **Form COS-16**.

### Rule 45: Procedure for dispatch of sample to Government Analyst
(1) The portion of the sample shall be sent by registered post or by hand in a sealed packet, enclosed with a memorandum in **Form COS-17**.
(2) A copy of the memorandum and a specimen impression of the seal shall be sent separately.

### Rule 46: Form of order not to dispose of stock
An order in writing by an Inspector requiring a person not to dispose of any stock shall be in **Form COS-18**.

### Rule 47: Prohibition of sale
No person in possession of a cosmetic in respect of which an order has been made shall sell or otherwise dispose of any stock.

### Rule 48: Procedure on receipt of sample
On receipt of a package, the Government Analyst shall compare the seals and note the condition. After analysis, he shall supply a report in triplicate in **Form COS-19** with full protocols.

### Rule 49: Dispatch of samples for test or analysis by order of court
Samples shall be sent by registered post or courier in a sealed packet, enclosed with a memorandum in **Form COS-20**.

### Rule 50: Recording of condition of seals
The packet shall be opened by an authorised officer who shall record the condition of the seal.

### Rule 51: Report of result of test or analysis
The result shall be supplied immediately after completion to the sender in **Form COS-21**.

### Rule 52: Issuance of certificates
Certificates issued by the Laboratory shall be signed by the Director or an authorised officer.

### Rule 53: Confiscation of cosmetics, implements, machinery etc.
(1) Where any person has been convicted for contravening provisions, the stock of cosmetics shall be liable to confiscation.
(2) Machinery, packages, vehicles, etc., used in the manufacture of misbranded or adulterated cosmetics shall also be liable to confiscation.

### Rule 54: Procedure for disposal of confiscated cosmetics
(1) The Court shall refer the confiscated cosmetics to the Inspector for report.
(2) If found not of standard quality, the Court shall order destruction under the supervision of the Inspector.
(3) If found of standard quality, the Inspector shall report to the Court accordingly.

## CHAPTER VIII: APPROVAL OF LABORATORY FOR CARRYING OUT TESTS ON COSMETICS AND THEIR RAW MATERIALS

### Rule 55: Application for grant of approval for testing cosmetics
(1) Any laboratory that meets the requirements and has been accredited by National Accreditation Body may apply in **Form COS-22** to the State Licensing Authority.
(2) A separate application shall be made for additional categories.
(3) The application shall be accompanied with the fee as specified in the Third Schedule.
(4) Fee for duplicate copy of approval shall be as specified in the Third Schedule.

### Rule 56: Approval for carrying out tests on cosmetics on behalf of licensees
(1) Approval shall be granted in **Form COS-23**.
(2) Conditions include well-lighted premises, adequate space, suitable equipment, and qualified staff.

### Rule 57: Inspection before grant of approval
(1) The State Licensing Authority shall cause the institution to be inspected jointly by Inspectors.
(2) The report of the inspection shall be rendered within twenty-four hours.

### Rule 58: Procedure of approving authority
(1) If satisfied, the State Licensing Authority shall grant an approval in **Form COS-23**.
(2) If not satisfied, the application shall be rejected with reasons.

### Rule 59: Further application after rejection
If within six months the applicant informs that conditions have been satisfied and deposits the fee, the Authority may grant the approval.

### Rule 60: Validity of licence
(1) A licence issued in Form COS-23 shall remain valid if the licensee deposits a retention fee before completion of five years.
(2) The retention fee shall be equivalent to the grant fee.
(3) Late fee at 2% per month up to six months shall be paid.

### Rule 61: Inspection for verification of compliance
The premises licensed shall be inspected jointly by Inspectors at least once in three years.

### Rule 62: General conditions after approval
Conditions include providing adequate staff, premises, and equipment as specified in the Eleventh Schedule, maintaining records, allowing Inspectors, and reporting changes.

### Rule 63: Withdrawal and suspension of approval
(1) The State Licensing Authority may withdraw or suspend an approval if the institution fails to comply.
(2) Appeal may be made to the State Government within three months.

## CHAPTER IX: MISCELLANEOUS

### Rule 64: Exemption of cosmetics
Cosmetics specified in the Twelfth Schedule shall be exempted from the provisions of Chapter III and Chapter IV.

### Rule 65: Voluntary recall of cosmetics
(1) If a manufacturer or authorised agent considers a cosmetic unsafe, they shall immediately initiate procedures to withdraw it from the market and inform the Authorities.
(2) They shall cooperate with the Authorities to prevent risks.

### Rule 66: Fees for examination of samples
(1) The fee for test and analysis of samples under section 25(1) shall be as specified in the Fifth Schedule.
(2) The fees for test or analysis under section 26 shall be as specified in the Fifth Schedule.

### Rule 67: Mode of payment of fee
(1) Fees to the Central Licensing Authority shall be paid through challan or electronic mode in Bank of Baroda.
(2) Fees to the State Licensing Authority shall be paid as specified by the State Government.

### Rule 68: Applicability in case of inconsistency
If there is any inconsistency between these rules and any other rules, the provisions of these rules shall prevail.

### Rule 69: Debarment of applicant
(1) If an applicant submits misleading or fake documents, they may be debarred by the Licensing Authority.
(2) Appeal may be made to the Central or State Government within thirty days.

### Rule 70: Digitalisation of Form
The Forms may be suitably modified by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation for digitalisation.

### Rule 71: Amendments in the Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945
The Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945 is amended as specified in the Thirteenth Schedule.

### Rule 72: Savings
(1) Approvals, permissions, licenses or certificates issued prior to commencement of these rules shall be deemed valid for a period of eighteen months.
(2) Any action taken under the 1945 Rules shall be deemed to have been taken under these rules.
