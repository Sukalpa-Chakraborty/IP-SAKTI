# Guidelines for Designated Repositories under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002

## 1. Document Overview

This document provides guidelines for Designated Repositories (DRs) under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002. It outlines the general and specific mandates for DRs, the categories of biological resources they receive, focal points, accession numbering, deposit requirements, ownership, procedures, monitoring, and resource support. It also includes formats for passport data and acknowledgement receipts.

The guidelines are issued in accordance with Sections 39(1-3) and 19(1) of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, read with Rule 14(6)(viii) of the Biological Diversity Rules, 2004, and clause 4(6) of the Regulation on ABS Guidelines notified in November 2014.

## 2. General Mandate for All Designated Repositories

<!-- chunk_id: dr-guidelines-general-mandate -->

To act as **Designated Repository (DR)** for safe deposit of:
- **Holotypes/isotypes/paratypes** of new taxa discovered in India.
- **Samples of biological resources** accessed by foreign citizens/entities for research or sent/carried abroad by Indian citizens/institutions for research.

Legal basis:
- Sections 39(1-3) and 19(1) of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002.
- Rule 14(6)(viii) of the Biological Diversity Rules, 2004.
- Clause 4(6) of the Regulation on ABS Guidelines notified in November 2014.

## 4. Focal Points for the Designated Repositories

<!-- chunk_id: dr-guidelines-focal-points -->

- The **Head of the facility** where biological resources are deposited and conserved shall be the **Scientist-Incharge** of the DR and shall serve as the **Focal Point for the NBA**.
- Each DR is required to designate the Focal Point for this purpose.

## 5. Allocation of Unique Accession Number to the Deposits

<!-- chunk_id: dr-guidelines-accession-number -->

- Done by the concerned **Head of the Repository**.
- The Head decides after the **5-year custody period** whether:
  - Sample will be added to the national collection for long-term storage; or
  - Discarded if the sample was of common occurrence (e.g., procured from local market) or appeared to be a duplicate of existing accessions.
- Wherever required, the **NBA will intimate the repository** to extend the period beyond five years in specific cases.
- Wherever any **new taxon** is deposited, it should be preserved for **indefinite period**.

## 6. Who are Required to Deposit Types/Specimens/Samples of Biological Resources in DRs?

<!-- chunk_id: dr-guidelines-who-deposits -->

(i) Any person who discovers a **new taxon** of biological resources occurring in India is required to notify it to the relevant DR and deposit its **holotype/isotype/paratype** there [Section 39(3) of the BD Act].

(ii) Persons/entities as identified u/s 3(2) accessing biological resources occurring in India [Sub-rule 6(viii) of Rule 14 of the Biological Diversity Rules].

(iii) Indian citizens/entities taking/sending abroad biological resource for non-commercial research by applying in **Form B** (Sub-regulation 1 of Regulation 13 of the ABS Guidelines notified in November 2014).

## 7. Where the Voucher Specimen/Reference Sample may be Deposited?

<!-- chunk_id: dr-guidelines-where-deposit -->

Based on the type of biological resource, the voucher specimen/reference sample may be deposited in DRs as notified by Ministry of Environment, Forests and Climate Change vide notifications:
- F.No. 26-14/2007-CSC, dated August 28, 2008
- F.No. 26-15/2007-CSC, dated September 12, 2012

## 8. Ownership of the Deposits and Duration of Safekeeping

<!-- chunk_id: dr-guidelines-ownership -->

- Under the Biological Diversity Act 2002, the **Government of India has sovereign rights** over biological resources occurring in or obtained from India.
- Deposited biological resources **belong to the State**; the Designated Repository acts as their **custodian** mandated for preserving/conserving them for **five years or beyond** as required by the NBA, under appropriate storage conditions from the date of deposition.
- If any **new taxon** is deposited, it should be preserved for **indefinite period**.
- During this period, deposited biological resources will be made available for **research/academic purposes within the country**.
- Researchers covered under the category of applicants u/s 3(2) of the Act shall be required to obtain **prior approval from NBA** for accessing the deposited materials.

## 9. Procedure for Submission of Biological Resources to DRs

<!-- chunk_id: dr-guidelines-submission-procedure -->

- All DRs will develop **detailed standard procedures** depending upon the type of biological resources deposited with them.
- All applicants/depositors will be required to deposit the biological resources in the relevant DR along with **passport data/information** as prescribed in **Annexure I**.
- This may be modified by the concerned DR, as required for the kind of biological resource to be deposited, with **prior approval of NBA**.
- Details of such procedure to deposit the biological resource would be displayed at the **official website of every DR** and linked with the **NBA website**.

## 10. Issue of Acknowledgement/Receipt

<!-- chunk_id: dr-guidelines-acknowledgement -->

- On depositing the voucher specimen in appropriate form, all applicants will be issued an **acknowledgement (Annexure II)** by the **Scientist-Incharge of DR** in reasonable period after satisfactory deposition of voucher specimen/reference sample.

## 11. Monitoring

<!-- chunk_id: dr-guidelines-monitoring -->

- The **Director/Head of the organization** where DR is located will be the **Competent Authority** to monitor the maintenance of the deposited biological resources from time to time.
- These **periodic reports** will be sent by him to the **NBA Secretariat** for information and follow up.

## 12. Resource Support to DRs on Request

<!-- chunk_id: dr-guidelines-resource-support -->

- Depending upon the work load of conserving the deposited materials and maintaining their database, **contractual technical manpower** may be provided to a DR, where justified, with appropriate financial support by NBA, if necessary.

## 15. RAG Q&A Extraction

### Q: What is the legal basis for these guidelines?
**A:** Sections 39(1-3) and 19(1) of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, read with Rule 14(6)(viii) of the Biological Diversity Rules, 2004, and clause 4(6) of the Regulation on ABS Guidelines notified in November 2014.

### Q: What is the general mandate of a Designated Repository?
**A:** To act as a safe deposit for holotypes/isotypes/paratypes of new taxa discovered in India and samples of biological resources accessed by foreign citizens/entities for research or sent abroad by Indian citizens/institutions for research.

### Q: Who must deposit biological resources in a DR?
**A:** (i) Any person who discovers a new taxon of biological resources in India; (ii) Persons/entities accessing biological resources under Section 3(2); (iii) Indian citizens/entities taking/sending biological resources abroad for non-commercial research.

### Q: What is the duration for which deposited biological resources are preserved?
**A:** Five years or beyond as required by the NBA; for new taxa, indefinite period.

### Q: Who owns the deposited biological resources?
**A:** The Government of India has sovereign rights; deposited resources belong to the State, with the DR acting as custodian.

### Q: What is the procedure for depositing biological resources?
**A:** DRs develop standard procedures; depositors submit resources with passport data as per Annexure I; details are displayed on DR and NBA websites.

### Q: What is issued after deposition?
**A:** An acknowledgement (Annexure II) is issued by the Scientist-Incharge of the DR.

### Q: Who monitors the maintenance of deposited resources?
**A:** The Director/Head of the organization where the DR is located acts as the Competent Authority and sends periodic reports to the NBA Secretariat.

### Q: Can DRs receive resource support?
**A:** Yes, contractual technical manpower and financial support may be provided by NBA, if justified.

### Q: What information is required in the passport data?
**A:** Name and address of depositor, NBA reference number, common and scientific names, site and date of collection, nature of specimen, description and quantity, host information for parasitic/symbiotic organisms, herbarium specimen and photograph for plant parts, and any other relevant information.
