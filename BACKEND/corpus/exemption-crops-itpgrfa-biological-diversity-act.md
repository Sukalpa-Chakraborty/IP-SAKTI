# Notification: Exemption of Crops Listed in Annex I of the ITPGRFA under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002

## 1. Document Overview

This is a Gazette notification from the **Ministry of Environment, Forests and Climate Change**, Government of India, dated **17th December 2014** (S.O. 3232(E)). It declares that the **Department of Agriculture and Cooperation** may specify crops from **Annex I of the International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture (ITPGRFA)** and exempt them from **Sections 3 and 4 of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002**, for the purpose of utilization and conservation for research, breeding, and training for food and agriculture.

The notification is issued in exercise of powers conferred by **Section 40** of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, and in fulfilment of India's obligations under the ITPGRFA.

## 2. Key Provisions

### Preamble

- India is a party to the **ITPGRFA**, signed and ratified on **10th June 2002**.
- The objectives of ITPGRFA are conservation and sustainable use of plant genetic resources for food and agriculture, and fair and equitable sharing of benefits.
- **Article 12** of ITPGRFA provides for facilitated access to plant genetic resources under the **Multilateral System**.
- The **Nagoya Protocol** on Access to Genetic Resources and the Fair and Equitable Sharing of Benefits (29th October 2010) is the instrument for implementing access and benefit-sharing provisions of the Convention on Biological Diversity.
- **Article 4** of the Nagoya Protocol provides that the Protocol does not apply to parties to a specialized instrument in respect of specific genetic resources covered by that instrument.
- **Section 40** of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 empowers the Central Government to exempt certain biological resources from the provisions of the Act.

### Notification

In exercise of powers under **Section 40** of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, and in fulfilment of India's obligations to ITPGRFA, the Central Government, in consultation with the **National Biodiversity Authority**, declares that:

- The **Department of Agriculture and Cooperation** may, from time to time, specify such crops as it considers necessary from amongst the crops listed in **Annex I of the ITPGRFA**, being food crops and forages covered under the Multilateral System.
- Accordingly, these crops are **exempted from Sections 3 and 4 of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002**, for the purpose of utilization and conservation for **research, breeding, and training for food and agriculture**.

**Provided that** such purposes shall **not include** chemical, pharmaceutical, and/or other non-food or feed industrial uses.

### Follow-up

- The Department of Agriculture and Cooperation shall keep the **National Biodiversity Authority** informed of all crops as may be specified by it from time to time, for providing access to plant genetic resources for food and agriculture under the ITPGRFA for the purposes aforesaid.

## 5. RAG Q&A Extraction

### Q: What is the purpose of this notification?
**A:** To exempt crops listed in Annex I of the ITPGRFA from Sections 3 and 4 of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, for research, breeding, and training for food and agriculture.

### Q: Which authority may specify the crops?
**A:** The Department of Agriculture and Cooperation.

### Q: What is the legal basis for this exemption?
**A:** Section 40 of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, which empowers the Central Government to exempt certain biological resources.

### Q: What is the ITPGRFA?
**A:** The International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture, signed and ratified by India on 10th June 2002.

### Q: What is the Multilateral System?
**A:** A system under Article 12 of the ITPGRFA that provides facilitated access to plant genetic resources for food and agriculture.

### Q: What purposes are covered by the exemption?
**A:** Utilization and conservation for research, breeding, and training for food and agriculture.

### Q: What purposes are excluded?
**A:** Chemical, pharmaceutical, and/or other non-food or feed industrial uses.

### Q: Which body must be kept informed?
**A:** The National Biodiversity Authority.

### Q: What is the date of the notification?
**A:** 17th December 2014.

### Q: What is the Gazette reference?
**A:** S.O. 3232(E), published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II—Section 3—Sub-section (ii).
