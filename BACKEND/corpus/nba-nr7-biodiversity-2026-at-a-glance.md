\# India's Seventh National Report (NR-7) on Biodiversity — 2026 At a Glance



\## 1. Document Overview



NR-7 presents India's progress in implementing the \*\*Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD)\*\*, India's \*\*Updated National Biodiversity Strategy and Action Plan (NBSAP, 2024–2030)\*\*, and the \*\*23 National Biodiversity Targets (NBTs)\*\* aligned with the \*\*Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (KM-GBF)\*\*.



India has been a Party to the CBD since 1994 and is one of the world's 17 megadiverse countries. It houses nearly 8% of the world's recorded species on 2.4% of global land area, spanning 4 global biodiversity hotspots, 10 biogeographic zones, and 27 biotic provinces.



NR-7 was submitted to the CBD on \*\*26 February 2026\*\*, ahead of the 28 February 2026 deadline. It is India's first report under the KM-GBF and tracks progress through \*\*142 national indicators\*\*.

\## 3. Call to Action



India's focus turns to sustained implementation of the \*\*Updated NBSAP (2024–2030)\*\*. Invitation to government agencies, State/UT authorities, research institutions, industry, civil society, and citizens to:



\- \*\*Contribute data:\*\* Feed field-level and citizen-science data into the National Biodiversity Monitoring System (NBMS).

\- \*\*Strengthen monitoring:\*\* Support regular assessment against the 142 national indicators.

\- \*\*Restore \& Protect:\*\* Scale up ecosystem restoration, PA management, and OECM identification.

\- \*\*Mainstream biodiversity:\*\* Embed NBTs into sectoral policy, budgets, and business disclosures.

\- \*\*Invest \& share benefits:\*\* Expand green finance and equitable ABS implementation.



\## 4. Continuing Priorities



\- Strengthen biodiversity monitoring \& regular assessments.

\- Address habitat degradation, pollution \& invasive alien species.

\- Improve human, financial \& technological capacity.

\- Strengthen long-term ecosystem research \& evidence.

\- Scale up restoration, sustainable use, benefit-sharing \& finance.



\## 5. NBSAP \& NR-7 Report



\- \*\*India's Updated National Biodiversity Strategy and Action Plan 2024–2030:\*\* A roadmap for conservation of India's biodiversity.

\- \*\*India's National Report on Biodiversity 2026:\*\* Government of India, Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, National Biodiversity Authority.



\## 6. Objectives of NR-7 Preparation



\- \*\*Assess Progress:\*\* Evaluate advancement on all 23 NBTs using the 142-indicator KM-GBF-aligned monitoring framework.

\- \*\*Demonstrate Global Leadership:\*\* Highlight India's contribution to KM-GBF's four long-term goals — ecosystem integrity, sustainable use, equitable benefit-sharing, and closing the biodiversity finance gap.

\- \*\*Promote Transparency:\*\* Present a data-driven, evidence-based account of outcomes, challenges, and strategies.

\- \*\*Engage Stakeholders:\*\* Reflect a "whole-of-government" and "whole-of-society" approach, spanning 30+ ministries, State Biodiversity Boards (SBBs), Union Territory Biodiversity Councils (UTBCs), Biodiversity Management Committees (BMCs), NGOs, academia, and the private sector.

\- \*\*Chart the Path Forward:\*\* Identify gaps and solutions to sustain progress toward 2030 and the 2050 Vision of "Living in Harmony with Nature".

\## 8. Review, Validation \& Submission



\- Led by the National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) under MoEFCC, with Wildlife Institute of India (WII) as technical partner and UNDP-India support under the GEF-8 Umbrella Programme.

\- Nationwide consultations across 30+ central ministries/departments, SBBs, UTBCs, BMCs, research institutions, and the private sector.

\- Data sources: primary reporting from central ministries/departments; secondary data from BSI, ZSI, ICAR, FSI, etc. (2020–2025).

\- Technology-enabled monitoring: remote sensing, satellite telemetry, DNA barcoding, and eDNA.

\- Draft review by Expert/Core Group and NR-7 Working Group.

\- National Consultation and Validation Workshop and finalisation.

\- NR-7 submitted to CBD on 26 February 2026.
