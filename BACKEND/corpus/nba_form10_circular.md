# National Biodiversity Authority Circular: Form-10 Declaration

## Document Summary
This circular from the National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) clarifies the commencement, scope, and applicability date for submitting a Declaration in Form-10 under the Biological Diversity Rules, 2024[cite: 1]. It outlines the statutory obligations for individuals or entities intending to use foreign biological resources or associated traditional knowledge within India[cite: 1]. 

## Key Dates and Applicability
*   **Notification Date**: The Biological Diversity Rules, 2024 were notified in the Official Gazette on October 22, 2024[cite: 1].
*   **Effective Date**: The rules came into force upon the expiry of sixty days from the date of notification, which is December 21, 2024[cite: 1].
*   **Statutory Obligation**: The requirement under Rule 18(1) to submit Form-10 became operative starting December 21, 2024[cite: 1].
*   **Applicability Timeline**: The mandate applies strictly to cases where access to foreign biological resources, or the intent to use them in India, arises on or after December 21, 2024[cite: 1].

## Scope of Rule 18(1)
Any person must submit a Declaration in Form-10 on the NBA web portal if they intend to use foreign biological resources or associated traditional knowledge obtained from a foreign country in India for the following purposes[cite: 1]:
*   Research[cite: 1].
*   Commercial utilisation[cite: 1].
*   Applying for or obtaining any form of Intellectual Property Rights (IPR)[cite: 1].

Submission is mandatory in the following circumstances occurring on or after December 21, 2024[cite: 1]:
*   Commencement of the use of foreign biological resources or associated traditional knowledge for research, commercial utilisation, or IPR-related activities in India[cite: 1].
*   Filing of any application for Intellectual Property Rights in India based on such foreign biological resources[cite: 1].

## Purpose and Regulatory Authority
*   **Regulatory Goal**: The declaration mechanism under Rule 18 is designed to enable the NBA to monitor and regulate the use of foreign biological resources within India[cite: 1].
*   **Legal Compliance**: It ensures compliance with the provisions of the Biological Diversity (Amendment) Act, 2023[cite: 1].
*   **Issuance**: Issued by B. Balaji, Member Secretary, NBA, with the approval of the competent authority on February 4, 2026[cite: 1].
