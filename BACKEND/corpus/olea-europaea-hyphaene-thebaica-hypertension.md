# Antihypertensive Effects of Olea europaea and Hyphaene thebaica (Ifanosine)

## 1. Document Overview

This is an abstract/summary of a journal article in **Clinical Research** (Evidence Grade B), Body System: Circulatory, Disease: Hypertension Uncomplicated (ICPC-K86 | ICD-110).

## 2. Abstract

<!-- chunk_id: olea-hyphaene-abs-001 -->

**Background:** *Olea europaea* L. and *Hyphaene thebaica* L. are used by traditional healers in Africa for treating and preventing hypertension, individually or in a polyherbal preparation (Ifanosine).

**Aim:** Primary: assess antihypertensive effects of *Olea europaea* leaves aqueous extract (OEL), *Hyphaene thebaica* mesocarp extract (HT), and Ifanosine on isolated rat aorta rings. Secondary: evaluate clinical benefits of a new oral formulation of Ifanosine.

**Materials and Methods:** In vitro studies using isometric transducer on rat aorta; Using chambers technique for intestinal bioavailability; HPLC for phytochemical composition; Subchronic toxicity in rats (5 g/kg for 28 days); Clinical observational study with 32 hypertensive patients receiving fixed oral dose of 3.5 mg three times a day for 30 days.

**Results:** Aqueous extracts induced dose-dependent relaxation of rat aorta rings. IC50 values: HT = 44.76 ± 1.35 ng/mL; OEL = 58.67 ± 1.02 ng/mL; Ifanosine = 29.46 ± 0.26 ng/mL. pA2 values: OEL = 1, HT = 0.6, Ifanosine = 0.06. Intestinal bioavailability: Prazosin > plant extracts. Toxicological studies demonstrated safety of Ifanosine. Biochemical analyses indicated flavonoids and phenolic acids as dominant active constituents. Clinical benefits: reduced SBP, DBP, LDL-c, VLDL-c, TAG; increased HDL-c; no overt adverse effects.

**Conclusion:** Study validates traditional use of OEL and HT for hypertension and advocates for alternative and combinatorial polyphytoherapy (ACP) to enhance traditional remedies.
