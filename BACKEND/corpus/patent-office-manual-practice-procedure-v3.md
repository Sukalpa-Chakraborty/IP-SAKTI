# Manual of Patent Office Practice and Procedure, Version 3.0

## 1. Document Overview

This is the **Manual of Patent Office Practice and Procedure, Version 3.0**, published by the **Office of the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trademarks (CGPDTM)**, Mumbai, on **26th November 2019**. It provides a comprehensive procedural guide for the prosecution of patent applications in India, covering filing, publication, examination, grant, opposition, post-grant procedures, compulsory licensing, patent agents, and time limits. It reflects the Patents Act, 1970, as amended in 1999, 2002, and 2005, and the Patents Rules, 2003, as amended up to 2019. The Manual is intended as a practical guide; it does not have the force of law.

## 2. Key Concepts and Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Appropriate Office** | Patent Office with territorial jurisdiction (Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai) as per Rule 4. |
| **Assignee** | Includes assignee of assignee and legal representative of deceased assignee. |
| **Capable of Industrial Application** | Invention capable of being made or used in an industry. |
| **Controller** | Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks. |
| **Government Undertaking** | Industrial undertaking by Government department, corporation, Government company, or institution financed by Government. |
| **International Application** | Application for patent made under PCT. |
| **Invention** | New product or process involving inventive step and capable of industrial application. |
| **Inventive Step** | Feature involving technical advance or economic significance, not obvious to person skilled in art. |
| **Legal Representative** | Person who in law represents estate of deceased person. |
| **Opposition Board** | Board constituted under Section 25(3) for post-grant opposition. |
| **Patentee** | Person entered on register as grantee or proprietor of patent. |
| **Person Interested** | Includes person engaged in or promoting research in same field as invention. |
| **Small Entity** | Enterprise with investment in plant/machinery or equipment within MSME limits. |
| **Startup** | Entity recognised as startup by competent authority under Startup India initiative. |
| **True and First Inventor** | Excludes first importer or person to whom invention communicated from outside India. |

## 3. Chapter Summaries

### Chapter 1: Introduction

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch1-001 -->

- The Manual codifies practices and procedures of the Indian Patent Office.
- Indian Patent Office functions from four locations: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai.
- Processing is multi-stage: filing, data processing, screening, classification, publication, examination, hearing, pre-grant opposition, grant/refusal.
- Common procedures: evidence by affidavit, e-filing, latest document supersedes earlier, Form-26 power of attorney stamped.

### Chapter 2: Key Definitions

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch2-001 -->

- Definitions as per Patents Act and Rules: appropriate office, assignee, capable of industrial application, Controller, Government undertaking, international application, invention, inventive step, legal representative, Opposition Board, patentee, patent office, person, person interested, prescribed, prescribed manner, small entity, startup, true and first inventor.
- Detailed explanations for small entity and startup, including MSME limits and foreign entity declarations.

### Chapter 3: Filing of Patent Application

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch3-001 -->

- **Applicants**: True and first inventor, assignee, legal representative. Proof of right required within 6 months.
- **Jurisdiction**: Based on residence, place of business, place of origin, or address for service in India. Territorial jurisdiction of offices defined.
- **Types**: Ordinary, Convention, PCT National Phase, Divisional, Patent of Addition.
- **Filing**: Form-1, provisional/complete specification (Form-2), fee. Documents: proof of right, Form-3 (foreign filing), Form-5 (inventorship), Form-26 (power of attorney), Form-28 (small entity/startup), priority document (if applicable), NBA permission (if biological material from India).
- **E-filing**: Comprehensive portal, DSC, payment gateway.
- **Language and paper size**: A4, margins, numbering.
- **Sequence listing**: Electronic format, WIPO ST.25.
- **Fees**: Offline additional 10%, online modes, refunds.
- **Processing**: Numbering system (YYYYJTNNNNNN), scrutiny, secrecy directions, atomic energy, withdrawal.

### Chapter 4: Publication of Application

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch4-001 -->

- **Normal publication**: After 18 months from filing/priority.
- **Early publication**: Form-9, published within one month.
- **Particulars**: Application number, country, dates, title, IPC, priority, applicant, inventor, abstract, pages, claims.
- **Effects**: Specification, drawings, abstract available to public. Applicant gets provisional rights from publication. No patent granted before 6 months from publication.

### Chapter 5: Provisional and Complete Specification

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch5-001 -->

- **Provisional**: Secures priority date, must file complete within 12 months.
- **Complete**: Full description, best method, claims, abstract, drawings. Must disclose biological material source and deposit if needed.
- **Contents**: Title, field, use, prior art, problem, objects, summary, detailed description, best method, claims, abstract.
- **Drawings**: A4, margins, numbered, no descriptive matter.
- **Abstract**: ≤150 words, technical field, problem, solution, principal use.
- **Claims**: Define scope, clear, succinct, fairly based on description. Structure: preamble, transitional phrase, body. Independent and dependent claims. Unity of invention.
- **Priority date**: Based on provisional/complete filing, convention, divisional, etc.

### Chapter 6: Divisional Application and Patent of Addition

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch6-001 -->

- **Divisional**: Filed before grant, if claims relate to more than one invention. Same filing date, separate fee, examined vis-à-vis parent. Cannot add new matter.
- **Patent of Addition**: For improvement/modification of main invention. Same or later filing date. No renewal fee. Expires with main patent. Cannot be challenged for lack of inventive step over main.

### Chapter 7: Convention Application, International Application and National Phase Application

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch7-001 -->

- **Convention**: Within 12 months of basic application. Complete specification required. Priority document and translation if needed. Multiple priorities possible.
- **PCT International**: Indian applicant can file via RO/IN or RO/IB. Foreign filing permission under Section 39 required in certain cases. ISA/IPEA options.
- **PCT National Phase**: Within 31 months from priority. Form-1, complete specification (as filed internationally), translations if needed. Deletion of claims allowed. Request for examination within 48 months.

### Chapter 8: Indian Patent Office as ISA and IPEA

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch8-001 -->

- IPO functions as ISA/IPEA from 15th October 2013.
- Applicants from India and Iran can select IPO.
- ISA establishes ISR and written opinion; IPEA establishes IPER.
- Fees, time limits, unity of invention, protest procedures, refunds.

### Chapter 9: Examination and Grant

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch9-001 -->

- **Request for Examination**: Form-18 within 48 months. Expedited examination (Form-18A) for startups, small entities, female applicants, government, etc.
- **Examination**: Search for anticipation, novelty, inventive step, industrial applicability. Sections 3 and 4 exclusions. Sufficiency of disclosure, unity of invention.
- **Report**: Examiner reports to Controller. FER issued within one month. Response within 6 months (extendable by 3 months). Hearing if needed. Grant or refusal.
- **Pre-grant opposition**: Representation under Section 25(1) after publication, before grant. Considered with examiner's report. Hearing.
- **Grant**: When application in order, no refusal, no opposition pending. Date of grant, certificate, register entry.
- **Post-dating**: Up to 6 months from filing.
- **Rights**: Patentee exclusive rights, co-owners, term 20 years, renewal fees.

### Chapter 10: Post-grant Opposition

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch10-001 -->

- Notice of opposition within 1 year from publication of grant. Form-7, written statement, evidence.
- Opposition Board constituted (3 members). Report within 3 months.
- Patentee reply within 2 months. Opponent reply evidence within 1 month.
- Hearing, decision: revoke, amend, or refuse opposition.

### Chapter 11: Post-grant Procedures

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch11-001 -->

- **Renewal**: Fees due before expiry of 2nd year and each subsequent year. Extension up to 6 months.
- **Restoration**: Within 18 months of cessation. Form-15, evidence, publication, opposition.
- **Assignments**: Form-16, proof of title, registration.
- **Surrender**: Offer to surrender, publication, opposition, revocation.
- **Working of patents**: Form-27 annually within 3 months of calendar year end.
- **Amendments after grant**: Form-13, allowed by disclaimer, correction, explanation. Publication if substantive.
- **Register of Patents**: Electronic, public inspection.
- **Rectification**: By IPAB.

### Chapter 12: Appeals

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch12-001 -->

- Appeals to IPAB from decisions under specified sections (15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 25(4), 28, 51, 54, 57, 60, 61, 63, 66, 69(3), 78, 84(1)-(5), 85, 88, 91, 92, 94).
- Procedure: certified copy of decision, within 3 months, Controller's right to appear.

### Chapter 13: Revocation of Patent

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch13-001 -->

- **Before High Court/IPAB**: Grounds under Section 64.
- **By Controller on Central Government direction**: Atomic energy, public interest, non-working.
- **Revocation for non-working**: After compulsory licence, if invention not worked.

### Chapter 14: Compulsory Licensing

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch14-001 -->

- **Section 84**: After 3 years from grant, any person interested. Grounds: reasonable requirements not satisfied, not available at affordable price, not worked in India.
- **Section 92**: National emergency, extreme urgency, public non-commercial use.
- **Section 92A**: Export of patented pharmaceutical products to countries with insufficient manufacturing capacity.
- **Terms**: Royalty, non-exclusive, non-assignable, predominantly Indian market.
- **Termination**: Form-21, if circumstances no longer exist.

### Chapter 15: Use of Inventions for Purposes of Government

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch15-001 -->

- Government use free of royalty if recorded before priority date.
- Rights of third parties, acquisition by Central Government, reference to High Court.

### Chapter 16: Patent Agents

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch16-001 -->

- Registration, qualifications, disqualifications, rights, restrictions.
- Removal, restoration, alteration of names.

### Chapter 17: Offences and Penalties

<!-- chunk_id: pom-ch17-001 -->

### Chapter 19: General Services

### Chapter 20: Scientific Advisors

## 5. RAG Q&A Extraction

### Q: What is the objective of this Manual?
**A:** To codify patent office practices and procedures for efficient prosecution of patent applications in India.

### Q: What are the types of patent applications?
**A:** Ordinary, Convention, PCT National Phase, Divisional, Patent of Addition.

### Q: What is the time limit for filing a complete specification after provisional?
**A:** 12 months from the date of filing of provisional specification. Section 9(1).

### Q: What is the time limit for requesting examination?
**A:** 48 months from the date of filing or priority, whichever is earlier. Section 11B, Rule 24B.

### Q: What is the time limit for putting an application in order for grant?
**A:** 6 months from the date of issuance of FER, extendable by 3 months. Section 21(1), Rule 24B(5).

### Q: What is the term of a patent?
**A:** 20 years from the date of filing of the application. Section 53.

### Q: What are the grounds for compulsory licence?
**A:** Reasonable requirements of public not satisfied, invention not available at affordable price, invention not worked in India. Section 84.

### Q: What is the time limit for filing a post-grant opposition?
**A:** Within 1 year from the date of publication of grant. Section 25(2).

### Q: What is the procedure for restoration of a lapsed patent?
**A:** Application in Form-15 within 18 months from cessation, with evidence that failure to pay was unintentional and no undue delay. Section 60, Rule 84.

### Q: What is the role of the Opposition Board?
**A:** Constituted for post-grant opposition; examines notice and documents, submits report with reasons within 3 months. Section 25(3), Rule 56.

### Q: What are the penalties for contravention of secrecy provisions?
**A:** Imprisonment up to 2 years, or fine, or both. Section 118.

### Q: What is the time limit for filing an appeal to IPAB?
**A:** Within 3 months from the date of decision, or as allowed. Section 117A.

### Q: What is the fee for expedited examination?
**A:** As prescribed in First Schedule; Form-18A with fee.

### Q: What is the procedure for amending a patent after grant?
**A:** Form-13 with fee; allowed by disclaimer, correction, explanation; publication if substantive; opposition within 3 months. Section 57, Rule 81.

### Q: What is the time limit for furnishing working statement?
**A:** Within 3 months from the end of each calendar year in Form-27. Section 146(2), Rule 131(2).
