\# The Patents Act, 1970 (India)



\## 1. Document Overview



The Patents Act, 1970 (Act 39 of 1970) consolidates and amends the law relating to patents in India. This version incorporates all amendments up to \*\*1 August 2024\*\*, including the \*\*Tribunals Reforms Act, 2021\*\* and the \*\*Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Act, 2023\*\*.



The Act extends to the whole of India and provides for the grant of patents, rights of patentees, opposition, compulsory licences, infringement, penalties, patent agents, and international arrangements.

\## 5. RAG Q\&A Extraction



\### Q: What is an invention under the Patents Act, 1970?

\*\*A:\*\* A new product or process involving an inventive step and capable of industrial application. Section 2(1)(j).



\### Q: What is the term of a patent?

\*\*A:\*\* 20 years from the date of filing of the application. Section 53.



\### Q: What are the grounds for opposition?

\*\*A:\*\* Pre-grant: Section 25(1). Post-grant: Section 25(2). Grounds include wrongful obtaining, prior publication, prior claiming, prior public knowledge/use, obviousness, not an invention, insufficient description, false information, incorrect biological material source, traditional knowledge.



\### Q: What is compulsory licence?

\*\*A:\*\* Licence granted by Controller after 3 years from grant if reasonable requirements not satisfied, not available at affordable price, or not worked in India. Section 84.



\### Q: What is the effect of non-payment of renewal fee?

\*\*A:\*\* Patent ceases to have effect. Section 53(2).



\### Q: What are the penalties for contravention of secrecy?

\*\*A:\*\* Imprisonment up to 2 years, or fine, or both. Section 118.



\### Q: What is the role of High Court in appeals?

\*\*A:\*\* Appeals from Controller decisions lie to High Court under Section 117A for specified sections.
