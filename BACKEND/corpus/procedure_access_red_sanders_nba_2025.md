## 1. Executive Summary

The National Biodiversity Authority states that it regulates access to confiscated and cultivated Red Sanders, in any form, under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 as amended in 2023.

For buyers falling under **Section 3(2)** of the Biological Diversity Act, prior approval from the NBA is required. The document states that this approval is subject to payment of **5% of the auction or sale amount as Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS)** before lifting Red Sanders wood.

After the applicant provides the required details and documents, NBA approval is granted through an **agreement executed between the applicant and an authorised officer of the NBA**, which is construed as the approval.

The order prescribes separate procedures for:

1. Red Sanders sourced from **cultivation/artificial propagation, including patta land**; and
2. Red Sanders sourced from **seized/confiscated material auctioned by Central/State Government agencies**.

The process came into force **with immediate effect** and continues until further order, superseding earlier office/note orders on the subject, including those concerning auctioned/seized Red Sanders.

## 2. Background and Regulatory Context

### 2.1 NBA regulation of Red Sanders access

The document states that the NBA regulates access to confiscated and cultivated Red Sanders under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, as amended in 2023.

For buyers covered by Section 3(2):

- Prior NBA approval is required.
- ABS payment is specified at **5% of the auction or sale amount**.
- The ABS amount must be paid before lifting the Red Sanders wood.
- Approval is issued through an executed agreement between the applicant and an authorised NBA officer.

### 2.2 DGFT export policy references

The order records the following background:

- **DGFT Public Notice No. 74/2015-20, dated 18 February 2019:** introduced an annual quota effective from 2017-2018 and procedure for export of Red Sanders wood of cultivation origin obtained from private land, including patta land.
- **DGFT Notification No. 50/2015-2020, dated 27 December 2022:** further amended the export policy for Red Sanders wood exclusively sourced from cultivated/artificially propagated origin, including patta land and confiscated sources, for specified states.

The states named in the document are:

- Andhra Pradesh
- Assam
- Chhattisgarh
- Gujarat
- Madhya Pradesh
- Manipur
- Tamil Nadu
- Telangana
- Rajasthan
- Maharashtra
- Uttar Pradesh
- Haryana
- Karnataka

### 2.3 Foreign-buyer applications

The NBA receives applications from foreign buyers seeking access to Red Sanders sourced from cultivation/artificial propagation, including patta land, for approval under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 as amended in 2023.

### 2.4 Earlier NBA authority on Red Sanders

The document records that the **32nd Authority meeting**, Agenda No. 32.07, held on **12 December 2014**, authorised the Chairperson, NBA, to accord approvals to Red Sanders access applications without following the normal procedure of seeking consent or otherwise of the BMC through the SBB.

### 2.5 Expert Committee on Red Sanders

The NBA reconstituted the **Expert Committee on Red Sanders on 5 September 2023** to examine its previous report and streamline/simplify processing of applications related to cultivated and confiscated Red Sanders wood.

The Expert Committee subsequently submitted its report and recommendations.

### 2.6 75th Authority meeting and approval of the procedure

The Authority, at its **75th meeting held on 13 October 2025**, under **Agenda Item No. 75.07**, considered the Expert Committee recommendation and approved the applicant checklist and NBA processing procedure for confiscated and cultivated Red Sanders wood.

The competent authority then prescribed the procedures in **Annexure-1** and **Annexure-2**.

# Annexure-1

## 4. Procedure for Applications Relating to Cultivated / Artificially Propagated Red Sanders, Including Patta Land

### Scope

This annexure describes processing of applications for access to Red Sanders wood/logs sourced from cultivation origin or artificial propagation, including patta land, under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002.

The heading of the annexure refers to successful bidders/buyers of Red Sanders wood in auctions conducted by different government agencies, while the detailed text principally addresses cultivated/artificially propagated material, including private land/patta land.

## 5. Annexure-1 Application and Processing Checklist

### Step A - Application and application fee

Upon receipt of **Form-2** with a **Rs. 20,000/- application fee** deposited in the **NBF** (abbreviation reproduced as written in the source), the NBA Accounts Section issues a **TR5 receipt** to the applicant.

Any amount paid by the applicant other than the application fee, including any ABS amount, is not taken into account at this stage.

### Step B - Required signed/sealed documents

NBA examines whether the applicant has submitted the required signed and sealed documents in accordance with the checklist/guidance documents on the NBA website.

Required documents include:

1. **Certificate of Origin** issued by the concerned Forest Department.
2. **Agreement between applicant and cultivator(s)** where the applicant accesses cultivated Red Sanders from private land owners/farmers.
3. **Purchase Order / buyer agreement** entered into between the applicant and trader(s).
4. Where cultivated Red Sanders is accessed from private land owners/farmers through traders, details of the private land owners/cultivators.
5. **Authorisation letter from the importer / Section 3(2) entity** authorising the company representative or, where applicable, a contact person in India to sign the agreement with NBA, together with identity proof.
6. **Duration of commercial utilisation**, starting from receipt of the material in the importing country.

### Step C - Cultivator / private-land-owner information

Where the applicant accesses cultivated Red Sanders through traders, the source document requires the following information for each private land owner/cultivator:

- Name of land owner
- Name of cultivator/farmer
- Address of cultivator/farmer:
  - Village
  - Taluk
  - Mandal
  - District
  - State
- Aadhaar number
- Mobile number
- Email address, if any
- Bank account details:
  - Account-holder name
  - Account number
  - IFSC code
- Quantity of Red Sanders supplied to the trader by each farmer, in kilograms
- Survey number of the patta/private land
- Complete address of the patta/private land:
  - Village
  - Taluk
  - Mandal
  - District
  - State

### Step D - Quantity reconciliation

The quantity of Red Sanders wood stated in the application must match the **Certificate of Origin issued by the Forest Department**.

If the quantities do not match, the applicant must submit an explanatory note describing the difference, variation, or deviation.

### Step E - Partial quantity

Where the applicant proposes to export only part of the total quantity permitted by the Forest Department, the partial quantity may be considered provided the supporting documents are found to be in order.

### Step F - Clarifications

Where applicable, NBA may seek relevant clarification, documents, or information from the applicant.

### Step G - Competent Authority approval

If the documents/details are in order:

1. A cover letter and draft agreement are submitted to the **Competent Authority** for approval.
2. After approval, the draft agreement is communicated to the applicant for execution.

### Step H - ABS payment

The applicant is instructed to pay **5% of the purchase/sale price** to the NBF, excluding bank transaction charges, **in USD only**, and to provide the transaction receipt to the NBA.

The ABS amount is to be paid **only by the applicant to the NBA**.

The NBA Accounts Section must verify receipt of the ABS amount in accordance with the **extant BDR 2024 / ABS Regulations 2025**, as cited in the source, namely **5% of the purchase price excluding transaction charges**.

After verification:

- The TR5 receipt is prepared.
- The receipt is forwarded to the Technical Section.

### Step I - Agreement execution and final approval

After receiving **two copies of the signed agreement** from the applicant:

1. The legal examiner verifies the agreement clauses.
2. The information is checked for completeness/correctness.
3. The exact/correct ABS amount must have been received.
4. The authorised signatory of the NBA may sign the agreement and approve the application.

### Step J - Communication after approval

A copy of the duly signed agreement and the TR5 receipt for ABS payment is communicated to the applicant.

A copy of the agreement is also endorsed to the concerned:

- State Biodiversity Board (SBB); and
- Forest Department,

for information.

### Step K - Disposal timeline

NBA may dispose of the application **within 90 days** from receipt of an application that is complete in all respects.

### Step L - Non-refundable payments

The source states that:

- The application fee is **non-refundable**.
- Any ABS amount paid is **non-refundable**.
- Such payments are not adjusted against any future application or transaction.

# Annexure-2

## 6. Procedure for Applications Relating to Seized / Confiscated Red Sanders Wood

### Scope

This annexure addresses applications for access to seized/confiscated Red Sanders auctioned by different **Central/State Government agencies**, under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002.

## 7. Annexure-2 Application and Processing Checklist

### Step A - Application and fee

Upon receipt of **Form-2** with a **Rs. 20,000/- application fee** deposited in the NBF, the NBA Accounts Section issues a **TR5 receipt**.

### Step B - Required documents

The applicant must submit duly self-attested documents/details according to the website checklist/guidance documents.

The source identifies the following documents:

1. **Certificate of Origin** issued by the concerned Forest Department/agency.
2. **Confirmation of allotment and demand notice for payment of sale value**, or an allotment order issued by the concerned Forest Development Corporation, Government Department, or organisation authorised to sell seized/confiscated Red Sanders wood.
3. **Agreement between the applicant and the department authorised to auction the seized/confiscated Red Sanders.**
4. **Authorisation letter from the importer / Section 3(2) entity** for the person authorised to sign the NBA agreement, together with identity proof. In the absence of such authorisation, the importer may authorise a contact person in India, with corresponding identity proof.
5. **Duration of commercial utilisation**, from the date of receipt of the Red Sanders material in the importer's possession through the end date.
6. **Quantity of Red Sanders to be accessed** according to the Certificate of Origin, Allotment Order, or Letter of Award issued by the concerned department.

### Step C - Verification against government records

NBA checks whether the details/documents submitted in the application match the information provided by the concerned government departments/agencies.

Where applicable, NBA may request additional clarification, documents, or information from the applicant.

### Step D - Competent Authority approval

If the documents/details are in order:

1. A cover letter and draft agreement are submitted to the Competent Authority.
2. After approval, the draft agreement is communicated to the applicant for execution.

### Step E - ABS payment

The applicant is instructed to pay **5% of the auctioned amount to the NBF**, excluding transaction/bank charges, **in USD only**, and furnish the transaction receipt to NBA.

The ABS amount must be paid **only by the applicant to the NBA**.

The Accounts Section verifies the ABS payment under the **extant BDR 2024 / ABS Regulations 2025**, with the source specifying **5% of the auctioned amount excluding transaction charges**.

A TR5 receipt is then prepared and forwarded to the Technical Section.

### Step F - Agreement verification and approval

Upon receipt of two copies of the signed agreement:

1. The legal examiner verifies the agreement clauses.
2. All information must be in order.
3. The precise ABS amount must have been received.
4. The authorised NBA signatory may sign the agreement and approve the application.

### Step G - Communication after approval

The applicant is provided with:

- A copy of the duly signed agreement; and
- The TR5 receipt for payment of the ABS amount.

A copy of the agreement/contract is endorsed to the concerned:

- State Biodiversity Board (SBB); and
- Forest Department/agency,

for information.

### Step H - Disposal timeline

NBA may dispose of the application **within 90 days** after receiving an application complete in all respects.

### Step I - Non-refundable payments

The source states that the:

- application fee; and
- ABS amount paid

are **non-refundable** and cannot be adjusted against any future application or transaction.

### Cultivated / artificially propagated material - key evidence

For cultivated/private-land/patta-land material, the application record is designed to establish:

- botanical/source provenance through a Forest Department Certificate of Origin;
- contractual relationship with cultivators;
- buyer/trader chain documentation;
- identification and contact/banking details of growers;
- quantity supplied by each grower;
- land survey details;
- importer/Section 3(2) authorisation;
- commercial-utilisation period; and
- quantity reconciliation against the Certificate of Origin.

### Seized / confiscated material - key evidence

For seized/confiscated auction material, the documentary trail is designed to establish:

- Forest Department/agency Certificate of Origin;
- government allotment/sale documentation;
- authorised auctioning department/agency agreement;
- importer/Section 3(2) authorisation;
- commercial-utilisation duration; and
- quantity correspondence with the relevant government-issued document.
