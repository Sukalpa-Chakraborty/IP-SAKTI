# Procedure for Closing of Applications Received Under the Biological Diversity Act 2002

- **Document topic:** Procedure for closure/termination of applications received under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002
- **Source pages:** 19-20 of the proceedings (2-page annex)

> **Source-era note:** This Markdown preserves the procedure as stated in the 2016 source document. It is not a current statement of Indian biodiversity law or legal advice.

## 1. Purpose and Background

The National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) is described in the source as the designated statutory body regulating access to biological resources occurring in or obtained from India, and associated knowledge, for purposes including research, commercial utilization, and bio-survey/bio-utilization.

The source states that the NBA decides applications made by:

- Foreigners, including foreign entities;
- Non-resident Indians (NRIs);
- Entities registered in India with non-Indian participation in share capital or management;
- Persons seeking to transfer research results to foreign entities; and
- Persons applying for intellectual property rights in India or outside India for inventions based on research or information relating to biological resources obtained from India.

The NBA may receive applications that become incomplete because applicants do not respond to queries raised during processing. The source states that such non-response can necessitate closure/termination of the application. The procedure was framed to establish due process and reduce arbitrariness in deciding when applications should be closed or terminated.

## 2. Situations Resulting in Closure/Termination

### A. Applicant voluntarily withdraws the application

An application may be closed/terminated when the applicant chooses to withdraw it, provided that:

- The withdrawal is requested unconditionally by the applicant; and
- There is no adverse implication from permitting the withdrawal.

### 2.1 Special provision for Form III applications linked to patent status

The source includes an inserted proviso stating that, where an applicant chooses to withdraw a Form III application because the related patent application has been:

- Abandoned;
- Refused to be granted; or
- Treated as dead in the patent office,

whether in or outside India, the NBA Secretariat may close the Form III application after obtaining the necessary documentation, as appropriate, without referring it to the Expert Committee/Authority.

**Footnote:** This proviso was inserted vide agenda no. 38.17.02 at the 38th Authority meeting held on 04 July 2016.

### B. Failure to fulfil requirements or respond to NBA queries

An application may be closed/terminated where the applicant fails to fulfil conditions stipulated under the Biological Diversity Act and Rules, or fails to respond to queries from the NBA Secretariat, despite the issue of **two reminders/notices**, each providing **30 days** for compliance.

### C. Failure to execute an agreement within 60 days

An application may be closed/terminated where the NBA directs execution of an agreement and the applicant does not execute that agreement within **60 days**.

## 3. Process for Closure/Termination of Applications

### 3.1 Examination by the Expert Committee on ABS

Except for the applications covered by the special proviso under A.1, applications proposed for closure/termination by the NBA Secretariat are to be examined and deliberated upon by the **Expert Committee on Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS)**.

The Expert Committee may recommend one of the following:

1. **Closure/termination** of the application.
2. **Further explanation:** Seek additional reasons for withdrawal from the applicant and then re-examine the matter.
3. **Enquiry:** Recommend an enquiry by designated persons, through the State Biodiversity Board (SBB), or through another relevant agency where there is non-response or suspicion of misappropriation/violation of the Act.
4. **Other action:** Recommend any other appropriate action.

### 3.2 NBA decision

Based on the Expert Committee's recommendation, the **NBA takes the decision** on whether the application should be closed/terminated or otherwise.

### 3.3 Communication of closure/termination

Once the NBA decides to close/terminate an application:

- A closure/termination letter is sent to the applicant.
- A copy is marked to the concerned **State Biodiversity Board (SBB)**, **Biodiversity Management Committee (BMC)**, or another relevant agency, as applicable.
- Where required, the decision is also communicated to the applicant's attorney/legal counsel.

### 3.4 Reopening a closed/terminated application

Once an application has been closed/terminated, the applicant **cannot reopen the same application**.

The applicant may instead submit a **fresh application**, subject to:

- Payment of the prescribed fee; and
- Fulfilment of the requirements under the Biological Diversity Act and Rules and the requirements specified by the NBA Secretariat.

### 3.5 Reasons for a subsequent application

The NBA reserves the right to direct an applicant submitting a new application on the same subject matter to furnish reasons for the actions or inactions that resulted in closure/termination of the earlier application.

### 3.6 Public display of closed/terminated applications

The source states that details of closed/terminated applications shall be displayed on the **NBA website**.

### 3.7 Amendment of guidelines

The NBA may amend these guidelines whenever it considers such amendment appropriate.

## 4. RAG Retrieval Facts

### Application closure triggers

- Voluntary, unconditional withdrawal with no adverse implication.
- Form III withdrawal associated with an abandoned, refused, or dead patent application, subject to the source's special proviso and documentation.
- Failure to comply with requirements of the Biological Diversity Act and Rules after **two reminders/notices of 30 days each**.
- Failure to execute an NBA-directed agreement within **60 days**.

### Closure decision workflow

**NBA Secretariat identifies closure issue -> Expert Committee on ABS examines (except specified Form III proviso cases) -> Expert Committee recommends closure, further explanation, enquiry, or other action -> NBA decides -> closure/termination letter issued -> relevant SBB/BMC/agency and, where required, legal counsel informed.**

### Consequence of closure

The same application cannot be reopened. A fresh application may be submitted after paying the prescribed fee and fulfilling applicable requirements.

### Investigation trigger

Non-response or suspected misappropriation/violation of the Act may lead the Expert Committee to recommend an enquiry through designated persons, an SBB, or another relevant agency.
