# Prophylaxis and Management of COVID-19 through AYUSH Interventions: Best Practices and Evidence-Based Approach

## 1. Document Overview

This document, published by the **Ministry of Ayush, Government of India**, presents a comprehensive overview of AYUSH interventions for the prophylaxis and management of COVID-19. It summarizes the research initiatives, clinical studies, and evidence-based approaches undertaken during the COVID-19 pandemic. The document includes recommended AYUSH formulations, doses, durations, estimated costs, and a bibliography of supporting studies.

The Ministry of Ayush constituted an **Inter-disciplinary Ayush R&D Task Force** and collaborated with premier institutions such as AIIMS, CSIR, DBT, ICMR, and IITs. A total of **139 clinical, pre-clinical, and epidemiological studies** were undertaken. The document highlights the outcomes of these studies and proposes AYUSH interventions for widespread use.

## 4. Evidence-Based AYUSH Interventions for COVID-19 Management

### 4.1 AYUSH-64
- **Studies:** 7 clinical studies, 500 participants.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Early clinical recovery.
  - Reduced duration of hospital stay.
  - No progression to severe/critical stage.
  - Improvement in Quality of Life (QoL).
  - Well tolerated and safe.
- **Community-based study:** 64,642 asymptomatic, mild, and moderate COVID-19 patients in home isolation.
  - Significant benefit: better clinical recovery, improved well-being, reduced need for hospitalization.
- **In-silico study:** 35 out of 36 phyto-constituents have high binding affinity against SARS-CoV-2.
- **Safety:** In use since 1980 for malaria and flu-like illness without adverse effects.

### 4.2 Guduchi Ghan Vati
- **Studies:** 2 clinical studies, 70 participants (asymptomatic, mild to moderate).
- **Outcomes:**
  - Early clinical recovery.
  - No adverse events.
  - 93.3% RT-PCR negative by day 10 in Ayurveda group vs 66.6% in standard care group.
  - All symptomatic participants clinically recovered by day 5 in Ayurveda group.

### 4.3 Guduchi-Pippali
- **Studies:** 2 clinical studies, 200 participants.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Early clinical recovery in add-on group (2 days earlier than standard group).
  - No worsening of disease.
  - No drug-herb interaction observed.

### 4.4 Kabasura Kudineer
- **Studies:** 7 clinical studies, 856 participants.
- **Nation-wide distribution:** 31,497 people benefited.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Early clinical recovery.
  - Significant reduction in viral load.
  - Lesser mortality rate in Kabasura Kudineer group.
- **Community-based study:** Asymptomatic, mild, moderate COVID-19 patients in home isolation showed early clinical recovery and significant improvement in well-being parameters (appetite, sleep, physical and mental health).

### 4.5 Other Interventions
- **Yashtimadhu, Ashwagandha-Shunthi, Nilavembu Kudineer, Eupatorium perfoliatum 30C, Camphora, Bryonia alba, Arsenicum iodatum 30C** evaluated as standalone or add-on.

## 5. AYUSH Interventions for COVID-19 Prophylaxis

### 5.1 Ashwagandha
- **Studies:** 4 studies, 7,000 people.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Prophylactic efficacy in reducing incidence.
  - Non-inferior to Hydroxychloroquine (HCQ) in high-risk healthcare workers.
  - Superior safety profile: significantly fewer adverse events than HCQ.
- **International study:** To be studied in UK with London School of Hygiene & Tropical Medicine (LSHTM).

### 5.2 Ayuraksha Kit
- **Composition:** Chyawanprash, Samshamani Vati, Ayush Kwath, Anu Taila (nasal instillation).
- **Studies:**
  - CCRAS study: 1.78 lakh participants.
  - AIIA study: 80,000 Delhi police personnel.
- **Outcomes:**
  - Less incidence of COVID-19.
  - Low mortality rate.
  - Safety and prophylactic efficacy established.

### 5.3 Guduchi Ghan Vati (Prophylaxis)
- **Participants:** ~1.12 lakh.
- **Outcomes:** Positive effects on immune status and general well-being.

### 5.4 Chyawanprash
- **Studies:** 7 prophylactic studies, 2,950 participants.
- **Outcomes:** Effective and safe in high-risk healthcare workers and healthy individuals; reduced incidence of COVID-19.

### 5.5 Anu Taila
- **Pre-clinical study:** Intranasal instillation limits viral load and severity in COVID-19 (hamster model).

### 5.6 AYUSH Kwath
- **Properties:** Immune-modulator, antiviral, anti-oxidant, anti-inflammatory, hepato-protective.
- **In-silico study (IIT Varanasi):** Beta-Sitosterol is a potential inhibitor of SARS-CoV-2.
- **Pre-clinical study (NIN Hyderabad):** Safety established.

### 5.7 Kabasura Kudineer (Prophylaxis)
- **Studies:** 2 studies, 30,000 participants.
- **Outcomes:** Effective and safe in high-risk healthcare workers and healthy individuals; reduced incidence and improved well-being.

### 5.8 Yoga and Pranayama
- **Study:** Randomized controlled pilot study, 280 participants.
- **Outcome:** Pranayama for 28 days significantly decreased COVID-19 positivity rate among exposed healthcare professionals.

### 5.9 Homeopathy and Unani
- Studies as add-on interventions revealed lesser incidence and trend of early recovery compared to control group.

## 6. Rationale for AYUSH Interventions

- Good immune status is vital for prevention and prevention of disease progression.
- AYUSH interventions modulate the immune system.
- Safety established through long-term clinical use.
- Wide-spread acceptance among masses.
- Formulations included in the "National Clinical Management Protocol based on Ayurveda & Yoga for management of COVID-19" and advisories by Ministry of Ayush.

## 10. RAG Q&A Extraction

### Q: What is the purpose of this document?
**A:** To present evidence-based AYUSH interventions for the prophylaxis and management of COVID-19, including recommended formulations, doses, costs, and supporting clinical studies.

### Q: How many studies were undertaken by the Ministry of Ayush?
**A:** 139 clinical, pre-clinical, and epidemiological studies.

### Q: What are the recommended AYUSH interventions for COVID-19 prophylaxis?
**A:** Ayuraksha Kit, Samshamani Vati / Guduchi Ghan Vati, and Ashwagandha tablet. Each with specific doses and durations.

### Q: What are the recommended AYUSH interventions for COVID-19 management?
**A:** AYUSH-64 (for asymptomatic and mild to moderate) and Kabasura Kudineer.

### Q: What is AYUSH-64?
**A:** An Ayurvedic formulation developed by CCRAS, used for malaria and flu-like illness, repurposed for COVID-19. It has shown early clinical recovery, reduced hospital stay, and safety.

### Q: What is the dose of AYUSH-64 for mild to moderate COVID-19?
**A:** 2 tablets of 500 mg thrice a day for 20 days. Estimated cost per individual: Rs. 352.20.

### Q: What is Kabasura Kudineer?
**A:** A Siddha polyherbal decoction. Dose: 5 gm twice a day for 20 days. Estimated cost: Rs. 212.

### Q: What is the Ayuraksha Kit?
**A:** A combination of Chyawanprash, Samshamani Vati, Ayush Kwath, and Anu Taila (nasal instillation). Dose: Chyawanprash 6 gm once a day; Ayush Kwath 75 ml once a day; Samshamani Vati 500 mg twice a day; Anu Taila 1-2 drops each nostril once a day. Duration: 30 days. Cost: Rs. 262.50.

### Q: What is the prophylactic dose of Ashwagandha?
**A:** 500 mg twice a day for 30 days. Estimated cost: Rs. 92.

### Q: What is the prophylactic dose of Guduchi Ghan Vati / Samshamani Vati?
**A:** 1 tablet (500 mg) twice a day for 30 days. Estimated cost: Rs. 40.

### Q: What evidence supports the use of AYUSH interventions?
**A:** Clinical trials, community-based studies, in-silico studies, pre-clinical studies, and systematic reviews. For example, AYUSH-64 showed 93.3% RT-PCR negativity by day 10 for Guduchi Ghan Vati; Ashwagandha was non-inferior to HCQ with superior safety.

### Q: What is the role of Yoga and Pranayama?
**A:** Pranayama for 28 days significantly decreased COVID-19 positivity rate among exposed healthcare professionals.

### Q: Where can I find the National Clinical Management Protocol based on Ayurveda & Yoga?
**A:** Available at: https://www.ayush.gov.in/docs/ayush-Protocol-covid-19.pdf
