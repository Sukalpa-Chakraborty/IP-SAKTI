# Guidelines for Upfront Payment for Access to Biological Resources

## 1. Document Overview

This document provides guidelines for calculating **upfront payment** for access to biological resources under the Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS) framework. It specifies the formula for payment calculation, special provisions for abundant bioresources, and the basis for assessment when soil or water is accessed.

The guidelines are issued by the National Biodiversity Authority (NBA).

## 2. Key Provisions

### 2.1 Payment for Abundant Bioresources
- For bioresources available in abundance, the upfront payment will be calculated at **50% of the amount prescribed** at column no. 2 & 3 for every **50 kg / site / collection**.
- **This formula will not be applicable for microorganisms.**

### 2.2 Quantity Determination
- The quantity of biological resources will be decided by the **NBA on a case-by-case basis**.

### 2.3 Calculation of Payment
The payment is calculated as follows:

a) **Aggregate of each species** multiplied by **quantity**, multiplied by **number of collections**, and multiplied by **number of sites**.

b) **Wherever soil/water is being accessed**, the basis of assessment shall be in terms of **ml/mg**, as the case may be, multiplied by **number of collections** and multiplied by **number of sites**.

### 2.4 Notes
1. The accrued amount will be shared as per the provisions given in the **ABS Regulations, 2014**.
2. **Above rates may be revised from time to time.**

## 3. RAG Q&A Extraction

### Q: What is the upfront payment for abundant bioresources?
**A:** 50% of the amount prescribed at column no. 2 & 3 for every 50 kg / site / collection, but not applicable for microorganisms.

### Q: Who decides the quantity of biological resources?
**A:** The National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) decides on a case-by-case basis.

### Q: How is the payment calculated?
**A:** Aggregate of each species × quantity × number of collections × number of sites. For soil/water, it is ml/mg × number of collections × number of sites.

### Q: How is the accrued amount shared?
**A:** As per the provisions given in the ABS Regulations, 2014.

### Q: Can the rates be revised?
**A:** Yes, the rates may be revised from time to time.
